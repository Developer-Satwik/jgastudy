# Proposed refinements before sharing

28 October 2026. For Satwik's review. The version 0.1 Word/PDF files and core question map are preserved. This is a proposed change list, not a record of client approval or changes already applied.

## Essential changes

1. **No recent case route after Q006.** Before the skip to Q014, ask where relevant: “Has an improvement you mentioned been put forward internally?” Then choose either “What happened next?” or “What has prevented it being raised?” Only never-raised needs enter the pre-proposal category. Older proposals remain separately labelled context. If a qualifying recent case emerges, confirm it and resume Q007–Q013; do not automatically skip or code it as pre-proposal. Preserve unclear timing/involvement rather than force classification.
2. **Success in Q008.** Optional probe: “What improvement was the proposal meant to deliver, and how would you know it had worked?”
3. **Evidence in Q010.** Select relevant probes about baseline or measure, which costs were considered and who expected to benefit. Do not require exact budgets or force all benefits into money.
4. **Realisation in Q013.** If delivery permits: “What has actually changed, and how is the result being assessed?” Where time/capacity was released, ask where it went. Preserve not measured/not yet assessed separately from success and failure.

## Clarify in the internal guide and coding

- Q002: service boundary for outsourced/mixed arrangements; employer-retained work and data handoffs.
- Q003/Q004: whose consequence matters and what supports the explanation. Distinguish not known to be available, unsuitable, unaffordable/value unproven, readiness/ownership, not evaluated, or no material shortfall; allow multiple codes.
- Q005: optional condition that would advance or reduce the priority. If AI arises, ask actual task, stage and oversight rather than assume a common definition.
- Q006: include people/capability change. A substantive decision milestone in an older programme may qualify within 24 months, with original start and milestone dates recorded separately.
- Q008: after the open answer, clarify mandatory deadline, lifecycle or discretionary trigger where useful.
- Q012/Q013: preferred retention versus transition constraints or contract timing, including combinations; practical supplier actions distinguished from known observed effects.
- All cases: record first-hand involvement versus reports/interpretation, expectation versus result and source of any metric. Do not expand the cohort or request employee records.

## Client pack changes

- Keep the eight chapters and14 core questions.
- Chapter1: include a concise historical context element, avoiding a false numerical time series.
- Chapter2: diagnose different types of unmet need and readiness gap, including needs not yet proposed.
- Chapter3: distinguish priorities, commitments and dated future conditions.
- Chapter5: explain intended value, affordability, baseline and who captures the benefit.
- Chapter6: distinguish go-live from observed improvement.
- Prospectus: one short sentence on needs that do not become proposals, and one on benefits beyond approval. Do not turn it into a method manual or add speculative findings.
- No fixed participant-duration promise before a timing check. Probes remain a menu; do not ask all of them in every conversation.
