## Changes implemented

1. Q006 now investigates whether a need was raised when no case is recalled. Never-raised needs, older context, uncertain eligibility and newly recalled recent cases have different routes. A qualifying recalled case resumes Q007–Q013; refusal to share is respected.
2. Substantive decision milestones in older programmes can qualify within the recall window. Original start and selected decision dates remain separate; continued operation alone is insufficient.
3. Q008 establishes intended outcome; Q010 probes baseline, cost scope and who benefits; Q013 separates delivery from actual benefit assessment. Released capacity is not automatically cash saved.
4. Context and gap probes distinguish retained employer work, handoffs, capability/fit, affordability/value, readiness and ownership. Unknown and no material shortfall remain valid.
5. Future-priority probes ask what could change the decision and clarify actual technology tasks and stage of use. The outline includes brief history and conditional planning signals.
6. The prospectus now includes needs never proposed and distinguishes approval/go-live from observed benefits. It continues to describe planned research, not findings already obtained.
