## Calculate the right unit

The concept specifies 12–15 practitioners. Add every additional distinct host, moderator or other selected attendee. Do not count one person twice because they perform two roles. A generic menu limit does not enlarge a specific room, and a larger room does not remove a menu's guest ceiling.

A future all-in cost needs the actual menu/seat count, eligible minimum spend, any separate hire, drinks, applicable charges and tax basis. Where actual terms confirm consumption offsets a minimum, model the relevant shortfall rather than adding the same minimum to the full eligible spend. A credited deposit belongs in the cash schedule and remaining balance, not as another expense.

Do not add VAT again to a VAT-inclusive price. Preserve Benares' discretionary service description; its calculation base, interaction with cover and any recoverability have not been established. No universal charge percentage is inferred for the other venues.

## Current source does not mean applicable future price

The initial search surfaced a Benares January guide with menus valid 2 January–30 April 2026. The live dining page actually links a September-onwards guide with different menu prices and cover/service charges. The January original and review images remain in the [excluded historical record](research-notes/t04/uk-costs/excluded-historical/january-manifest.json); they are not current inputs.

The September guide still ends 15 November 2026. All menus/prices are subject to change. Its published lead times include menu selection at least 14 days ahead and final numbers 72 hours ahead. A seasonal deposit statement for 15 November–30 December supplies neither an amount nor a general no-deposit rule outside those dates. It is insufficient for a future cancellation or cash-exposure schedule.

## Inputs still needed for the selected case

Actual event date and city; total distinct people and layout; availability; menu suitability and non-alcohol options; private discussion, acoustics and access; employer hospitality acceptability; minimum-spend eligibility and hire mechanics; charge bases; deposit, balance, cancellation and refund terms. Research production, JGA time, recruitment, moderation, travel and commercial servicing are additional cost areas beyond this venue screen.

These are model requirements, not a new client questionnaire or permission to contact venues. No quotation, booking, participant record, financial-register row or recommended venue is created here. Published rates can inform a clearly labelled scenario later if their date and assumptions are explicit; they do not become confirmed future costs.
