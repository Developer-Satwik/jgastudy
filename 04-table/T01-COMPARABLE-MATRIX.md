## Implications to carry into the financial model

- Research-only and event-supported cases need the same core research cost basis. Event costs and benefits are incremental.
- Keep subscription/servicing labour, research production, recruitment, sales, rights administration and hospitality visible. Public provider offers do not supply JGA costs.
- Partner funding can bundle research-use rights; count the bundle once. Do not count the same licence again as a separate sale.
- Free practitioner attendance creates a funding requirement. It does not establish which sponsor would pay, how much or when cash arrives.
- The current USD1,500 commission is a first-project investment, not proof of recurring unit cost. Public comparator annual offers do not create recurring contracted JGA revenue.
- Prices, delivered outcomes, event appetite, servicing capacity and funding timing need later evidence or clearly labelled assumptions. No financial inputs have been added in T01.
