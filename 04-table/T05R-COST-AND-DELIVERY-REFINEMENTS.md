## Delivery routes to compare later

- **Agreed supplier package:** one price with explicit complete outputs and inclusions. Retain JGA review and any genuinely separate work. Do not add the same supplier labour again.
- **Scoped external work:** role rates multiplied by actually needed and bookable work. Check capability, handoff, minimum engagements and overlap.
- **Internal or hybrid delivery:** identify who performs each task, time available and the resource/cash basis. Existing salary does not make the work free, while a resource allocation is not automatically extra cash payroll.
- **Assisted work within a route:** a specific tool-supported task with appropriate review. This is a process choice within a delivery route, not evidence that a complete report can be produced at zero labour cost.

## A short costing note to use with a real quote or assumption

Record the chosen task/output and edition; available existing asset; responsible role and relevant skill; hours or booked unit; quantity and rate/fee currency; inclusions; extra handoff/review; whether cash is additional or already committed; payment and cancellation terms; source date and assumption status. Unknown applicable costs remain visible.

Use the existing task IDs where the work already exists. Do not add new rows merely to rename the same work. Additional customer/service, sales and event work will be costed in their own units, including any genuinely shared-work credit.
