Turn insight into action: Join Hays' expert events and webinars

23 September 2026

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Hays
ICAS Partner

Looking for practical workforce insights, market intelligence and fresh ideas to help your organisation stay ahead? Explore upcoming Hays events and webinars and register for the sessions most relevant to you.

The world of work continues to evolve at pace. From changing skills demands and talent shortages to AI adoption and employment legislation, organisations are navigating increasingly complex workforce challenges. Staying informed is no longer a nice-to-have. It's essential for making confident hiring, retention and workforce planning decisions.

That's why Hays brings together industry experts, business leaders and recruitment specialists through a programme of webinars, leadership panels, networking events and roundtable discussions. Designed to deliver practical, actionable insight, these sessions explore the issues shaping today's workforce and the trends likely to influence tomorrow's.

Whether you're a finance leader looking to understand emerging governance challenges, an HR professional preparing for legislative change, or a business leader exploring how AI is transforming workplace capabilities, Hays events provide direct access to expert knowledge and real-world perspectives. Topics span recruitment, workforce strategy, salary and skills trends, leadership, diversity, technology and career development.

More than just presentations, these events create opportunities to connect with peers, share experiences and learn from organisations facing similar challenges. Attendees leave with practical takeaways, fresh thinking and a clearer understanding of how to respond to the changes affecting their sector and workforce.

Available both online and in person across the UK, the programme is designed to make expert insight accessible, wherever you're based. With new sessions added regularly, there's always an opportunity to explore emerging trends, hear from industry specialists and gain the market intelligence needed to make better workforce decisions.

In a rapidly changing market, knowledge is a competitive advantage. Hays Events & Webinars help turn insight into action, giving organisations the confidence to plan ahead, adapt to change and build the talent strategies they need for future success.
