## The missing test is at asset level

Before reducing a future effort estimate for reuse, identify the particular asset and work already complete: the relevant sections, evidence period, task and country fit, data/quotation rights, currentness checking and the work still needed. A genuine reusable design or method can save work without making previous findings current. A free article, public podcast or existing search report cannot silently replace the commissioned original practitioner contribution.

This sharpens WR01–03, WR06–10, WR12 and WR16. It does not justify cutting any hours by a fixed percentage, shrinking the complete report, lowering the six-to-ten contributor requirement or adding a new country quota. No competitor route supplies measured labour savings, actual staff availability, booked rates or a validated future JGA price. T5C-C01–C03.

## Earlier questions expose scope assumptions

The [prior-study bridge](prior-study-bridge.json) retains three exact questions and all 24 saved answers across four tools/two repeats. Three ChatGPT first-run texts were semantically reread; the other 21 received exact text/hash and metadata checks. No question was rerun and no recruiter recommendation count was transferred to this analysis.

- **S04-C, transformation:** the authored scenario requires a UK/European systems-programme leader across payroll, integration and change. The reread answer adds evidence categories and a weighted scorecard. Those weights are generated advice, not observed buyer criteria or measured research cost. It is useful as a capability-fit counterexample, not a staffing rate.
- **S07-C, ordinary replacement:** the authored scenario is a permanent Payroll Manager replacement at a medium-sized UK employer. The answer itself distinguishes public specialisation from proof of current delivery. It provides no basis to impose executive-transformation work on every ordinary hiring case.
- **S11-C, small-team growth:** the authored scenario adds one hands-on Senior Payroll Specialist to a small team. The answer introduces a London focus not present in the question. This is a concrete warning against silently adding geography or seniority when selecting evidence and estimating work.

The different task premises can help design selective probes and assess how much evidence checking is needed. They do not establish spontaneous demand, an actual hiring need, candidate availability, causal mechanisms or feasible production hours. An AI-generated shortlist is also not evidence that an automated report could deliver the contracted qualitative work at a particular saving. T5C-C04.

## Implications to carry forward

1. Retain an asset-reuse note before any future lower-effort assumption: usable evidence/method, date, rights, remaining verification and editing. Quantify a saving only when its actual work basis is supported.
2. Select capability and task scope before rate mapping. A general market-research rate can value an explicit comparison, but a role title, geographic office or generated capability checklist does not verify a supplier's payroll expertise.
3. Keep client-specific interpretation, generic compensation data, hosted expert content and newly collected decision evidence separate. Their costs and fulfilment should be defined for the actual task rather than inferred from a format label.
