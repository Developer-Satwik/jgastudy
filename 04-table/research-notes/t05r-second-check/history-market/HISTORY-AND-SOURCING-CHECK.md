## Practical result

A later model can compare an actual supplier package, scoped bought-in tasks and an internal/hybrid route. Their complete scopes and JGA retained effort must reconcile, with shared work counted once.

Keep the7.5-hour normalisation separate from bookable days/minimum engagements. Keep applicable platform fees, tax, FX and payment timing separate from role-rate arithmetic. Do not silently inflate2027 rates, infer an Indian sourcing discount or apply an automation saving. Those require their own actual evidence or clearly labelled scenarios.

No numerical input change is necessary from this strand. The [four selective conditions](application-conditions.json) should guide later sourcing and cash inputs. Participant access, permission, review and complete editable delivery still require their existing work and cannot be removed merely because a public headline rate is lower.
