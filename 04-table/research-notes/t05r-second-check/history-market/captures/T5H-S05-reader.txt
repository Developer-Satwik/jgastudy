L20: When you hire on Upwork as a client on our free, Basic plan, you pay a Client Marketplace Fee of up to 7.99% on all payments you make to freelancers, including:
L21: 
L22: 
L23:   * Fixed-price and hourly contracts
L24:   * Project Catalog projects
L25:   * Bonuses
L26:   * Direct Contracts
L27:   * Consultations
L28:   * Payroll
L29:   * Expenses
L30:   * Miscellaneous charges
L31: 
L32: 
L33: Good to know: The Client Marketplace Fee is higher if you are on a Business Plus plan. 【11†Learn more】.
L34: ### Is there a way to get a discount on the Client Marketplace Fee?
L35: 
L36: Yes, if you live in the U.S. and 【12†qualify】, you can use your bank account as your billing method. If you do, you'll pay a reduced Client Marketplace Fee of 3%.
L37: 
L38: 
L39: Note: This fee is not refundable, even if a payment is reversed or refunded.
