# Copywriter Survey 2025

474 respondents · Fieldwork February · Published July 2025

474 UK copywriters told us how AI is changing their work. More use it than ever, and more would happily see it gone.

## Headline findings

AI adoption

### Adoption keeps climbing

69% of copywriters now use generative AI tools, up from 59% in 2024. Nearly one in five say clients, colleagues or employers insist on it.

AI sentiment

### A slim majority would switch it off

Given a magic button that erases generative AI from the world, 54% would press it. Nearly half value it as a tool.

### A large leap

The average day rate rose 9% to £480. On the median measure we now use, it was £430.

Gender pay gap

### The gap grows again

The gender pay gap grew in nearly every sector we measured, to 21% overall. Only those who mix freelancing and employment closed it.
