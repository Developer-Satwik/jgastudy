# T02: funding-partner and practitioner propositions

30 October 2026. Internal concept drafts for root integration and review. These describe possible future Table products; they are not offers to send, invitations, granted rights or new delivery obligations under the signed research agreement.

## Ownership boundary

Future partner/member use choices describe JGA’s proposed downstream product terms, not restrictions on JGA imposed by Riseklix. Under the working agreement section7, commissioned assignable original IP transfers to JGA on full payment, subject to contributor and third-party rights. JGA remains responsible for final publication and later licensing/commercial decisions; this draft grants no new customer rights and creates no Riseklix approval veto.

## The shared idea

Use a defined research edition to examine how payroll needs turn into investment decisions, then add a useful peer exchange where it improves the result. The commercial and practitioner exchanges need to work separately. A partner funds defined work and receives stated benefits. Practitioners contribute on a practical permitted basis and retain the original free-attendance/free-research benefit. An event or separately agreed commercial access may be part of a transparent combination, but neither is implied by research funding.

## Funding partner: a defined independent research edition

The proposed unit is one edition, a bounded findings briefing, disclosed funding acknowledgement and specifically agreed uses. A named product, commercial or strategy decision or a clear communication use must explain why that combination is valuable. Supplying an equivalent report copy is not an automatic second sale where a practitioner already receives the research free.

| Minimum output to test | Useful purpose | What still needs definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finished research edition | Provide bounded decision evidence and implications for the agreed audience | Topic, audience, actual evidence, final method/release and edition |
| One focused findings briefing | Help a defined team interpret the result for its intended use | Agenda, audience, format, duration, preparation and servicing capacity |
| Accurate funding acknowledgement and agreed use rights | Make the support relationship clear and allow specified uses | Funding statement, logo permission, internal/external use, channels, version and period |

The proposed partner does not receive method or findings control. JGA retains final publication responsibility under the working agreement. Topic/focus can be agreed before research; funding does not buy favourable results or suppression. A factual check of the partner's own description, if useful, is different from approval of the findings. No default private answers, participant contacts, guaranteed meetings, exclusivity, lead list, ongoing analysis or future-edition reuse is included.

An access/event component is possible only as a separately defined part of the combination. Establish what is bought, who attends, what is disclosed, what is voluntary and which rights apply. Preserve research/member fit beside any partner-nominated invitation route. Do not turn sponsor payment into proof that an audience, research asset or meeting benefit is ready.

### Short concept wording, only after unaided needs and alternatives

> One option we are assessing is a founding research partnership for a defined edition on how payroll leaders turn operational needs into investment decisions. The proposed package would fund the research and give your team a focused findings briefing, agreed recognition as a funding partner, and clearly defined rights to use the finished work. Funding would not give a partner control over the findings. Private answers and participant contacts would not be included by default. Any event involvement or commercial introductions would need a separate, transparent scope. We are testing whether that would serve a real decision or communication need for your team.

The existing B01–B06 guide provides the route. Stronger evidence would identify a real use, previous alternatives, appropriate evidence, purchase decision route and a permitted next step. General approval of the idea is not a sale. A need for raw identities, favourable findings or an unsuitable target-only cohort would require rejecting or materially revising the package.

## Practitioner: useful research and peer exchange

The proposed unit is one defined research/peer cycle, rather than an assumed recurring subscription or an obligation to attend four cities. Keep the original concept: free attendance, free research, one industry question and optional council recognition. Free research is not silently reduced to a teaser, and this draft does not invent individual-only or employer restrictions to create a paid tier.

| Minimum benefit to test | Useful purpose | What still needs definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finished research free to contributors | Let practitioners use the work their input helped inform | Actual edition/release route and agreed uses |
| Relevant facilitated peer exchange if the chosen format proceeds | Explore practical decisions with appropriate peers | Admission/role fit, moderator, agenda, mode and participant choices |
| One short cycle follow-through note | Leave useful takeaways, evidence limits and open questions | A proposed incremental benefit to test and cost, not a promised monthly service |

A contributed industry question need not become an additional mandatory report question; not every question can be guaranteed an answer. The follow-through note should help someone retain the substance without becoming technical payroll advice, an expert helpdesk or ongoing bespoke support. The continuing-member question is what useful output earns a reason to return, not whether another dinner appears on a calendar.

### Short concept wording, only after unaided needs and alternatives

> We are exploring a small senior payroll peer group built around practical research and useful discussion. The working concept includes free attendance and free research, one industry question from each participant, and an exchange with relevant payroll leaders. One possible extra is a short follow-up separating useful takeaways from questions that still need evidence. Council recognition would be optional and separate from permission to publish a contribution. We are assessing the format and its value before arranging any event or asking anyone to commit.

Use existing M01–M06 selectively. Ask what would earn their time, relevant peer mix, practical input mode and alternatives if no event proceeds. An interested person is not yet a member, an attended seat or a usable research contribution. Actual report overlap still requires separate role verification and usable permitted report input under WP03.

## Participation and output modes

Council recognition is optional and separate from research participation, actual attendance, a quotation, naming a person or identifying their employer. Avoid a public recognition list that implicitly links a private account to a participant. Choose a meeting mode before the session. If the named Chatham House Rule is adopted, its current restrictions apply to that meeting; a generic recording choice does not override it. Separate approved research interviews can use different terms.

If hospitality or travel cannot be accepted, assess whether research-only, written or remote input is useful and permitted. None automatically counts as attendance or usable report evidence. The proposed four-city capacity is a changing-cohort concept, not a longitudinal panel or commitment to the same people.

## Work to recognise before pricing or launch

Partner: define use/topic, secure suitable evidence/rights, produce and review the edition, prepare a bounded briefing, manage acknowledgement and permitted-use records, and service the actual promise. Member: screen relevance, prepare moderation, manage practical participation, release the research, produce the scoped follow-through and retain separate permission/count records. Any event adds recruitment, venue, access, hospitality, travel, cancellation and cash timing work. These are requirements to cost later, not supplier quotes or financial inputs.

Do not assume an existing staffed research/advice service, guaranteed connections, regular new reports, annual renewal, four-city viability or measurable commercial return. No participant, partner, date, price, licence, employer permission or operating arrangement has been confirmed.
