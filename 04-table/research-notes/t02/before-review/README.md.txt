## Working rules

- One canonical source register supports both workstreams. Sources are not independently replicated into separate datasets.
- Every topic has a decision, evidence requirements, counterevidence search and a documented conclusion or unresolved gap.
- Records distinguish published evidence, practitioner observations, interpretation, calculated results and financial assumptions.
- Research notes are internal until reviewed. A search result or a source in the queue is not a finished finding.
- No external email, interview invitation, prospect contact, form submission, purchase or booking is part of this planning pass.
- No identifiable participant records belong in this repository. An ignored folder is not secure storage. Before collection we will agree the tools, access, notices, permissions and applicable transfer arrangements.
- No website or public dashboard is being added to scope. The contracted handover uses portable PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files.
