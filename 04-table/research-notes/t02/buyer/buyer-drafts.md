# T02 research-buyer drafts

30 October 2026. Internal concepts for review, not commercial terms or new services added to the signed project. These make H01 concrete after the six-model review; H02–H04 remain separate. No buyer, price, permission or demand has been confirmed.

## Recommended concept to examine first

**Payroll Decision Evidence Pack:** the finished, dated research edition plus a clear path through the evidence for a real payroll investment/change decision. A bounded reader briefing is a possible extra component to scope and cost, not a service promised by the present project.

The signed project delivers the complete contracted report, feasibility work and materials to JGA. These drafts examine JGA's possible future customer offers. They do not replace the finished report with a smaller pack, grant customer rights or add bespoke/service work to Riseklix's current scope.

The working agreement's section 5 names five likely-buyer categories to assess: payroll technology, outsourcing, HCM, consulting and investment organisations. They are hypotheses, not qualified prospects. The existing B01/B02 sequence should establish each person's actual decision and alternatives before presenting the concept. Role-specific wording can clarify the same question without expanding the guide.

| Contract-named buyer category | Concrete use to examine | Evidence and rights boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payroll technology | Product, strategy or commercial work on a defined payroll buyer problem/investment barrier | Qualitative decision evidence is not market prevalence or product-market fit; internal use and public marketing differ |
| Outsourcing | Service/proposition decisions about employer transition needs, retained work and readiness | No favourable provider ranking, lower-cost claim or proof of the provider's own delivery |
| HCM | Payroll/HCM change context, organisational handoffs or commercial positioning in a defined country/employer setting | Not technical integration testing or broad HR demand research; establish internal versus partner/public use |
| Consulting | Discovery/framing for a defined client decision or internal advisory evidence base | Client-specific recommendations remain the consultant's work; external client deliverables require their own use definition |
| Investment | Identify sector/diligence questions or decision frictions worth further investigation | Exploratory context only, not valuation, TAM, forecasts, investment advice or a diligence conclusion; establish internal versus portfolio/public use |

An employer-side payroll/finance/change lead may also benefit from the edition, primarily as a practitioner/research beneficiary in the present concept. A paid employer variant is only an additional hypothesis if it provides a genuinely different wanted use or service beside the promised free practitioner research. No automatic second sale or new internal-use restriction follows.

### The minimum useful payload

1. The specified dated edition, with summary and actual source/method context. This uses the research asset already being commissioned, not a new bespoke study.
2. A short reader path to relevant sections, actually supported case contrasts and practical templates for the identified task. It should make absent or partial evidence visible, not turn a small qualitative cohort into a market benchmark.
3. A traceable evidence boundary: public sources and appropriately cleared aggregate practitioner material. No raw identities, private documents or unrestricted quotations.
4. A clear record of edition, intended use and any unresolved circulation or external-use question. This is a definition to agree, not a licence granted by this draft.

A **scoped reader briefing** may be useful if the buyer wants interpretation beyond the edition. Before inclusion, JGA would need to agree the audience, task, preparation, named delivery owner, time and follow-up limits, availability and cost. This is interpretation of the existing edition, without new interviews, private employer analysis, a payroll helpdesk or implementation advice. No duration or turnaround is promised.

### Keep the free benefit intact

Participating practitioners are promised the research free in Nick's concept. That remains the research, not a teaser, shortened summary or artificially restricted copy. A contributor's employer must not be forecast as an automatic second paid sale of equivalent access. Establish what they already receive and what they actually intend to do; do not presume that their employer has or lacks particular rights.

Additional briefing, clearly distinct client/public reuse or another genuine service may justify a separate offer only if wanted, deliverable and agreed. If the same use is already covered, do not charge or count it twice. CLM-0522, CLM-0506, CLM-0515–0516.

### Short concept wording, only after unaided discovery

> We are shaping a practical research pack around payroll investment and change decisions. It would bring together the dated findings, relevant case comparisons and tools for thinking through the evidence and next steps. A short, separately scoped briefing could help your team apply the material to a decision you have described. Which part, if any, would add something to what you already use?

This is wording for a future authorised exploratory conversation. B01/B02 must establish actual needs and alternatives before the concept is shown. It is not a pitch to send now, a promise of a staffed service or proof of incremental value.

## Optional variant: defined client or external use

This uses the same research edition. It changes the intended use and the preparation/permissions required, rather than inventing a second study.

For an adviser, supplier or other organisation with a named client deliverable, article, presentation or public communication, define the exact material, context, user, channel, extent, period and attribution. An optional prepared excerpt/graphic can be scoped if it is genuinely useful and JGA can authorise it. Context about dates, geography, method and small-cohort limits must survive the use. A public link is not permission to copy third-party assets.

> If you want to use parts of the research in client work or public material, we could define that use separately: which findings or graphics, where they would appear and what context needs to stay with them. That would use the same research edition, with only the additional permissions or preparation you actually need. What would you want to use, and for whom?

No unrestricted redistribution, ownership, co-branding, exclusivity, endorsement, sublicensing, resale or raw-participant access is included by assumption. Whether any is requested, feasible or appropriate remains a specific agreement question. Do not sell a variant where equivalent use is already covered. CLM-0497, CLM-0506, CLM-0509, CLM-0515–0516, CLM-0522.

## What would make this worth progressing

- A concrete decision, actual user and missing input remain after free/public/internal alternatives are considered.
- The actual evidence can support that use within its country, role, recency and qualitative limits.
- The additional output, service or permission is clear and feasible.
- A relevant owner is willing to review a defined scope. That is a possible next decision, not a sale.

Stop or change the design if equivalent material already meets the need, the buyer requires reliable population rates or guaranteed outcomes, the wanted country/decision evidence is absent, or value depends on private identities, favourable findings or rights JGA cannot authorise. Interest in an event does not establish demand for this research product.

## Delivery and model inputs to define later

A named capable owner and actual capacity are needed. The current USD1,500 commission is not proof that future editions cost the same. Count any bundled rights once. No amounts, recurring revenue, staffing capacity or turnaround have been entered here.

Identify the delivered edition and actual evidence period. Agree a correction route; future editions, fresh data, new research, recurring calls and automatic renewal are not silently included. Contract renewal, cash receipt and useful continued engagement remain separate states. CLM-0503, CLM-0509, CLM-0516.
