# T04: commercial routes and model units

30 October 2026. Internal design specification, not a calculated financial model, price recommendation or adoption decision. No new source research is needed for these relationships: they apply the reviewed T02 offers, scope baseline and existing financial-model specification.

## Four matched cases answer two decisions

| Case | Research basis | Physical events | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-PILOT | Specified initial edition/use route, matched pilot window | None | What could the research asset achieve without a dinner? |
| B-PILOT | Same core edition and horizon | One proposed UK gathering | What extra cost, work, value and funding exposure does the event add? |
| A-ANNUAL | Same selected editions and annual window as B | None | What could a research-only annual operating route require? |
| B-ANNUAL | Matched annual research basis | Proposed London, Manchester, Dublin and Texas-city-to-be-agreed | What would the four-location option add, and what must be proved before expansion? |

Model A's main route remains syndicated research sold through multiple licences without events. A specifically chosen funding partnership or briefing can coexist with that route, but is not an assumed second charge or an exclusive licence. Model B adds the event programme. Use the same core research assumptions and horizon within each pair. Do not compare one-month research sales with a full-year event programme.

The actual edition count, start/end dates and future cadence remain blank until selected for the model. Four cities do not imply four editions or a longitudinal panel. One common edition is costed once; genuine additional editions/refreshes require their own work. An annual offer is not proof of renewal, continuing participants or contracted recurring cash.

## Revenue belongs to a defined order and benefit

| Revenue line | Purchased unit | Double-counting boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RV01 Standalone research licence | Defined edition and intended use, outside an equivalent free or bundled entitlement | Do not count a free contributor copy as an automatic employer sale, or a partner's included licence again |
| RV02 Founding research partnership | One edition/purpose package with specified briefing, recognition and use benefits | The package fee occurs once; notional benefit values are not extra sales |
| RV03 Additional briefing/use preparation | Genuinely additional, wanted, scoped service or asset | Not a second charge for an included briefing or an agreed-scope correction |
| RV04 Optional event/access support | A separately defined transparent benefit, only if appropriate and agreed | No default contacts, private answers or guaranteed meetings; a combined package is not also a second package sale |

A customer organisation, partner, order, licence and copy are different units. One organisation may have more than one real nonoverlapping order, but remains one customer for unique-customer reporting. Define the actual user/use and entitlement first. Supplier marketing, adviser client work and employer reading do not automatically buy the same rights.

A signed package, invoice, receipt and fulfilled benefit also differ. Record actual or scenario cash timing separately from the planning revenue/benefit basis. This is a modelling convention to define, not a formal revenue-recognition or tax opinion. A deposit received does not prove the report, permissions, audience or service are ready.

## Costs need three useful views

1. **Decision-incremental operating contribution:** the attributable operating cash cost for the specific route/decision, with excluded committed/initial items disclosed. This answers what changes if the route is chosen; it is not a complete profitability or bank-cash measure.
2. **Fully loaded resource result:** include the real JGA research, recruitment, sales, moderation and administration time as well as supplier costs. Existing salaried/opportunity time is different from extra cash paid. Bridge those classifications so the same hours are not counted twice and unpaid founder time is not silently free.
3. **Initial investment recovery:** show the existing USD1,500 commission once where relevant, with the USD1,000 report / USD500 Table allocations retained. Do not multiply it by cities or customers, or assume future editions can always be produced at that fee. Do not call the whole nominal contract all paid or irrevocably sunk; actual payment/commitment/avoidable status remains to be established.

Each work object needs an owning cost classification. Common research and required research collection belong to the matched base; incremental live-event recruitment, hosting, travel and follow-through are added to B. Licence/briefing/rights service work belongs to the actual order or a transparently allocated shared pool. Current commissioned production is not charged again through another labour line. Costs of future production/upkeep cannot disappear merely because an initial asset exists.

Track event room terms, hospitality, drinks/service/taxes, moderation, administration, travel, insurance/other necessary items, cancellation exposure and contingency with actual inclusion boundaries. Menu-per-person and private-room minimums are different. Practitioner places, host/moderator roles and actual catering seats must reconcile; one person doing two roles is not two people. Twelve-to-fifteen practitioner places is proposed capacity, not attendance or the six-to-ten report-contributor target. The up-to-four exploratory Table contribution events are a separate research-scope count, not these commercial dinners.

## Cash, currencies and funding exposure

Preserve original GBP/EUR/USD amounts, source/date, net/gross/tax/service inclusions and the direction of any chosen exchange rate. GBP is only the proposed reporting currency. No tax recovery, universal service charge, rate or settlement outcome is assumed. Planning conversion and actual bank settlement may differ.

Cash closes as opening cash plus dated receipts minus dated payments. Deposits, late receipts, refunds and non-refundable commitments matter. A monthly schedule may need finer dates if a deposit creates an intramonth shortfall. Compare prospective exposure with funds actually confirmed and available; uncontracted expected sales are not cash already available.

## What the calculations must do later

- Event increment is B result minus matched A result. Do not attribute all research sales to dinners. Event-driven revenue uplift is a separate untested scenario assumption, if included at all.
- Break-even licences use a defined positive incremental contribution per standalone unit, with the same cost basis as the gap. If the gap is already covered, zero extra units may be needed. If a gap remains and unit contribution is zero/negative, show that the product cannot close it; do not return a false healthy zero.
- Different licence tiers need a stated mix or separate scenario, not a hidden average. Included partner rights cannot add standalone units.
- Annual totals reconcile city increments with common work once. Texas remains unchosen; do not fill a US city's cost as though confirmed.
- Separate zero sales, loss of a partner, delayed receipts, higher staff/event costs and cancellation scenarios. Blank applicable inputs remain unresolved rather than silently becoming zero.
