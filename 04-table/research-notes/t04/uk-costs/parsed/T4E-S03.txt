0063: DINING
0064: Benares has 4 private dining rooms and each can be tailored to the needs of the guest. The restaurant can be hired for exclusive events such as corporate functions, product launches and personal celebrations, accommodating 100 guests for a sit-down dinner and 250 guests for a standing reception.
0065: Please contact our Events Manager to discuss your next event at Benares or to arrange a viewing of our exclusive private dining rooms in Mayfair.
0066: If you are keen to book your festive lunch or dinner at Benares, please visit our Christmas At Benares Page for more details.
