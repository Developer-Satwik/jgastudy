0018: A private dining room of classical proportions, The Lindsay Room epitomises timeless Mayfair luxury. Named after Richard Corrigan’s first Michelin-starred restaurant, Lindsay House in Soho, this elegant space features historic photographs and career-spanning awards in polished silver frames displayed above a marble fireplace. Discreetly integrated into the room is a hidden LED screen, available for presentations or video displays as needed.
0019: On The Menu
0020: A stunning private dining room offering a seasonal set menu curated by Richard Corrigan himself. With a focus on the finest produce available, the Lindsay Room guarantees a luxurious dining experience. Optional wine pairings, caviar and canapés elevate the occasion further, making this space perfect for decadent, exclusive gatherings in the heart of Mayfair.
