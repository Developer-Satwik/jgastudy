Groups Semi Private Dining

Experience the perfect blend of intimacy and vibrant atmosphere in our two elegant semi-private dining areas.

Ideal for corporate events, family celebrations, and special occasions, these spaces comfortably accommodate up to 30 or 50 guests, offering an unforgettable setting where you can enjoy both privacy and the energy of the restaurant.

Hawksmoor Manchester is located on Deansgate, near Spinningfields.

If you’re looking for a private dining room in Manchester for your birthday party, corporate lunch or special occasion, our Private Dining Room in Manchester can seat groups of 10-14 people.

Cocktail receptions are available on arrival, there are three specially designed sharing menus to choose from, which offer a chance to sample a range of Hawksmoor classic dishes, our team can also suggest wine pairings that are tailored to your choice of menu.
