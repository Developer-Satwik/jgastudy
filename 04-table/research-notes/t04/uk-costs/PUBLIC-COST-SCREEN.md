# UK public venue-cost screen

Completed 31 October 2026. Original official pages and PDFs captured 30 October; live room pages and menu links rechecked 31 October. This is a bounded screen of three UK venue examples for T04. No venue is selected and no enquiry, quote request, reservation or spending occurred. The separate T05 model has not adopted these numbers.

The useful finding is that a room, menu, minimum spend and cash schedule are different parts of the decision. A per-person price alone cannot establish a viable private Table gathering. The whole party includes 12–15 practitioners and every additional distinct host, moderator or other required seated attendee. A person serving multiple roles occupies one seat.

Passing a numerical capacity check does not confirm privacy, acoustics, employer hospitality acceptability, accessibility arrangements, date availability or layout. Published room ranges also do not alone establish a minimum chargeable party.

Benares’s tasting menu is limited to an entire table of up to 16. It must not automatically be attached to a Dover party of 17 or more. Its paired wine price is additional to the tasting food price. The two smaller Benares rooms’ published minima are not a substitute for Berkeley or Dover terms.

## Cost and timing details worth retaining

Benares’s menu prices include VAT, followed by a 2% cover charge and a discretionary 13% service charge. Their calculation base and any interaction require actual terms; do not add VAT again, infer compounding or assume recovery. The guide requests menu selection at least 14 days ahead and final numbers 72 hours ahead. It identifies a deposit period of 15 November–30 December but supplies no amount, refund or cancellation schedule. That wording does not establish that other dates require no deposit.

Hawksmoor’s package A example is £23 per person for the specified half-bottle wine package. The arrival beer, Prosecco and Champagne variants are £30, £35.50 and £45 for the whole package, rather than additions of those totals to £23. Wine/vintage changes, non-alcohol choices and actual menu suitability remain open. The inspected menu does not settle the private-room minimum, tax/service basis or final event total.

For any selected venue, the model needs eligible minimum-spend mechanics, any separate hire fee, all attendee charges, drinks, dietary provision, actual VAT/service treatment, deposit and balance dates, cancellation/refund exposure and any required equipment. Offset only the eligible spending against a minimum where confirmed. Do not add the same minimum and food bill twice. A credited deposit belongs in cash timing and is not automatically another expense.

The January PDF, text, renders and original hashes are preserved in [the historical manifest](excluded-historical/january-manifest.json), excluded from current-price use.
