Privacy Policy

Who Are We?

iVentiv Limited (“iVentiv”) (“we”) is the operator and copyright holder of the website operated by iVentiv Limited. The Data Controller (Data User) is iVentiv Limited (“iVentiv”). iVentiv is an independent event service of Corporate Learning and Talent focused conferences.

Important: Your Consent

iVentiv is committed to protecting your data and respecting your privacy. Your choice to register with this iVentiv site is a freely made, active indication that you agree to collection and use of your personal data as set out in this Privacy Notice. If you neither understand nor agree to the use of your data in this way, please DO NOT REGISTER.

What Personal Data Do We Collect?

It is important that you understand WHAT personal data we collect. Information submitted to iVentiv Ltd is collected for the purpose of completing your registration or inquiry or otherwise servicing your request. This information is submitted voluntarily. If complete information is not submitted we may be unable to service your request(s). Information submitted may be used to provide a personalised web experience. All information submitted will be held with the strictest confidence. Information collected by iVentiv Ltd may include, but may not be limited to Name, Company, Job Title, Address, Phone and Fax Numbers, Email address. iVentiv Ltd is the sole owner of the information collected through this web site. iVentiv Ltd may also collect information, with your consent about your computer hardware and software including, but not limited to: IP Address, Operating System, Browser Used, Access Time and Duration, Referral Websites. This information is collated to enable iVentiv Ltd to better understand users computer specifications and details, and thus enable a better web experience. When you browse our sites you passively provide personal data via cookies and similar technologies which we use to build your user profile (please visit our Cookies policy page www.iventiv.com/cookies). We also collect voluntary survey responses provided by event attendees prior to our events. These responses are used to create relevant event content and identify trends.

How do we use personal data

Registering and consenting for iVentiv to use the personal data you provide, requires that you understand how we use personal data. iVentiv will use the personal data in your user profile to help ensure that any marketing we direct to you via post, telephone, email, social media and SMS is both relevant and informative. As part of iVentiv's community of excellence, if you have attended an event we will share your contact details with participants who attended the event. If you neither understand nor agree to the use of your data in this way, please contact us on 01926 800 236 or at privacy@iventiv.com and we will be happy to update your details. Occasionally, pre-event-questionaire responses are aggregated and used to produce reports about trends and to ensure event content is as relevant as possible. This may include anonymised quotations to highlight specific trends or concerns. Any responses will be anonymised when producing reports or any other materials.

Images & Video

When registering for an iVentiv event, you consent to iVentiv using photographs and/or video recordings captured at this or previous events including images of you both internally and externally to promote iVentiv events. These images could be used in print and digital media formats including print publications, websites, advertising, video, social media, and research purposes. It is also understood that the image - or any copy of it - may not be given to any third party beyond the need for the above-stated use.

Your rights

You have the right to request to see a copy of the information we hold about you and to request corrections or deletions of the information that is no longer required. You can ask iVentiv to stop using your images at any time, in which case it will not be used in future publications but may continue to appear in publications already in circulation.

Publishing

Research by iVentiv may be published online, through smartphone applications or in print. Any data we use for research and trend analysis is anonymised to protect your privacy. Quotations or data points used in our reports will not be linked to specific individuals.
