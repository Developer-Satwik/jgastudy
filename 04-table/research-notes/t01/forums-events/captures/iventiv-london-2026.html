About This Event

About This Event: 

At the invitation of Duncan Reynell, Group Talent & Development Director, we are delighted to bring you back together in London at the Lloyds Banking Group offices for this exclusive conversation with CLOs and Global Heads of Learning.

iVentiv is the market leader in events for senior L&D executives. At an iVentiv Executive Knowledge Exchange you have the opportunity to engage in real conversations with others working in similar roles in a unique, interactive format.

"Really enjoyed the two days, allowing me to expand my network and learn from peers. Well facilitated with great hosts. Thanks!"
- Head of Group Capability, Tesco
