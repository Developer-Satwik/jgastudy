# Model 06: iVentiv Learning Futures London

Desktop review, 29 October 2026. Executive Knowledge Exchange announced for 17–18 November 2026 at Lloyds Banking Group. This is a forthcoming L&D event, not an observed payroll event or proof of delivered attendance.

## Why this product

This product pairs a free qualified executive seat with paid sponsor participation and a facilitated peer agenda. Its public documentation is unusually explicit about meetings, contact sharing and pre-event inputs. That makes it useful for testing what The Table would need to sell, disclose or deliberately exclude. Quartz Network was screened as an alternative; iVentiv was selected for the clarity of these public mechanics. A payroll-specific iVentiv product was not verified, so the comparison is about the business model rather than payroll audience overlap. [London event](https://www.iventiv.com/events/learning-futures/learning-futures-london-executive-knowledge-exchange-2026)

## Data, independence and commercial boundaries

The privacy policy discloses sharing contacts with fellow event participants, voluntary pre-event input, anonymised aggregate trend reporting and an option to decline surveys or report inclusion. That demonstrates an explicit data exchange. It does not establish permission for a JGA sponsor to receive individual research answers. Nor was an independent-research publication contract or sponsor veto restriction found. Sponsor participation in sessions and sponsor choice of plenary content are clearly part of this event product. [Privacy policy](https://www.iventiv.com/privacy-policy)

Its generic event terms require payment before the event, while giving inconsistent 21-day and 14-day invoice periods. They describe credits/cancellation exposure and travel-cost exclusions. Their application to a specific sponsor order or free delegate is not clear. Treat payment dates, deposit protection, venue terms and refunds as unquoted. Do not copy an invoice interval into JGA's financial model. [Event terms](https://www.iventiv.com/terms-and-conditions)

## Implications for The Table

Analyst application: There are two different products here. A sponsor-access event sells meetings, visibility and contact opportunities. A founding research partnership sells disclosed support and defined aggregate research/reuse rights. JGA should choose and document that distinction before pricing. Research independence and participant confidentiality should not be used as marketing shorthand while also promising default sponsor contacts.

The Table's smaller discussion would need screened recruitment, seating and access rules, a facilitated agenda, participant data choices, contribution handling, aggregate-output review, separate sponsor reporting and cancellation management. These are operating requirements to plan and cost, not iVentiv cost estimates. Sponsor funds should be committed on written terms before JGA incurs non-refundable liabilities; the comparator does not establish when that will be achievable.

A lower-cost substitute is to distribute useful payroll research and hold a remote peer exchange. CPO's no-cost quarterly payroll content is a concrete reading alternative; the forum provides a remote peer route subject to its invitation terms. The incremental value of travel should be validated independently. The announced London event, published sponsor packages and positive testimonials do not demonstrate demand, margin or funding for The Table.
