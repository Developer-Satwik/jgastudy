# Model 05: PayrollOrg CPO Forum

Desktop review, 29 October 2026. Candidate comparison only. This is an ongoing senior-payroll forum with an accompanying public editorial offer, not a dinner club or a research panel.

## Why this product

PayrollOrg describes CPO as serving senior corporate payroll leadership, with an invite-only group in The Payroll Community. Its public January 2026 account gives concrete examples of peer questions about demonstrating payroll's business value and selecting executive metrics, and reports an October 2025 launch. This is closer to the proposed Table audience and recurring peer exchange than a broad payroll training conference. It is also a credible substitute that does not require travel. [About CPO](https://cpo.org/about/) · [Published forum account](https://cpo.org/forum-helps-communicate-payrolls-value-at-executive-level/)

Screened alternatives: GPA Payroll Summit 2026 is a broader payroll/HR conference and exhibition dated 5 March; CIPP's Public Sector Pay Forum is a sector-specific one-day event dated 16 July; PayrollOrg's 2026 Executive Summit is a senior forum but the reviewed account is retrospective. All those dates are past at review. CPO gives the clearest current, ongoing senior-payroll mechanism. Screening routes and limits are in model-candidates.json; those alternatives have not received the full package audit.

## Governance and operating implications

The broader community rules prohibit solicitation and advertisements, allow moderation/removal, and caution members about posting material they would not want widely known. They combine permission-seeking etiquette for republishing with a broad reuse licence for posted material. This should not be described as a confidential research environment. No CPO-specific independence policy, sponsor veto restriction, confidential research licence, or editorial timetable was verified. [Community rules, updated 2 October 2025](https://community.payroll.org/get-started/termsandconditions)

Analyst application: The Table needs an explicit contrast in delivered value: a useful decision brief, a bounded confidential contribution process and cleared aggregate findings. A private meal alone is a weaker differentiator against an existing remote peer forum. Maintaining quality would require invitation screening, prompt curation, facilitation, permission management and ongoing editorial work. These are proposed duties for JGA, not observed CPO staffing costs.

There is no basis here to insert membership revenue, retention, sponsor income or forum margins into JGA's model. Nor does CPO's existence establish demand for The Table. T03 would separately test whether suitable leaders want its particular question/research exchange. Event attendance and research contribution must remain separate records.
