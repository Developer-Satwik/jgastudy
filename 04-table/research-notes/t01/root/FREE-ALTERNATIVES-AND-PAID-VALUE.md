# Public alternatives and the paid-value question

Working T01 desktop comparison, 29 October 2026. No buyer validation or final commercial recommendation.

## What is already available

CIPP's public catalogue separates in-house, outsourced and payroll-professional report routes. It currently has conflicting heading and link edition labels, so this review establishes the visible routes only. It does not create a new 2027 dataset or resolve the existing edition conflict. [CIPP catalogue](https://www.cipp.org.uk/resources/payroll-insights-survey-series-section-landing/view-the-survey-reports.html), canonical SRC-0010, candidate T1R-C01.

Payroll Influences lists reports, white papers, buyer cases and pulse research. Named partner collaborations show that funded research is already a visible format in payroll. The public catalogue does not disclose prices, editorial rights or realised commercial returns. [Research catalogue](https://www.payrollinfluences.com/research), SRC-0013, T1R-C02.

The catalogue pages were freshly retained as original HTML with separate parsed text in [the capture manifest](capture-manifest.json). Public readability is different from permission to redistribute. Existing R01 report-level reviews, with their original dates, populations and limitations, remain the substantive evidence; this comparison adds no new survey findings.

## What a paid proposition must explain

A buyer is unlikely to need another broad trend summary merely because it has a premium dinner behind it. That is a product-design judgement to test, not an observed buyer rejection. A more specific hypothesis is a decision pack: carefully bounded original decision cases, evidence gaps, alternative responses, resource/authority requirements and a practical briefing for a named decision. The report's six linked decision views provide a starting structure, not a validated product outcome.

Three questions should guide the next step:

1. Which decision would the buyer make differently or with less uncertainty, and which free material or internal work currently supports it?
2. What evidence, recency, access, interpretation or usable tool is missing from those alternatives?
3. What exact output and internal/external reuse rights would justify payment, separately from event attendance or sponsor recognition?

These are T02/T03 hypotheses to explore. No price, unique market gap, demand forecast, customer saving or exclusivity is established by this desk work. T1R-C03.

## Keep the exchanges separate

A research licence describes output and reuse rights. A founding partner funds defined work and receives stated benefits. A practitioner contributes under specific permissions and should gain relevant peer or research value. Optional commercial introductions need their own transparent basis. A public partner logo alone establishes none of those private rights, and no named provider is assumed to grant them. T1R-C04.

This public-alternatives pass therefore supports specific buyer-use questions for the six-model comparison, not a decision to launch or a financial input. No outreach occurred.
