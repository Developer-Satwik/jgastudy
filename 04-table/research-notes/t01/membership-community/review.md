# Membership and practitioner-community comparisons

Two concrete models are ready for root integration. These are T01 public desktop comparisons, not evidence of demand for The Table, member retention, margins or a viable JGA price.

## Model 3: The Payroll Centre Corporate Payroll Membership

The annual organisational support bundle is the stronger buyer-unit comparison than CIPP personal professional membership. Its public offer serves payroll, HR and finance teams with practical support, resources and training. The named Corporate plan is displayed at GBP 1,122 per year, and the business terms say prices exclude VAT unless stated otherwise. This is a public list price on 29 October 2026, not a quote. No price or VAT gross-up is proposed for a JGA model.

Its relevance is the clarity of the recurring service unit and continuing practical use. The difference is substantial: staffed technical support and training are not a syndicated research licence, senior research council or premium event seat. The comparison-table feature marks are graphical, so flattened text does not assign every displayed benefit to every tier. The organisation should confirm the final Corporate entitlement schedule if procurement ever becomes relevant.

The terms restrict materials to authorised users and limit third-party commercial exploitation and republication. The public membership is not a licence to republish research or sell material to clients. Auto-renewal is a contract feature, not achieved retention evidence. Promotional claims about renewal and membership volume were deliberately not adopted.

CIPP remains a useful screened alternative: its actual professional grades and confidential technical advice are visible, but they are individual rather than organisation-wide units. The current tariff is unconfirmed; captured price text is not used as a current price. Magazine frequency also conflicts across its public pages. No expired CIPP tariff or numerical benefit cadence was carried into financial inputs.

## Model 4: The Conference Board Human Capital Center Europe

The Center offers research, experts, peer exchange and ongoing programming to EMEA CHROs. The separate Council layer adds invitation-only, vetted, vendor-free individual peer participation with annual fees and member-led issues. A Center membership is not automatically a Council seat; the published membership paths have different entitlements. Neither a current Center price nor a Council fee amount was established, and VAT is unknown.

This is a research-led community architecture, not a second dinner club. Its useful design lesson is to connect ongoing questions, interpretation and practical outputs between events while separating researcher judgement from member agenda input. Public information does not establish that council members form a research sample, that members buy publishable research rights, or that sponsorship permits editorial control or contact access.

The website terms distinguish limited material use from separate permission and licensing. They also describe broad rights for site submissions. That rule must not be silently transferred to confidential Council meetings. For The Table, private meeting participation, research-interview participation, aggregate publication and attributable quotes need their own explicit rules. Chatham House Rule is not a full research-consent or anonymisation protocol.

Fosway was screened as a plausible analyst-network alternative, but its public reader result did not settle an actionable membership fee/admission/entitlement schedule, and original retrieval returned HTTP 403. PayrollOrg research council was not advanced because a sufficiently clear current distinct package was not established in the bounded search. Neither is counted as a validated model.

## What remains open

- Why an actual JGA buyer would pay rather than use free professional-body reports or public content.
- Whether practitioner value survives without a premium dinner, and what between-event work members want.
- The licensed-use unit and sponsor/editorial separation, plus a scoped confidentiality/publication protocol.
- Staff hours, recruitment difficulty, service workload, confirmed prices and the eventual financial scenarios.
- T03 real buyer/member conversations. No contacts, signups, private accounts, forms or spending were used.
