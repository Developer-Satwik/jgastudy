# T01-M03: Corporate Payroll Membership

Provider: The Payroll Centre

Status: public desktop comparison, 29 October 2026. Actual buyers/members and T03 remain untested.

## Selection

A concrete annual organisation-wide payroll support bundle, with public fee unit and business terms. Closer to employer purchase/account budgeting than CIPP individual professional grades. Distinct from event sponsorship and research licensing.

## Buyer decision

Whether continuing payroll guidance, team training and shared practical resources justify an organisation-funded annual subscription. Actual payer/decision maker not validated; payroll lead with finance/L&D procurement is a buyer hypothesis.

## Audience

Payroll, HR and finance teams and organisations running payroll in house; all experience levels are described. Not restricted to senior payroll directors.

## Admission

Public plan application; provider discretion to accept or decline. No verified senior-only practitioner screening.

## Benefit and value unit

Annual team-support access. Entitlements depend on plan; neither a research publication right nor ownership of materials.

## Funding and revenue

Subscription fees are publicly offered. Overall revenue mix, cross-subsidy, realised retention and profitability unknown.

## Rights and restrictions

Named member and authorised-user boundaries, limited non-transferable materials licence, restrictions on third-party use and republication. Membership is not a research resale licence.

## Research and editorial boundaries

Primarily ongoing advice/training/resource access. Public offer does not disclose a paid original-research sample, recruitment method or sponsor editorial rights. Any policy/market research claim would need separate source review.

## Contact access

No automatic sponsor or prospect contact access inferred. This is not a supplier lead-list purchase.

## Free alternatives

Official employer guidance and publicly discoverable professional-body research/news can answer routine information needs. They do not replicate a staffed, scoped helpdesk or guarantee an answer to a specific case. SRC-0010 remains discovery-only, with no upgrade of its status.

## Operating work, analyst inference

- Maintain and review time-sensitive payroll content and resources.
- Staff and triage technical queries with clear limits and expert escalation.
- Produce and host live/on-demand learning; administer users, support and renewals.

## Conditional lessons for JGA, analyst inference

- Make the paid unit explicit: organisational research-use rights, individual council participation and advisory work are different products.
- State a recurring decision/use case and delivery cadence beyond hospitality.
- Cost research upkeep and member-service time; do not assume a recruiter can supply technical payroll advice.

## Limits of transfer

- Does not validate willingness to buy The Table.
- Fee includes materially different services from a JGA research licence, so no price anchoring.
- No achieved retention or profitability data adopted, even though promotional pages contain such claims.
- Actual buyer/member interviews and T03 remain open.

## Alternatives screened
