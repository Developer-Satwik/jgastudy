CIPP payroll membership
Achieve your professional payroll goals
CIPP membership is designed to support you through every stage of your career. There’s a membership level for everyone, with a wide range of benefits designed to support, educate and assist you in achieving your professional goals.
From the support of our Advisory Service, regular, informative webinars with our Policy and Research team, to our Professional magazine and discounts on IPPE training courses, membership with the CIPP can help you gain the recognition and support you deserve.
Becoming a member of the CIPP has enabled me to join a large community of support, knowledge and advice and gives me peace of mind that I have access to the latest information. It also ensures I receive change updates and can keep my CPD up to date.
Helen Sherwood ACIPP - HR Manager at Languageline Solutions
