FAQ
What is Payroll Membership?
Payroll Membership provides your team with ongoing access to expert payroll support, training, and resources to help you manage payroll accurately, confidently, and in line with current legislation.
Who is Payroll Membership designed for?
Payroll Membership is designed for payroll professionals, finance teams, HR teams, and businesses responsible for running payroll in-house, regardless of size or sector.
How many people in my team can use the membership?
Membership supports your entire payroll team, allowing multiple users to access support, training, and resources under one membership.
What type of support is included?
Members receive access to expert payroll guidance via the helpdesk, along with practical resources, updates, and training to support day-to-day payroll queries and more complex scenarios.
Is the training live or on demand?
Training includes both live sessions and on-demand video content, so your team can learn in real time or catch up at a time that suits their workload.
Do I need a certain level of payroll experience to join?
No. Payroll Membership supports all experience levels, from those new to payroll through to experienced professionals looking to stay compliant and up to date.
How do I access the resources and training?
All resources, guidance, and training are accessed online through the member portal, making it easy for your team to find information whenever they need it.
Does Payroll Membership help with payroll compliance?
Yes. Membership helps you stay informed of payroll legislation changes, best practice, and guidance, supporting compliance throughout the year.
How long does the membership last?
Payroll Membership runs on an annual basis, giving you continuous support and access throughout the year.
How do I get started?
Simply choose the membership option that best suits your payroll team and complete the sign-up process. Access is provided shortly after joining.
