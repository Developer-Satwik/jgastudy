Our Commitment to Equality, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI) Statement
At CIPP and IPP Education Ltd, we are committed to creating a culture where everyone is treated with dignity, fairness and respect. We recognise that equality, diversity and inclusion are fundamental to delivering high-quality education, professional membership services and employment opportunities.
We value the unique experiences, perspectives and contributions that individuals bring to our organisation and are committed to providing an environment where people feel welcomed, supported and able to achieve their full potential.
We actively promote equality of opportunity and do not tolerate discrimination, harassment, bullying or victimisation of any kind. Our commitment extends to all employees, learners, members, customers, contractors and partners, regardless of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by law.
Through inclusive practices, accessible services, continuous learning and regular review of our policies and procedures, we strive to remove barriers, celebrate diversity and foster a sense of belonging for everyone who engages with our organisation.
We believe that embracing diversity strengthens our organisation, enriches learning and professional development, and helps us better serve the communities we support.
Signed by: Jason Davenport, CEO
Date of Issue: 02 June 2026
