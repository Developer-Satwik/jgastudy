Meet the Advisory team

One of the most popular membership benefits, the advisory service provides essential advice, support and guidance through their technical helpline.

Our Advisory Service officers Chevonne Wild MCIPPdip, Monica Blajut MCIPPdip, Marie Bennett MCIPPdip and Leanne Sinclair MCIPPdip provide members with up to date information on a whole range of payroll, pensions and reward questions.

All calls made to the Advisory Service are treated in absolute confidence. Your membership number will be required in order for the team to answer your query. 

CIPP members can contact the team during the office hours of Monday – Thursday 9 am – 5 pm and Friday 9 am – 4.30 pm via 0121 712 1099 or email [email protected].

Visit our most frequently asked questions page where you can find the top questions we get asked. You could find the answer to your question there.
