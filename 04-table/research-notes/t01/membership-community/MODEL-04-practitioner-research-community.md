## Selection

A current EMEA research-led leadership offer with expert access and programming, plus explicitly distinct vetted peer-council membership. Stronger comparison for research/community architecture than selecting a second dinner club.

## Buyer decision

Whether senior leaders or employers should fund recurring research, expert interpretation and relevant peer exchange. Any exact business case, renewal motive or buyer authority is unvalidated.

## Audience

Center Europe names CHROs across EMEA; broader membership paths address executives and leadership teams. Council eligibility is vetted and role-specific; not a claim that payroll directors automatically qualify.

## Admission

Council is invitation-only and vetted. Center membership enquiry is not equivalent to Council admission. No application or private membership reviewed.

## Benefit and value unit

Center/organisation research and team support depends on membership path; Council is a distinct individual peer-seat unit. No standard bundle assumed.

## Funding and revenue

Public membership architecture and annual fee for Council seats. Nonprofit status is provider-declared. Exact fee schedule, funding mix, sponsorship contribution and margins unknown.

## Rights and restrictions

Website material remains rights-controlled; separate permission is needed beyond the stated licence. Internal member access or Council participation does not grant a commercial research republication or resale licence.

## Research and editorial boundaries

Research catalogue, experts and programmes coexist with member-led Council topics. No universal claim that councils provide representative research data, that all members are study respondents, or that agenda influence is editorial control. ## Contact access

- Separate member-led questions from editorial findings, methodology and publication decisions.
- Create between-event practical outputs and a clear reason to return.
