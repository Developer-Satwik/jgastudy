# Licensed analyst intelligence: ISG Buyers Guide

Reviewed 29 October 2026. T01 desktop comparable; not buyer validation or an adopted Table model.

## Concrete offer and selection

ISG's enterprise Buyers Guide subscription and software-provider licence packages are the selected example. Their public service pages separate access for decision-makers from permission to use research assets in marketing. This is clearer commercial-unit evidence than the screened NelsonHall vendor announcements and reprints.

The concrete payroll example is the Global Payroll Buyers Guide 2025, dated 17 December 2025. It is an earlier edition available at review, not a new 2026 study. Its public executive summary assesses 14 eligible software providers and explains its category, RFI-style framework and inclusion rules. [T1RP-S06](https://research.isg-one.com/buyers-guide/business-technologies/employees-and-hcm/global-payroll/2025)

## Two buyers and two uses

Enterprise and service-provider subscribers use published research and analyst context to assess existing suppliers and build software shortlists. ISG describes annual access to market reports and a 30-minute research-director inquiry with each guide. The full report adds detailed results and inclusion/product lists beyond the executive summary. [T1RP-S04](https://research.isg-one.com/research/buyers-guides/services/enterprise-and-service-providers)

Software-provider marketing and sales teams can buy packages including consultation and licensed reports/graphics for marketing-funnel or sales use. ISG distinguishes provider report, snapshot and insight formats. Those are different depths of output, not interchangeable pricing units. [T1RP-S05](https://research.isg-one.com/research/buyers-guides/services/software-providers)

These offers show commercial access and licensing routes. They do not establish sales volume, renewal rates, contracted recurring cash or financial performance.

## Method, participation and independence

ISG states that eligible software suppliers are invited to evaluation at no cost. Incomplete participants can be assessed from public information, with an explicit warning that this may affect ratings. This is an eligibility-bound vendor assessment, not a representative practitioner survey. The publisher calls the research independent and says rankings supplement the buyer's own evaluation rather than guarantee suitability. These declarations were not independently audited. [T1RP-S06]

There is no evidence here that buying a licence purchases a better rank. Conversely, marketing statements alone do not establish the actual contractual protections against commercial influence.

## Price, rights and access limits

- Enterprise unit: annual published-research access with bounded analyst inquiry. Exact price, seats, internal sharing and contract mechanics are not public in the reviewed text.
- Provider unit: separately scoped report/graphic licence. Price, licence period, territory, channels, exclusivity, copying and sublicensing are unknown.
- A public report or executive summary does not convey reproduction rights. Enterprise reading access does not imply external promotional use.
- Research-director inquiry is analyst access, not buyer contacts. No individual raw-data, respondent-list or vendor-contact entitlement is stated.

## Operating work and alternatives

Analyst inference: reliable delivery needs category/inclusion rules, evidence review, consistent assessment, report/version control, rights administration and capacity to service inquiries. None of these operating costs is established by public package language.

The public executive summary already supplies headline findings and method; an enterprise can also run its own supplier RFI. Paid value must be deeper decision support, current evidence or explicitly authorised use, rather than withholding generic headlines. [T1RP-S06]

## Conditional lesson for JGA

If Table research is licensed, distinguish internal-use access from separately agreed external-use rights and cap any included briefing. Do not copy analyst ratings or describe a small invited council as a validated market benchmark. Buyer demand, rights, quality and servicing cost need separate validation. No ISG price is imported into a JGA forecast, and no annual programme is assumed to be contracted revenue.
