# Sponsored research: HR.com Custom Research

Reviewed 29 October 2026. T01 desktop comparable; not buyer validation or an adopted Table model.

## Concrete product and evidence boundary

HR.com's publicly accessible Custom Research help article describes a topic-specific custom survey and branded report. It was last updated on 30 April 2021; the linked advertising page returned 403. The package's current availability and full terms therefore remain unverified. [T1RP-S01](https://hrcom.kb.help/whats-included-in-hrcoms-custom-research/)

A separate concrete output is HR.com's February 2025 Future of Payroll report. Its cover names IRIS as sponsor; its final page includes sponsor and publisher profiles. This establishes sponsorship recognition in an actual payroll report. It does not establish that IRIS bought the Custom Research package, how much it paid, or whether funding was cash or in kind. The retained canonical PDF, SRC-0065, was visually checked on pages 1, 3 and 59.

## Buyer, audience and value

The potential commercial buyer is an organisation seeking topic-specific branded research for thought leadership. Practitioners receive findings; the sponsor receives visible association in the observed output. Neither conversion, qualified lead delivery nor a sale is demonstrated.

The generic recruitment FAQ describes interest-based emails to subscribed HR.com members, used to produce reports and infographics. This is a recruitment channel, not representative sampling. [T1RP-S02](https://hrcom.kb.help/how-does-hrcom-collect-data-for-their-research/)

The observed payroll survey reports 206 HR-professional responses collected November 2024 to January 2025, mostly North American, especially US. It is not evidence about a UK-only council of senior payroll directors. The report describes advisory-board input to survey development and invites research-influencer participation with rewards; the exact incentive for this study is unknown. [SRC-0065](https://www.irisglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2-26-25-HRdotcom-Future-of-Payroll-2025-Research-Report-IRIS_V3.pdf)

## Commercial unit and rights

- Unit: custom survey plus branded report; any sponsorship package needs its own scope. It is not a dinner seat or an annual membership.
- Paid price: not public in the accessible reviewed material. Currency, package term, distribution obligations and sponsor exclusivity are unknown.
- Rights: branded output is described at a broad level. Copyright ownership, reuse, sublicensing and publication rights are unverified.
- Independence: no sponsor-veto, publication or negative-findings protection was established. Advisory participation is not proof of an editorial firewall.
- Contacts and individual data: no entitlement to respondent lists, introductions or raw responses was established.

## Operating implications and free alternatives

Analyst inference: this product requires question/audience design, permissioned recruitment, response checks, analysis, report production, distribution and separately agreed sponsor/editorial/privacy terms. The reviewed routes disclose no production costs or operating margin.

Existing HR.com research is available free after account sign-in. Public access is not a commercial redistribution licence. A new paid study must solve a specific unmet decision rather than duplicate free headline information. [T1RP-S03](https://hrcom.kb.help/does-it-cost-money-to-download-hrcom-research/)

## Conditional lesson for JGA

If a buyer funds a Table research cycle, sell a defined research output and licence, with sponsor recognition separated from editorial control, contacts and individual records. Verify the buyer's intended use, willingness to pay and delivery cost before adoption. This example supplies no JGA price, revenue forecast, sponsor commitment or launch recommendation.

Evidence: T1RP-C01 to C06; three new original HTML captures plus canonical SRC-0065. No external contact, account, form, quotation or spending.
