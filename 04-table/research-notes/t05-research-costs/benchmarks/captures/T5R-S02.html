Copywriter Survey 2026

573 respondents · Fieldwork February · Published July 2026

What UK commercial writers charged, how they worked and what they thought about AI in 2026.

Download the full report (PDF)
Copywriter Survey
2026
573 UK commercial writers

Headline findings

¾
of copywriters use generative AI
AI adoption

Use and concern rise together

Almost three-quarters of copywriters now use generative AI. The share rating their concern at 7 out of 10 or higher has nearly doubled in two years.

74%, up from 69% in 2025

£440
median freelance day rate
Day rates

Still climbing

The median freelance day rate is £440, up 10% in two years. Clients are paying more for strategy, not less.

£430 in 2025 (median)

64%
would erase generative AI
AI sentiment

Most would switch it off

Given a button that erased generative AI from the world, 64% would press it. A year earlier it was 54%.

54% in 2025

31%
median gap, men over women
Gender pay gap

The widest we've recorded

Men earn a median 31% more than women, the biggest like-for-like gap in the survey’s history. Only copywriters who mix freelance and employed work closed it.

No median published in 2025; the mean gap was 21% in both years
