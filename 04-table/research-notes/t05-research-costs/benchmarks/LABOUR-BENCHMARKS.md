## How the evidence should shape the worksheet

CIEP's figures are suggested fee minima, rather than observed averages or enforced prices. Its explanation includes freelancers' leave, pension, contributions, equipment and running costs; those fees are not comparable directly with employee pay. Do not add the same business overhead a second time. Tax treatment still needs its own explicit basis.

ProCopywriters' median supports a writing-cost comparison. It does not price research design, recruitment, qualitative collection, coding, analysis and verification. The total sample is not the number answering the rate question. Day length and VAT basis are not supplied in the inspected summary, so converting £440 to an hourly rate requires a labelled model assumption.

YunoJuno describes more than 182,000 mixed platform data points across 2024–25, covering profiles, bookings, rates and other activity. That is neither a unique-person sample nor the observation count for either selected role. Its rate definition uses approved contractor rates and confirmed bookings; the publisher calls the averages directional. UK GBP/day and US USD/hour refer to different markets and must not be treated as currency/day-length conversions. Individual role pages do not separately publish their exact observation dates or sample counts.

The graphic-designer role value also differs from the wider Designers discipline average (£385/day) in the report. Select a scope deliberately; do not switch between them because one makes the model look preferable. Neither figure establishes a minimum booking, platform fee, all-in report fee or available supplier.

## Cost choices to keep explicit

1. Retain the current USD1,500 commission and its agreed report/Table split as the actual first-project contract basis. These market references do not amend it or justify a new fee.
2. Build a future replacement-cost comparison from defined tasks and estimated effort. A researcher, writer and editor can cover different tasks, but charging each for the same complete work would overstate cost. A single person performing several roles still has one reconciled time record.
3. Preserve the differences between suggested minimum, survey median and platform role average. They are not lower/base/upper statistical bounds and should not be averaged into an invented “market rate”.
4. Keep JGA review, introductions, commercial work and management time visible. Actual employee resource cost requires the relevant employment-cost and productive-time basis; contractor rate references are not a substitute.
5. Define the paid work unit before conversion: billed days, hours, minimum blocks and revision/scope terms. The published day rates do not establish a seven- or eight-hour day, nor any task's required duration.
6. Label any selected benchmark, conversion, workload and tax allowance as a scenario input until actual terms are supplied. UK references do not establish Indian outsourcing cost, Riseklix's internal margin, payroll-specialist quality or actual availability.
