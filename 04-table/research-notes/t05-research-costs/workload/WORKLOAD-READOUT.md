## Keep the current fee and future effort distinct

The current agreement commissions report/supporting assets for USD1,000 and Table feasibility for USD500, total USD1,500. Ordinary research, analysis, writing, design, handover and Provider-chargeable taxes are included. The reported USD750 upfront and completion-triggered USD750 balance are payment states, not sponsor income. These workload candidates do not change those terms, justify an extra invoice or establish what future suppliers would charge.

For JGA's current external cash cost, use the applicable agreed fee once. For future production, choose an actual supplier package fee or a bottom-up cost of the tasks—or reconcile a known split where different providers perform different work. Adding the full package price and all producer task costs for the same work would double count. JGA retained time can still appear separately in the full-resource view.

WR16 covers the report's pricing/licensing recommendations. If the same actual analysis also informs Table feasibility, count that work once with an explicit allocation. It is not the full Table commercial assessment.

## Separate units, only when selected

The [optional unit candidates](optional-unit-candidates.json) keep four kinds of work outside the report totals:

- Exploratory Table validation: setup plus each actual substantive buyer/member contribution and synthesis. A four-event illustration is 16/25/39 provider hours before any actual shared-work credit. It is not four dinners, four extra report people or permission to exceed the current up-to-four scope.
- Customer use preparation, order fulfilment and bounded briefings: effort follows actual tasks, including benefits already bundled; modelling their cost does not make them another sale.
- Commercial proposal work: count unsuccessful qualified proposals too. General prospecting/sales management still needs a separate programme input.
- Event preparation, incremental member scheduling, moderation and optional follow-through: separate from common research production. Travel, venue/hospitality and other direct costs are not labour zeroes. A host who also moderates has one seat and actual nonoverlapping work time.

An eligible permitted report/council account can serve both workstreams. Credit only the scheduling, collection or coding actually reused; Table-specific questions and analysis still take time. No arbitrary overlap percentage is applied.

## What to resolve in the workbook

Assign actual sourcing/roles and an explicit rate basis to each task; choose whether a rate is external cash or retained staff resource; retain any day-to-hour conversion assumption; show selected scope, quantity and uncertainty. Public day rates are context for an appropriate work type, not proof of JGA's salaries or a complete fixed report price.

Participant incentives, paid tools/datasets, translation, specialist legal work and travel are not verified zero costs. They remain applicable-scope choices. No spending or collection is authorised here. One consolidated revision allowance is planning effort, not a cap on contractual corrections or a new automatic fee.

The [structured candidates](workload-candidates.json) retain each task's purpose, boundaries, role and DW mapping. [Verification](verification.json) checks arithmetic and scope only; it does not validate future delivery time. Existing core questions, agreements and delivery maps are unedited.
