0014: Market Insights: Payroll & Reward
0015: Market Overview
0016: The payroll market is growing steadily, both in the UK and globally, driven by increasing workforce complexity, stricter compliance requirements, and the continued shift towards cloud based HR technology. Globally, organisations are adopting automated and integrated payroll platforms to manage multinational workforces more efficiently, while in the UK, demand is also influenced by regulatory changes such as the IR35 Off Payroll Working Rules and evolving employment regulations.
0017: Tech automation is becoming increasingly embedded in payroll systems as companies adopt more integrated and data-driven solutions to support calculations, identify anomalies, strengthen compliance monitoring, and provide predictive workforce insights. At the same time, organisations are seeing a rise in dual or blended roles, where payroll professionals are taking on responsibilities across benefits, compensation, reward, and broader HR functions, reflecting a shift towards more integrated people operations. To discuss the payroll market in more detail, or to explore how these trends are shaping hiring across payroll, please contact Stacey Radford, Senior Manager – Payroll
0018: The reward market continues to evolve as organisations place greater focus on total reward strategies that go beyond salary alone. We are seeing growing demand for reward professionals who can support pay transparency, internal equity, benchmarking, benefits, and incentive design, as businesses look to build fair, competitive frameworks that strengthen attraction, retention, and overall employee value proposition. To discuss the market in more detail, or to explore how these trends are shaping hiring across reward and analytics, please contact Marie Robery-Bliss, Senior Manager, Reward and Analytics.
