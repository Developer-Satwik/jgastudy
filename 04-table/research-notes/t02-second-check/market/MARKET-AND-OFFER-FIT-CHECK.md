# T02 second challenge: market and offer fit

30 October 2026. Selective challenge of the actual TP01–TP03 drafts, not a repeat of T01's six-model survey. Three useful refinements remain within the present design. They do not establish a sale, price, permission, staffing capacity or market demand.

## 1. A relevant label is not a relevant evidence payload

The five contractual buyer categories remain sensible hypotheses. They do not mean one small cohort will contain equally useful evidence for every task, country or decision. Before promising a decision reading path, map the actual material that can be released to the requested use. A collected account, an analyzable account and a case that can be published with enough context are different objects.

This selectively applies QQE's emphasis on context, achieved coverage and inference. ISG's payroll guide also illustrates a deliberately scoped product assessment with suitability and participation limits; its ratings are not transferred to this project. [QQE](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/quality-in-qualitative-evaluation-qqe-html) · [ISG guide](https://research.isg-one.com/buyers-guide/business-technologies/employees-and-hcm/global-payroll/2025). TM2-C01.

This is not a new minimum case count, country quota or requirement to satisfy every segment. A bounded case can be useful. If the actual evidence supports only exploratory context, describe that use honestly. A narrower use or no-fit conclusion does not replace the complete contracted report, weaken the free practitioner benefit or create new study obligations.

## 2. Interpretation cannot create missing research

ISG separately describes an inquiry about published findings and an additional purchased service. The useful Table application is to define where a briefing interprets an existing edition and where a request needs new evidence or client-specific work. A reading path can select and explain relevant material; it cannot supply facts absent from the edition. [ISG enterprise service](https://research.isg-one.com/research/buyers-guides/services/enterprise-and-service-providers). TM2-C02.

For example, a consulting client's new country/vendor assessment is not automatically a small formatting change to the existing report. It may require a narrowed answer, a statement of what remains unknown or separately agreed work. Tailored interpretation is still possible; the distinction is the actual work/evidence needed, not the organisation label. No new service, duration or price is promised.

## 3. A partner benefit needs its own delivery basis

The public iVentiv offer itemises commercial benefits. For the different Table research partnership, specify the actual acknowledgement, agreed use asset or briefing being supplied. A logo placement can be delivered without proving any particular readership or commercial response. Its presence is not evidence of reach, leads or impact. [Sponsor inventory](https://www.iventiv.com/sponsors). TM2-C03.

A transparent separately defined event/access combination remains possible. Nothing here grants contacts or meetings, imports the provider's terms, or establishes that a funder wants the research combination. Keep promised deliverables and any later measured effect separate in both the package and the financial model.

## Consequence for the three drafts

- TP01 remains a possible edition/read-path product, provided the use matches actual releasable evidence. Optional briefing is interpretation with a work boundary.
- TP02 remains a defined funding/briefing/recognition/use combination. State each benefit's fulfilment object, without replacing that with speculative visibility or sales returns.
- TP03 retains free research and possible useful peer exchange. Limited publishable detail does not justify withholding the promised research, inventing a paid restriction or treating one industry question as guaranteed bespoke research.

The [three conditions](selective-conditions.json) include constructed counterexamples. The [blank fit note](blank-offer-fit-note.json) is an optional analyst aid, not another participant form. All actual fields are empty. The existing three propositions, four hypotheses, fourteen report questions, Table contribution cap and inactive collection arrangements remain unchanged. No financial input or canonical record was edited by this author.
