# T02 historical check: edition, evidence and later service

30 October 2026. Focused second challenge of the concrete Table offers. This does not repeat the earlier programme-retention review or adopt a new service promise.

## Three times describe different things

The retained ISG service page offers annual research access and a bounded context inquiry. Its Global Payroll guide is dated December 2025, while individual evaluated product versions/dates appear separately. These observations do not establish a JGA refresh cycle. The useful Table distinction is edition/publication time, evidence/case period and the actual briefing/service date. A new reading path or briefing cannot make an older source current merely by being delivered later. TH2-C01.

## A correction is different from new evidence

ONS separates repair of a published mistake from revisions using later information or improved methods. That narrow distinction is a useful analogy; its statistical release rules and exceptions are not imported. A Table change record should say why something changed and which edition/use it affects. TH2-C02.

For the concrete offers, triage an error or agreed-scope defect, a genuinely later evidence refresh and a newly requested user task separately. Preserve what was supplied rather than silently relabel an old edition. This is not a mechanism to charge for fixing defects: the working JGA agreement includes factual/formula/scope remedies without extra fees, and requires an agreed written change for expanded work. No request, price, support period or service-level rule is classified or created here. TH2-C03.
