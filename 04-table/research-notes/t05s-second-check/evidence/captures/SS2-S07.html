Payroll Search
Payroll Search on a Global Scale
Everything we do in payroll is driven by our deep proficiency. We understand the technical complexity, regulatory exposure and organisational dependencies that distinguish payroll from other functions, and what effective leadership looks like across different operating models, markets and stages of growth.

Our practice brings nearly two decades of specialist recruitment experience to permanent appointments, interim leadership and transformation programmes. We advise CPOs, finance leaders and senior payroll professionals on securing the expertise to strengthen delivery, establish effective governance and lead complex change.

Our credibility rests on established industry relationships and a detailed understanding of the work our clients need people to deliver. We assess technical capability alongside leadership judgement, stakeholder influence and the ability to operate within each organisation’s culture and commercial environment. That depth allows us to challenge a brief constructively, calibrate expectations and distinguish relevant experience from an impressive title.

Beyond the Shortlist

We believe recruitment partnerships should deliver value well beyond the delivery of a shortlist. Our team invests time in understanding each organisation’s strategy, structure, cultural alignment, and future capability requirements, enabling us to act as an informed extension of the client’s business. Our talented search team provides honest market feedback, realistic salary guidance, and access to highly regarded payroll professionals who are both exclusive to our brand and not active on the market.

As global payroll becomes more strategic, data‑led and technology‑enabled, organisations require leaders who can balance operational accuracy and compliance with transformation and commercial thinking. Middlemore enables businesses to secure this capability while supporting payroll professionals in advancing their careers.

Clients work with us for informed advice, candid market intelligence and access to respected professionals whose next move is shaped through trusted relationships. Candidates engage with a partner who understands their expertise and can offer a considered view of their next appointment. Both receive the discretion, rigour and personal accountability expected of a specialist search partner.
