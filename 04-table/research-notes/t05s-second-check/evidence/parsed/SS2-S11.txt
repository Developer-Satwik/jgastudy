 Analyst Access Policy 
Analyst Access Policy 
Each Forrester license purchased by a client is a User License that entitles its holder to specific benefits. One of the benefits for specific User Licenses is the ability to schedule, attend, and participate in either inquiry or guidance sessions, depending on their license. This policy, in addition to our Terms of Use, Master Services Agreements, and Purchase Agreements, defines what is acceptable and allowable as part of a User License with respect to scheduling, attending, and participating in inquiry and guidance sessions.
Inquiry Sessions 
Inquiry sessions are engagements with a single Forrester analyst:
For the benefit of Forrester Market Insights Leader, Emerging Growth Leader, and AI Access Plus license holders.
Delivered in a 30-minute audio or video session or via email.
Covers no more than three questions related to a specific piece of research, technology, or the client’s market.
Can be submitted as needed; back-to-back inquiry sessions or multiple inquiry sessions on the same topic are prohibited.
May not be recorded or transcribed.
Attendance and participation:
Inquiry sessions must be scheduled for, attended by, and address the questions of the Forrester Market Insights Leader, Emerging Growth Leader, or AI Access Plus license holder.
Forrester Market Insights Team license holders are permitted to attend, though participation is limited to the Forrester Market Insights Leader or Emerging Growth Leader license holder, since inquiry sessions are to address the questions of and for the benefit of the Leader license holder.
Individuals without a Forrester Market Insights Leader, Forrester Market Insights Emerging Growth Leader, Forrester Market Insights Team, or AI Access Plus license may not attend inquiry sessions.
Depending on the specific contract, Group Reader Access or Reader Access Plus license holders may also be able to attend inquiry sessions. Contact your account manager or client success manager for details.
Forrester Market Insights Leader and Emerging Growth Leader license holders are not permitted to invite license holders to join their inquiry sessions on a frequent or recurring basis. When additional attendees join an inquiry session, Forrester will record and share their names with the Forrester account team and account administrator. Repeat additional attendees will be prompted to purchase an appropriate license or product to better meet their needs.
A third party may attend inquiry sessions only if they are licensed according to our contractor licensing policy. Third parties may also conduct an approved perception survey so long as they conform to the limitations described in the section below.
Inquiry sessions may be used to ask about:
A specific piece of research available to the client.
A technology.
The client’s markets (customers, competitors, trends).
An analyst’s research agenda or coverage (permitted subject to analyst availability).
Inquiry sessions may not be used:
To brief an analyst.
To get detailed feedback on a client’s initiatives: such as how to implement a project or to get feedback on a client’s internal documents/go-to-market messaging.
(The above type of feedback is available to Team Executive, Team Leader, Team Member, Executive, and Leader license holders in a Forrester Decisions service or through our consulting offerings.)
To receive feedback on other Forrester engagements (e.g., briefings and advisory sessions).
To conduct document or messaging reviews.
(The above types of feedback are available through our advisory and consulting offerings.)
To discuss the details of evaluative research, such as a Forrester Wave™, including inclusion criteria.
For back-to-back inquiry sessions or multiple inquiry sessions on the same topic.
Clients who would like more detailed, custom feedback than we can offer in an inquiry session may inquire with their account manager about Forrester’s advisory services.
Perception surveys
Forrester allows Forrester Market Insights Leader license holders to get feedback from multiple analysts on their strategy or event in limited circumstances. These “perception” surveys are typically designed to gather tactical feedback from analysts and are not a replacement for analyst interactions such as advisory sessions.
Forrester Market Insights Leader license holders seeking to conduct these surveys should submit the request to our inquiry service (inquiry@forrester.com) and copy their Forrester account manager. The following limitations apply:
Clients must limit the surveys to one survey per account per year.
Surveys must be limited to a maximum of 10 analysts per survey.
Surveys may take no more than 20 minutes for an analyst to complete.
Surveys should mostly avoid open-ended questions and/or allow for 1–2-sentence responses. Surveys should always include an option for analysts to pass on questions if they are not able to respond.
The individual responsible for the survey must be a Forrester Market Insights Leader license holder.
Survey results may only be used for the benefit of the Forrester Market Insights Leader license holder.
Survey results are for the internal use of the client with inquiry access and may not be used in any external communications.
See the inquiry FAQ page for further guidance.
Guidance Sessions 
Guidance sessions are designed to provide ongoing support for functional leaders to advance their initiatives through Forrester analyst advice and the application of research findings, best practices, tools, frameworks, and data.
They are defined as engagements between a Forrester analyst and an eligible Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder, where the analyst provides the license holder with advice, guidance, and/or recommendations based on existing research, their areas of expertise, and/or application of Forrester tools, templates, or data.
Criteria and limits of guidance sessions:
Addresses 1–3 specific questions.
Topic is aligned to an entitled Forrester research coverage area(s).
Topic can be answered/addressed thoroughly in 50 minutes or less.
Involves only one analyst at a time unless otherwise determined by Forrester.
Analyst prep time is about 1 hour or less.
No client-specific deliverables are developed either before or after the session.
Includes only individuals with active eligible licenses at that account.
Guidance sessions are unmetered in most Forrester products and can be submitted as needed, but back-to-back guidance sessions are prohibited. They can also be recorded and transcribed by Forrester (see guidelines here).
Attendance and participation:
Guidance sessions must be attended by and address the questions of an individual with a Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license.
Forrester Decisions Team and Team Member license holders are permitted to attend guidance sessions that are scheduled to address the questions of and for the benefit of the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, or Team Leader license holder.
Team Executive, Team Leader, and Team Member license holders may invite a limited number of nonlicensed individuals (guests) to join their guidance sessions providing the individuals have been registered in advance with the license holder’s Forrester account team to ensure proper coordination.
The guidance session must be scheduled by, attended by, and address the questions of and be for the benefit of the Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder.
Guest attendees must be employees of the license holder’s organization.
Guest attendance by nonlicensed users is at Forrester’s sole discretion and may not be used in lieu of a Forrester license. Forrester reserves the right to monitor attendance on guidance sessions and record names of nonlicensed attendees. Repeat attendees will be required to purchase an appropriate license to meet their continued usage needs.
Individuals with a Forrester Decisions Executive or Leader license may not invite nonlicense holders to their guidance sessions.
Depending on the specific contract, other license holders may also be able to attend guidance sessions. Contact your account manager or client success manager for clarification.
Forrester Decisions Executive and Leader license holders should not invite other Forrester Decisions license holders to join their guidance sessions on a frequent or recurring basis. When additional attendees join, Forrester will record and share their names with the Forrester account team and account administrator. Repeat attendees will be required to purchase the appropriate license or different product that better meets their continued needs. A third party may attend Guidance Sessions only if they are licensed according to our contractor licensing policy.
Guidance sessions may be used by individuals with Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, and Team Member licenses to:
Get answers to key questions within their designated area of Forrester’s research.
Receive guidance applying frameworks and models.
Seek best-practice advice on specific challenges.
Get feedback on the output of a Forrester assessment, tool, or template.
Receive written feedback on documents such as planning templates, org charts, messaging, RFPs, or product roadmaps.
Analysts will provide up to one review of each document (of up to 20 pages or equivalent) total, with a scope limit of 1–3 specific questions and no more than 1 hour of prep.
Request data and benchmarks through a data guidance session.
Guidance sessions may not be used to:
Access research or advice outside of the license holder’s designated service.
Request custom work from the analyst either before or after the guidance session.
Engage, educate, or inform nonlicense holders of the client account.
To review a document longer than 20 pages (or equivalent), even if the request is to review only a portion of that document.
To conduct back-to-back sessions (e.g., a guidance session at 2 p.m. and then another one at 3 p.m.), even if on different topics or with different analysts.
Individuals whose needs extend beyond what we can provide in a 30- or 50-minute guidance session should speak with their account manager about other options for how Forrester analysts and consultants can support their initiatives through our advisory and consulting offerings.
For further guidance, read our guidance sessions FAQ here.
Education Guidance Sessions 
Guidance sessions are engagements between a Forrester analyst and an eligible Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder where the analyst provides the license holder with advice, guidance, and/or recommendations based on existing research, their areas of expertise, and/or application of Forrester tools, templates, or data.
There may be times when the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member needs to educate others who are not license holders on their team or within their organization about a concept, tool, or best practice covered in their entitled Forrester research. In those rare instances, they may leverage education guidance sessions.
For example: An education guidance session could be with just one or two individuals who need to understand and buy in to a new framework, concept, or approach for which the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder is responsible, or it can be to inform a small cross-functional team who will be involved in helping ensure that an initiative that the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member is leading is successful.
Education guidance sessions are subject to the same policies and rules for guidance sessions, though with some specific differences.
Rules for guidance sessions that continue to apply to education guidance sessions:
Must be scheduled by the eligible license holder (i.e., Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member).
Topic is aligned to an entitled Forrester research coverage area(s).
Topic can be answered/addressed thoroughly in 50 minutes or less.
Involves only one analyst at a time unless otherwise determined by Forrester.
Analyst prep time is about 1 hour or less.
No client-specific deliverables are developed either before or after the session.
Exceptions for education guidance sessions:
Education guidance sessions must be scheduled by and attended by the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder, though they may mainly be for the benefit of other attendees. This is especially helpful for Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, and Team Leader license holders whose high-profile initiatives often require the understanding and buy-in of individuals on their team — or in other functions — to be completed successfully.
Education guidance sessions can include nonlicense holders from the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member’s organization, but note that no more than 20 individuals involved in or impacted by the initiative are permitted to attend. (A third party may attend an education guidance session only if they are licensed according to our contractor licensing policy.)
It is recommended, though not required, that a separate guidance session be scheduled for planning/preparation prior to the education guidance session. This planning discussion should take place with the Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder, since the key to an effective education guidance session is that the analyst understands ahead of time the purpose of the session, the desired content to be covered, and the way in which it connects with an initiative that the Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, or Team Leader is currently working on.
Attendance and participation:
The Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, or Team Member license holder must attend and introduce/set the context for an education guidance session.
Education guidance sessions can only be scheduled one at a time; the next one cannot be scheduled until an already scheduled one is completed.
Education guidance sessions may not be recorded or transcribed.
Education guidance sessions may be used:
To educate up to 20 members of the license holder’s organization on a concept, tool, or best practice covered in Forrester research and related to a priority for which the Executive, Leader, Team Executive, or Team Leader is responsible (and that is ideally captured as an initiative).
Education guidance sessions may not be used:
For audiences of greater than 20 people total.
(Example: conducting the same education guidance session twice, each with a set of 15 different people.)
Cover concepts outside of the license holder’s designated Forrester research coverage area(s).
(Example: A license holder with the Forrester Decisions for B2B Marketing Executives service may not discuss topics or research content that are exclusively included in the Forrester Decisions for Security & Risk service or ask for an overview of their specific markets, customers, and competitors, which are topics covered in Forrester Market Insights.)
We have other offerings, such as certification and consulting engagements, if you need to accommodate a broader set of topics or attendees.
Education guidance sessions are a benefit provided to clients who purchase Forrester Decisions Executive, Leader, Team Executive, Team Leader, and Team Member licenses that extend beyond the specific entitlements defined in a Purchase Agreement. The expectation is that clients will use education guidance sessions infrequently and only when they need to educate or align across their organization to make progress on their initiatives. Forrester provides this flexibility to help clients at our discretion and reserves the right to limit the number of education guidance sessions conducted by any one individual or account if we believe that the spirit and intent of education guidance sessions are not being respected.
