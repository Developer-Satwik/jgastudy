How to create an event for free and get discovered
Yes, you can create an event for free on Eventbrite. Our platform is always free for events with free tickets, with no ticketing platform fees. For paid events, our simple event pricing consists of a small fee per ticket, giving you the flexibility to either cover the cost or pass it on to your attendees.
