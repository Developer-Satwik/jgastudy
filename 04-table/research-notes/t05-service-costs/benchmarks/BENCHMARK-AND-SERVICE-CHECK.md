## Costing application

- Record qualification, discovery, proposal, negotiation and follow-up time for opportunities actually pursued, including unsuccessful ones. Count work once across the commercial period. Do not cost only successful orders, divide by an assumed win rate or treat an unknown sales quantity as zero.
- Define the bought edition, organisation/user/audience, permitted internal/client/public use, entitlement and responsible owner. Distinguish access fulfilment, a bounded briefing and a separately scoped new deliverable. A package containing a licence and briefing is one package; its included rights and work cannot be counted twice as an extra sale or extra purchased supplier service.
- Cost preparation, live delivery, necessary follow-up and scheduling/entitlement/asset administration in their actual units. A 30-minute customer session is not proof of only 30 minutes of total labour. A subscription’s metered hours are not necessarily all provider person-hours. Elapsed response windows are not labour hours.
- New analysis, additional customer-specific outputs or broader audiences need a separately defined scope and appropriate capability. Ordinary delivery corrections remain delivery/correction work. Reconcile overlap with existing T05R WR09–WR16 and JGA review/approval work before adding hours.
- Keep shared edition-level commercial assets separate from additional organisation-specific fulfilment. Preserve bundled rights once and price/cost separately. Internal time needs a resource basis; already committed payroll is not automatically additional cash. A supplier package and its already included labour must not both be charged into the cost model.

For each chosen route record task/output, owner/skill, hours or booked unit, currency, rate/fee basis, inclusions, tax/fees, payment timing, remaining JGA work and any shared-work credit. The existing 7.5-hour normalisation is an analyst assumption, not a source billing day. Do not mix salary with freelance rates, mean with median, or UK day rates with US hourly data. No geographic savings, automation saving, actual demand, renewal or conversion follows from these pages.
