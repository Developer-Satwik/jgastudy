# Future service and sales workload candidates

1 November 2026. Internal candidate catalogue for root review; no adopted budget or actual workload.

The proposed component contains **21 task rows in nine cost families, with 15 distinct quantity drivers**. It separates the cost of trying to sell from won-order setup, actual fulfilment, explicitly scoped briefings and partner obligations. The [structured candidates](workload-candidates.json) hold the exact units, exclusions and assumptions. All quantities, selected effort cases, rates, cash classifications, staffing and actual records remain blank or empty.

The existing complete commissioned report, current USD1,500 fee and ordinary delivery/correction duties are unchanged. Practitioners retain free full research. Nothing here creates a new mandatory service, commercial contact, event, hiring plan, customer target, price, renewal or recurring production commitment.

## Per-unit effort suggestions

These are **untimed analyst person-hour assumptions**, supplied to make a later transparent unit-cost catalogue possible. They are not timesheets, supplier quotes, delivery promises, an upper bound or empirical validation. Select one effort case consistently as a starting point, then override an individual row when actual scope, reusable assets or delivery arrangements justify it. Keep that override and its basis visible.

The low / working / high cases reflect less / moderate / greater complexity within the stated unit. They are not sales-volume scenarios or probability ranges. Hours describe the total person-time for the task; they must not be applied in full to every role mentioned. A two-person live session legitimately has more person-hours than a one-person session. It does not imply that two staff are required or available.

A useful first workbook can show each unit's hours and, where appropriate role rates are selected, its resource value. It can therefore be useful before demand is known. Programme hours/cost remain unavailable until the relevant quantities and rates are chosen. No quantity is inferred from edition count, a four-city concept, customer count, event capacity or an unmeasured conversion rate. Each actual unsold, unanswered or abandoned attempt can still incur its own cost. Preserve that work rather than charging only successful customers. Conversely, an unchanged resend or document save is not automatically another proposal/negotiation unit.

## Free research, partner bundles and repeated work

- **Free practitioner research:** distribute the full research. A common paid/bundled/free batch uses WS12 once. An extra free-only batch, recipient-specific intervention or genuine extra route can create incremental effort. No second production cost or paid employer licence is presumed because a practitioner received research.
- **JGA handover versus distribution:** WR14 already covers final report QA and portable handover to JGA. It does not automatically include every later practitioner/customer dispatch. WS21 is only the campaign's genuinely additional delivery-readiness operation; WS12 is the actual run. Reuse a tested configuration and record only its necessary update. No new website, platform or restriction is required.
- **One won order:** WS07 counts one contract/order once, including a combined partner bundle with a licence and briefing. Separate use assets and sessions are counted only when actually scoped/prepared/delivered. Do not add a bundled licence to unbundled licence revenue/units or pretend a bundled briefing has no delivery cost.
- **Partner benefits:** WS10 records accurately what is funded and promised. WS11 tracks only those explicitly agreed benefits. A partner buys no implied finding control, contact list, attendance disclosure, exclusivity, access meeting or commercial outcome.
- **Legitimate repeats:** a second distinct session, actual negotiation round, invoice cycle or distribution run can consume real time. Do not erase it simply because it uses the same asset or customer. Remove only the same work/person-time counted twice. Reused briefing preparation, a shared correction send or one note delivered to many recipients is common work once; distinct delivery/handling can remain.
- **Optional cycle note:** WS20 is the T02 short follow-through idea if later chosen. It uses existing permitted material, with evidence limits and identification review. It creates no new research, answer guarantee, monthly cadence or event obligation.

Do not manufacture a new paid tier by narrowing the original free benefit. Any additional employer circulation/use or briefing must be genuinely additional and wanted. A funding acknowledgement or permission record never creates rights to third-party material or identifiable participant answers.

## Role and later costing suggestions

Keep the relevant skill and task visible, with no named hire or staffed analyst bench. Research-presenter tasks can be compared with an appropriate research-role reference; fulfilment/scheduling work can be compared with appropriate coordination references, provided currency, date, delivery unit, booking minimum and scope match. **JGA commercial, relationship, decision-owner and finance time still need their own suitable valuation/cash basis.** Do not replace missing commercial rates with an employee salary or declare existing staff time free.

For a mixed task, allocate the actual performer/reviewer hours to roles so they sum to the task total. Do not price every listed role for the full hours. If a supplier package covers preparation, delivery and follow-up, reconcile that package to the rows once. Do not add the package fee and all the same bottom-up labour, or an overhead-loaded rate and identical overhead again.

Keep additional cash, already-budgeted JGA resources and full resource/opportunity value separate. Invoice, cash receipt and fulfilled benefit also remain different states. Supplier minimums, expenses, applicable tax/platform costs and payment/cancellation terms need their own selected scope and basis; no missing applicable cost becomes zero here.

Before later adopting an hours reduction, record the exact reusable asset/version, task and necessary remaining validation/handoff. An assisted tool is a process choice with required review, not a general percentage saving. Time a real permitted delivery or obtain a sufficiently scoped quote before treating these assumptions as validated capacity.

Only this workload folder is changed. No first workbook, canonical/control record, financial register, actual customer/participant record or external contact is changed.
