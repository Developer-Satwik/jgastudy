# Service-cost method application

1 November 2026.

The [Government Finance Function and HM Treasury guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/service-costing-in-government/service-costing-in-government), published 6 August 2026, supports explicit service boundaries, output units, workload drivers, documented estimates and proportionate assurance. Its cost-allocation approach helps distinguish shared report production from extra customer work. This is a method analogy; the government examples do not establish JGA hours, wages, overheads or demand.

For this component, prepare a per-unit labour catalogue first. Leave activity quantities and JGA-specific rates unresolved. Keep preparation, live delivery and follow-up within the complete unit where they occur; separately identify genuinely additional work and legitimate scope changes. Per-unit costs with different denominators must not be added as a programme budget.

The separate first research/staffing workbook remains unchanged. Ordinary within-scope corrections stay under the current agreement, and no public government charging rule is applied to JGA.
