## Two useful refinements for the parent

1. **Qualify the use and role, not only the organisation name.** A firm may be a source, content substitute, possible distribution partner, service buyer or funding prospect in different situations. Ask which specific task and output is at issue where material; do not pre-populate a sponsor list from this comparator table. The actual user, budget owner and external/internal reuse purpose may differ. T1C-C05.
2. **Separate direct revenue from distribution and client-service value.** A publication can support an existing service as well as a possible licence. Free visibility does not prove a weak model, but no attributed conversion or recruiting return can be entered merely because a report/podcast/event exists. Cost the intended product and define evidence of value separately; count bundled rights once. T1C-C06.

These are selective conditions to apply to the existing T02 hypotheses and later model, not extra report questions, new paid services, a pricing recommendation or original buyer evidence.

## Earlier study and constructed counterexample

The [three-question bridge](PRIOR-STUDY-BRIDGE.md) preserves the earlier study’s exact wording and answer lineage. It supports an assumption-boundary check, not buyer demand or a fact-check of generated advice. T1C-C07.

Constructed test for root: a hypothetical recruiter likes a draft report for internal client briefings and offers to distribute it, but has not requested an external reuse licence or committed money. Treat this as a possible distribution/use hypothesis with unknown rights and value, not two sales, a paid sponsor or guaranteed lead flow. If a separate employer wishes only to use public salary data for one UK growth hire, do not infer demand for a global payroll council. This is an unexecuted analyst challenge design, not collected fieldwork.
