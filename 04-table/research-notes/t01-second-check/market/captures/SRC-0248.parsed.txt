0016: Payment Policy
0017: Payment is due in full at time of registration, or within twenty-one (21) days of invoice. Payment must be received prior to the event.
0018: Payment includes lunches, refreshments, one evening meal and programme materials.
0019: Registration will be confirmed on receipt of payment and may be subject to cancellation. A receipt will be issued upon payment.
0020: Due to limited event space we advise early registration and payment by credit card to avoid disappointment. Your registration will not be confirmed until payment is received.
0021: Admission to the conference will be refused if payment has not been received.
0022: Unless stated otherwise on the booking form, all payments must be made in GBP (£).
0023: iVentiv Substitution Policy
0024: Delegates may be substituted up to fourteen (14) days prior to the event on receipt of change of details submitted in writing to iVentiv. Refunds are not issued in the case of cancellations by the delegate.
0025: Substitutions between fourteen (14) days and the date of the event will be allowed subject to an administration fee of 10% of the registration fee already paid.
0026: iVentiv Cancellation Policy
0027: Where a cancellation is received four (4) weeks prior to the event a 100% credit note will be issued to be used at another iVentiv event within eighteen (18) months of issuance.
0028: For cancellations received four (4) weeks or less prior to an event (including week four (4)), no credit can be issued.
0029: Cancellations must be received in writing four (4) weeks prior to the event in order to obtain a full credit note for a future iVentiv event. Thereafter, the event fee is payable in full and is non-refundable.
0030: Payment terms are fourteen (14) days and must be received prior to the event. Non-payment or non-attendance does not constitute a cancellation.
0031: In the unlikely event that iVentiv cancels or otherwise postpones an event iVentiv is not responsible for travel, accommodation or other costs incurred by clients.
0032: Intellectual property rights of all materials produced by, and or distributed by, or on behalf of iVentiv is expressly reserved and any unauthorised duplication, publication or distribution is prohibited, all materials Copyright iVentiv Ltd.
0033: In the event that iVentiv postpones an event, delegate payments at the postponement date will be credited towards the rescheduled event date. In the event that the delegate is unable to attend the rescheduled event, the delegate will receive a 100% credit note representative of the payments made, to be used at a future iVentiv event. The credit note will be valid for eighteen (18) months from the date of issuance.
0034: iVentiv is not responsible for any loss or damage as a result of substitution, alteration or cancellation/postponement of an event. iVentiv shall assume no liability whatsoever should an event be cancelled, rescheduled or postponed due to a fortuitous event, Act of God, unforeseen occurrence or any other event that renders performance of this event impracticable or impossible. For purposes of this clause, a fortuitous event shall include, but not be limited to: fire, war, labour strike, weather conditions or other emergency.

As such, iVentiv reserves the right to alter or modify the advertised speakers and/or topics.
