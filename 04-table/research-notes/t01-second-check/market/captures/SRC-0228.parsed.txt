0113: Buyers Guide Services - Software Providers
0114: Assessing and Evaluating Software Providers and Products
0115: Enterprises are challenged with finding authoritative information about software providers they can rely on to help shape decisions on existing and future software products. Access to an in-depth analysis based on reliable and objective research is essential to an organization’s success. Software providers that embrace and use authoritative research will benefit from its credibility and recognition that could negatively impact the total cost of ownership and return on their investment.
0116: About the Methodology
0117: The Buyers Guide is independent research-based insights that evaluates software providers and their products. The guide is designed to help influence the market and support decisions with an RFI/RFP approach. The carefully crafted, best practice-based methodology called the Value Index draws on our independent research to provide software provider and product ratings and rankings. The Buyers Guide is a tool that assists enterprises in assessing software providers and helps them avoid making an unwise decision that could negatively impact the total cost of ownership and return on their investment.
0118: Using the Buyers Guides
0119: Current and future market demands require authoritative research to achieve and maintain a strong market position. The Buyers Guide service offers marketing and sales team members the opportunity to understand better the market landscape and how best to promote their respective Buyers Guide ranking and ratings to retain or acquire new customers. The Buyer Guide provides the following value:
0120: Provides evaluation of your company and product offerings compared to competitors.
0121: Educates buyers on the strengths of your offerings in the context of their needs.
0122: Demonstrates your commitment to using research to validate your offering.
0123: Available in multiple packages that can include the following:
0124: A market consultation to gain research-based insights on the products from a software provider and technology perspective.
0125: Licensed use of the reports and graphics to engage prospects in the marketing funnel or sales process by using authoritative research to address their needs and options.
0126: Buyers Guide Report Types
0127: Provider Report
0128: Includes the full Buyers Guide report and a detailed performance results page for a single software provider.
0129: Provider Snapshot
0130: Includes the performance summary, key strengths and a quote from the Research Director for a single software provider.
0131: Buyers Guide Insight
0132: Includes targeted performance detail, key points of differentiation and a quote from the Research Director for a single software provider.
