0016: FAQs
0017: Question: How much does it cost to attend an iVentiv Exchange?
0018: Answer:
0019: iVentiv events are free for VP/Director level executives and other senior corporate delegates from companies with 10,000 or more employees. All registrations are subject to iVentiv approval.
0020: Question: Why have I not heard of iVentiv before?
0021: Answer:
0022: iVentiv is a specialist company that creates bespoke events for C-suite executives. Over the last 15 years and 250+ events, the iVentiv community has grown predominantly through word-of-mouth recommendations and direct contact with eligible participants. To see why your peers value the iVentiv experience so highly, take a look at their feedback.
0023: Question: Who attends iVentiv's events?
0024: Answer:
0025: iVentiv events are designed for VP/Director level executives and other senior leaders from companies with more than 10,000 employees based in corporate headquarters and having global/regional responsibility for their function. If you’re not sure you fit this category but would still like to attend please contact iVentiv directly on 0044 (0) 1926 800236. Alternatively, for information about attending as an event partner please enquire about sponsorship.
0026: Question: Will the events be in the same location every year?
0027: Answer:
0028: No, iVentiv has a core group of topics that are covered on an annual basis however the venue location changes from year to year.
0029: Question: Do you cover my travel costs to join the event?
0030: Answer:
0031: Registration for iVentiv events is free (for VP/Directors and other senior corporate leaders) however, we do not cover travel or accommodation costs. We negotiate a special rate with all our event venues which is available to all of you attending our events. You will receive information on how to book when you register.
0032: Question: Tell me more about the 1-2-1 meetings?
0033: Answer:
0034: Every iVentiv event is free to attend thanks to the support of our trusted event partners. Those partners and their representatives are carefully selected to bring real value and relevant expertise to the conversation.
0035: When you attend, you may have 1-2-1 networking sessions scheduled with our partners, giving you both the opportunity to make connections and understand more about one another’s work in a friendly and informal setting. You will have the chance to indicate the partners you would most like to meet.
0036: The overwhelming majority of attendees at iVentiv events find the sessions useful, and many go on to forge long and profitable relationships from connections made at an iVentiv event. You will attend a maximum of two or three 1-2-1 sessions in a day. If you do have any questions at all, please contact a member of the iVentiv team.
0037: Question: I have specific topics I would like to see covered at the event, is it possible to contribute?
0038: Answer:
0039: Yes, we send all participants a series of pre-event questions and use the responses to help define the topics to be discussed. There are also opportunities throughout the event to put forward additional topics, or to pick out emergent topics which you would like to discuss.
0040: Question: How many people attend an event?
0041: Answer:
0042: iVentiv runs bespoke events which are attended by a maximum of 40 executive-level participants. Free places are available on a first-come, first-served basis.
0043: Question: I have been invited to an iVentiv event but cannot attend. Can I extend the invitation to a colleague or peer?
0044: Answer:
0045: As long as your colleague/peer is a VP/Director level executive or equivalent we would be more than happy for them to join.
0046: Question: Why should I join an iVentiv session?
0047: Answer:
0048: As a senior-level participants, you will find an iVentiv session to be the best event in this space. We share feedback from all of our past events, so please have a browse and review the feedback from senior leaders just like you. With an all-time average event score in excess of 92% we are not aware of another business that offers the same quality, interactive experience.
