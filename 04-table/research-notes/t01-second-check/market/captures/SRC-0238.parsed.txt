0089: FAQ
0090: What is Payroll Membership?
0091: Payroll Membership provides your team with ongoing access to expert payroll support, training, and resources to help you manage payroll accurately, confidently, and in line with current legislation.
0092: Who is Payroll Membership designed for?
0093: Payroll Membership is designed for payroll professionals, finance teams, HR teams, and businesses responsible for running payroll in-house, regardless of size or sector.
0094: How many people in my team can use the membership?
0095: Membership supports your entire payroll team, allowing multiple users to access support, training, and resources under one membership.
0096: What type of support is included?
0097: Members receive access to expert payroll guidance via the helpdesk, along with practical resources, updates, and training to support day-to-day payroll queries and more complex scenarios.
0098: Is the training live or on demand?
0099: Training includes both live sessions and on-demand video content, so your team can learn in real time or catch up at a time that suits their workload.
0100: Do I need a certain level of payroll experience to join?
0101: No. Payroll Membership supports all experience levels, from those new to payroll through to experienced professionals looking to stay compliant and up to date.
0102: How do I access the resources and training?
0103: All resources, guidance, and training are accessed online through the member portal, making it easy for your team to find information whenever they need it.
0104: Does Payroll Membership help with payroll compliance?
0105: Yes. Membership helps you stay informed of payroll legislation changes, best practice, and guidance, supporting compliance throughout the year.
0106: How long does the membership last?
0107: Payroll Membership runs on an annual basis, giving you continuous support and access throughout the year.
0108: How do I get started?
0109: Simply choose the membership option that best suits your payroll team and complete the sign-up process. Access is provided shortly after joining.
