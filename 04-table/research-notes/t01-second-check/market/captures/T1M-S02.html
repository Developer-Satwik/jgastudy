Supporting Payroll

Professionals

Globally

The GPA is a one-stop-shop for global and in-country payroll professionals. Our expansive range of resources offers everything from interactive training courses to comprehensive directories and in-depth country resources. Join us to connect with world-leading payroll experts and network with other like-minded professionals.

Being part of the GPA opens doors for our worldwide membership to access online and physical Payroll events across the globe, webinars and podcasts with guest specialists in their field and a series of training options on in-country payroll legislation. The GPA run summits and symposiums around the world and a dedicated awards event - the Global Payroll Awards - to celebrate the payroll industry.

We are passionate and dedicated champions of all things payroll and, most importantly, we are champions of the people in payroll.

GPA Memberships

As this ever-evolving sector continues to increase in complexity, the demand for informed global payroll and HR insights also goes on growing exponentially.

Membership of the GPA grants immediate access to a contemporary toolkit of specialist information, opportunities for personal and career development, networking events and global payroll resources. All refined by our comprehensive knowledge of the industry.

GPA members span the globe and hold a spectrum of industry positions at every professional and financial stage. Our memberships are as varied as the industry itself, tailormade to ensure you find the perfect fit for your aspirations, budget and time.

The GPA has everything you need to achieve excellence and to progress in your career. Membership provides the valuable foundations you need to drive a compliant operation and chart an assured course to your successful payroll industry future.
