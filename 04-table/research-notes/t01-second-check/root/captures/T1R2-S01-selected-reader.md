The Rule permits use of information while protecting speaker and participant identity/affiliation. It is a pre-agreed meeting guide. The current FAQ says no photography/video in the room, recording, or AI note-taking/transcription. Outside-room images need explicit permission and cannot indirectly identify other participants. Speakers may disclose their own attendance and comments afterwards. Parts may be designated under the Rule, but hosts/speakers cannot alternate remark-by-remark.

Table implication is an analyst application: choose meeting/output rules upfront. Council recognition, attendance disclosure, research participation and attributable publication are different permissions. Specific research-output approval does not silently waive the chosen meeting mode. This is not legal advice or evidence that JGA has adopted the Rule. Existing recording choices apply only within the applicable approved format.
