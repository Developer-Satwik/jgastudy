# T01 second challenge: historical development and member lifecycle

30 October 2026. Focused challenge of existing CPO, payroll-support membership and dated sponsored-research comparisons. This adds no seventh model, actual contributor, market forecast or automatic subscription policy.

## What materially changes the interpretation

### 1. A community is a sequence of separate outputs, not one recurring audience

CPO's origin page links its creation to PayrollOrg's 2024/2025 Executive Summits; the January 2026 forum article reports an October 2025 launch. The newly retained magazine archive lists issues through October 2026. These are distinct publisher-described formats and dated outputs. They do not show that the same executives moved between them or continued participating. T1H-C01–C02, canonical SRC-0240/SRC-0241 and new T1H-S01.

For JGA, an annual four-city programme should not be described as a longitudinal panel. An event cohort, ongoing council relationship and repeated publication have different units. The design needs a useful next output and a responsible delivery process, rather than treating another event date as evidence that people return. This is a product-design implication, not a causal finding.

### 2. JGA already has a specific public role in the adjacent ecosystem

The9 October 2026 retrospective describes the May 2026 PayrollOrg Executive Summit, its Strada sponsorship and Nick Day's moderation role. This is stronger evidence of existing public involvement than assuming JGA is an unknown entrant. It is not proof of a present formal agreement, participant access, partner willingness or endorsement of The Table. A direct event moderator role should not be inflated into a distribution partnership. T1H-C03 / T1H-S02.

A broader PayrollOrg Accomplishments page appeared in primary search results with a JGA collaboration statement. Its live raw request and web reader returned only an application shell, and local browser use was disabled. That route is retained solely as a screened lead; the collaboration claim is not adopted. No contact occurred to resolve it.

The strategic question is therefore complementarity: what specific decision evidence and member benefit would The Table add alongside established payroll content and forums? It should not claim the whole senior-payroll conversation is absent. No recommendation to approach PayrollOrg or Strada follows from this research.

### 3. Renewal mechanics and continuing value are different

The Payroll Centre's existing public terms specify renewal, reminder and cancellation mechanics. This establishes an offered contract lifecycle, not achieved retention or service effectiveness. Its access restrictions also distinguish a person, their organisation and a valid service period. Current signed customer arrangements and JGA terms remain separate. The direct renewal mechanism is covered by market candidate T1M-C07; history candidate T1H-C05 covers the distinct access lifecycle / SRC-0239.

For The Table, a person changing role may still be a useful professional contact while the fit of their new role, employer disclosure and reuse rights need separate consideration. Do not infer that all prior permissions expire or automatically transfer. Any later continuing-member plan should describe eligibility, available benefits, service period and replacement/withdrawal handling in proportion to the chosen product. This is a selective planning condition, not a new mandatory report question or a proposal to impose auto-renewal.

### 4. Dated pages are not a product-evolution study

HR.com's retained help articles date to 2021, 2023 and2024. They establish separate custom-output, reader-access and recruitment statements. They do not establish how its offer evolved or one current package. The observed 2025 sponsored payroll report remains a separate output already reviewed in T01. No archived-price trend, current fee or renewal assumption is reconstructed from those dates. T1H-C06.

## Condition to carry forward

Distinguish programme, event cohort, ongoing member relationship and research-use version/period. For each relevant exchange, separate planned output, contracted entitlement, actual payment, useful participation and permitted contribution. This is optional analyst application T1H-C07; it does not change the fourteen report questions, activate collection or demonstrate commercial viability.

The existing six mechanisms remain useful. The challenge sharpens lifecycle and ecosystem fit rather than replacing the model selection. Sponsor receipts, membership renewal, same-person engagement, incremental event value and actual Table rights still require their own evidence.
