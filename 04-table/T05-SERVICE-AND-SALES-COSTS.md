# Customer delivery and sales: the next cost component

1 November 2026. Future service and sales labour for The Table's proposed research offers. This adds a per-unit catalogue to the preserved research/staffing component. It does not select a price, forecast sales or change the current agreement.

The practical finding is that distributing a finished report, helping a buyer use it and winning the order are different activities. A partner package may contain all three. Its price needs to cover the actual work without counting a bundled right, joint session or existing report task twice.

## What is now modelled

The [editable workbook](../../../outputs/jga-t05-service-costs-2026-11-01/Service%20and%20sales%20costs.xlsx) has a unit-cost view, a visible task calculation and one input sheet. Twenty-one tasks use fifteen distinct activity drivers. Each has lower, working and higher effort assumptions. The working case is saved; the alternatives change the same calculation.

| Activity | What the cost unit covers | What it does not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign | Additional commercial-pack adaptation, common administration and fulfilment-route setup. | A new research edition, automatic monthly campaign or repeat charge for the existing preview/use-options work. |
| Contact attempt | One individually handled approach or short follow-up, including unsuccessful contact. | A lead quota, permission to contact or a customer gained. |
| Sales discussion | Relevant preparation, substantive conversation and immediate record. | An order, a Table research contribution or a completed meeting after a no-show. |
| Proposal and extra negotiation | One initial scoped proposal; a genuinely additional material round has its own driver. | Counting the same writing again merely because the document was resent, or discarding effort after a loss. |
| Won-order setup | Agreement, use and entitlement setup for one actual order. | Counting a combined partner/licence order as several contracts by default. |
| Reading path and use asset | Additional selection or preparation for the customer's defined use of existing evidence. | Fresh research, invented missing evidence or unrestricted publication rights. |
| Partner administration | Recognition setup and administration of explicit benefits. | The complete cost of a partner package. Actual order, asset, briefing and distribution work remains distinct. |
| Distribution run | One incremental release/delivery run to its actual audience, including free practitioner recipients. | A paid licence per recipient or repeated charging for the original portable handover. |
| Briefing session | Preparation/scheduling, live delivery and bounded follow-through. | A new study, unlimited advice or a separate session for every attending entitlement. |
| Support case | Incremental triage, handling, routing and close. | An extra fee for ordinary within-scope correction or repeating the report's substantive correction work here. |
| Invoice, renewal and cycle note | Actual invoice/receipt administration, initiated renewal/expiry handling and an optional scoped follow-through note. | Received cash merely because invoiced, automatic renewal or a monthly research promise. |

## How the monetary comparison works

Seven specialist tasks use the existing broad research-role reference of £372 per day, normalised using the explicit 7.5-hour assumption. Seven coordination tasks use the existing £46.19 hourly editorial project-management minimum as an adjacent-work proxy. Neither establishes actual payroll research, sales, finance, design or rights-review capability. The role mapping is an analyst choice, not a supplier scope or employee wage.

Seven commercial-owner tasks have an unresolved hourly resource value. JGA has not supplied an applicable rate, and a generic researcher or editorial rate should not silently replace it. Existing salary can be a resource allocation without an additional cash payment. An externally bought service would need actual terms and retained JGA effort.

For example, the working briefing allowance is **five person-hours**: three for preparation/scheduling, one for delivery and one for follow-through. At the reused research comparison rate, this is **£248 of labour**. It is an illustrative planning allowance, not a selling price, observed effort, staffed service or promised duration. The higher effort case is not an upper limit.

The workbook separates specialist/administrative reference value, commercial value and combined value. It preserves full precision in calculations and formats monetary results to two decimals. None includes tax, platform/payment charges, other supplier expenses or a profit margin. Normalised hours do not establish bookable fractions of a supplier day.

## Keep the programme quantities unresolved until there is a basis

All fifteen programme quantities start blank. A user can enter a defined period's actual or explicitly assumed activity counts, including zero where no incremental activity is selected or the work is already covered elsewhere. An applicable unknown remains blank. The programme total remains unavailable until its necessary quantities and rates are supplied.

A proposal count, an order count, a distribution run and a briefing session are not interchangeable customer counts. Per-unit values therefore have no useful grand total without corresponding quantities. The workbook does not derive contacts or sales from a guessed conversion rate, calculate acquisition cost per customer or turn service entitlement into automatic demand.

Preparation for unsuccessful opportunities still consumes resources. [Northern Ireland's official tender guidance](https://www.nibusinessinfo.co.uk/content/should-you-bid-tender) supports distinguishing bid preparation from fulfilment and recognising unsuccessful effort. Its tender context does not quantify JGA's direct-sales process.

## Bundled benefits and shared work

A licence included in a partner package is not another unbundled sale. Its real fulfilment can still require work. Briefing quantities include actual sessions delivered within partner packages as well as separately sold sessions. One shared session for two partners is one delivery session; two separate sessions are two. Preparation that is genuinely reused receives a specific credit rather than being charged in full again.

A customer reading path reused in a briefing is the same asset to the extent the work is identical. Keep only the necessary additional preparation. Similarly, recognition setup does not repeat the original report's graphic design or create a right to disclose participant identities. A free full-research distribution run can serve contributors and paying recipients together where appropriate; count the actual incremental run once and preserve the free benefit.

Current T03 exploratory contributions are not assumed sales meetings, customers or future dinners. If a later authorised activity genuinely serves more than one purpose, identify and allocate the actual shared work once, retaining the existing contribution-count and permission rules.

## What the public service comparisons contribute

[ABI Research's inquiry FAQ](https://www.abiresearch.com/analyst-inquiry-faq/) separates report access, metered inquiry, request administration and additional custom work. It also distinguishes provider-error correction. [Forrester's access policy](https://www.forrester.com/policies/analyst-access/) distinguishes session time, preparation and custom-work limits; its [citation policy](https://www.forrester.com/policies/citations-policy/) illustrates the extra work around defined external uses and replay/reprint material. These are provider examples, not JGA contract terms, available staff, typical delivery hours or a licence tariff.

The [Government Finance Function and HM Treasury guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/service-costing-in-government/service-costing-in-government) supports explicit service boundaries, meaningful activity drivers, documented estimates and proportionate review. Its public-service examples and charging arrangements are not applied as JGA requirements or rates.

All five new primary routes retain original HTML and parsed text.
