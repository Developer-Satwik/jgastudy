# Service and sales costs: second-check findings

1 November 2026. Review before event costing. The original twenty-one-task catalogue is useful for its defined units, but the complete programme model also needs to account for work incurred before completion and benefits still owed later. Those requirements are now explicit.

No arithmetic error requiring a change to the first workbook was identified. Both workbooks and all seventy financial inputs remain preserved. This second check adds scoped evidence, concrete applications and six conditions for programme integration. It does not establish actual customers, production hours, rates, staffing or willingness to pay.

## The most important finding: incurred work is not a completed unit

The catalogue's working briefing unit contains preparation, live delivery and follow-through. If a briefing is cancelled after preparation, a count of zero delivered sessions produces zero briefing-unit cost in the current formula. That is correct for the selected completed units, but incomplete as a description of work already incurred.

The full programme model must retain that preparation separately, or allocate it through a supported average where a valid completed-unit denominator exists. It cannot allocate a cancellation across completed sessions when none were delivered. A no-show, an abandoned proposal, partial fulfilment or a deferred service can create the same distinction. A failure to win the order does not remove the cost of preparing its proposal.

This is a requirement for the unfinished programme model, rather than a reason to rewrite the preserved per-unit comparison or create a cancellation charge. Ordinary current contract delivery and correction obligations remain covered by the existing agreement.

## Joint delivery is an option to qualify

A common report does not guarantee that customers can share a session. Questions, audience permission, confidentiality, competing commercial interests and necessary expertise may require separate delivery. Forrester's own [access policy](https://www.forrester.com/policies/analyst-access/) is one example of account and attendee boundaries. Its restrictions are not imposed on JGA.

Count actual people-time as well as elapsed session time. A one-hour discussion with two necessary presenters consumes two live person-hours. Conversely, a prepared asset reused without material change should not be produced again for every nominal package. Neither saving nor extra staffing should be assumed before the actual scope is defined.

## Historical and lifecycle perspective

Forrester's retrospective [timeline](https://www.forrester.com/about-us/forrester-timeline/) describes its research/guidance portfolio in 2021, continuous guidance in 2024 and self-service access in 2025. This shows changing product boundaries, rather than a stable entitlement or a measured JGA efficiency gain.

Its [2025 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1023313/000119312526105114/forr-20251231.htm) separates fulfilment, selling and administration, and says strategy-consulting sales would stop while backlog work would be fulfilled during 2026. This illustrates why ending new sales does not itself end work owed. Corporate accounting policies, cost ratios and retention figures are not transferred to JGA.

For our model, use the actual edition, service term and remaining benefit. A later briefing does not refresh old research. An invoice or order does not prove received cash or completed delivery. A short campaign-period cost cannot stand for an entire year of promised support. Renewal remains an untested optional future action, not assumed revenue.

## Six refinements to carry into the full model

The [implementation note](T05S-PROGRAMME-COST-REFINEMENTS.md) and [structured map](T05S-second-challenge-map.json) specify:

1. Completed units and work already incurred, including zero-completion periods.
2. Suitable audiences, actual performer time and necessary delivery capacity.
3. Exact reusable assets, shared batches and recipient-specific handling.
4. Task, country, edition, evidence fit and the buyer's existing alternatives.
5. Benefit periods and obligations still owed after the current period or end of new sales.
6. Capable delivery routes, applicable resource rates and separate cash/non-labour treatment.

These are selective cost-planning conditions, not extra mandatory participant questions, country quotas, new service promises or price changes. The commercial rate and all programme quantities remain unresolved. Existing salary allocation is not automatically additional cash, while free or bundled fulfilment can still consume resources.
