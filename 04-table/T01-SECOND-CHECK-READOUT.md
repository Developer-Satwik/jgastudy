# The Table: second challenge

30 October 2026. Historical, segmented-market, specific-competitor, prior-study and participation/output checks. First-pass six models and four hypotheses remain preserved. This is desktop design refinement, not original buyer validation.

## What the second look found

**The proposed research pack remains worth examining, but its paid value must be more specific.** Existing recruiter reports, interpretation, expert interviews and payroll events already address parts of the audience need. The distinctive question is which actual decision the Table evidence helps and what usable evidence or service is missing. No gap or demand has been proven.

**Free research for practitioners changes the licence logic.** That is already in Nick’s concept. A paid employer offer needs a genuinely different agreed service or use, rather than a forecast second sale of equivalent access. Supplier promotion, adviser client work or a decision briefing may be different exchanges, but only where wanted and deliverable. Preserve the free benefit and count the real bundle once. CLM-0522, CLM-0506, CLM-0515.

**JGA has relevant public involvement, which is different from a new partnership.** The retained PayrollOrg retrospective names Nick as a May 2026 summit moderator. Its broader online formats provide adjacent routes, rather than an empty market. A wider collaboration statement surfaced in search, but its original page was only an application shell, so it remains an unverified lead. No audience access, permission, endorsement or Table partnership follows. CLM-0500.

**Continuity has several meanings.** CPO’s origins, forum launch, publication archive and later summit are distinct dated outputs. They do not establish the same returning participants. TPC renewal provisions are contract mechanics, not a verified retention rate. JGA’s four-city capacity remains a proposal for changing cohorts, not a longitudinal panel. CLM-0498, CLM-0499, CLM-0501, CLM-0503, CLM-0510.

**Geography and attendance need to fit the decision.** IPASS Irish support and GPA global resources sharpen the substitute comparison, without adding two selected models. A Dublin venue is not a definition of Irish or global evidence needs. An eligible person’s interest and employer permission to accept hospitality are different; a permitted remote/written contribution may still be useful. The public employer-policy example is deliberately scoped to the Planning Inspectorate. CLM-0504, CLM-0505, CLM-0507, CLM-0520, CLM-0521.

**Meeting rules affect the product we can publish.** The current Chatham House originator FAQ excludes recording, AI note-taking and in-room imagery for meetings under the Rule. This applies only if that mode is adopted. Council recognition, attendance disclosure, research participation and later attributed material remain separate. No Rule, tool or permission has been adopted by the review. CLM-0518, CLM-0519.

## Six selective conditions

| Condition | What to establish where relevant | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TC01: Actual use and free/paid entitlement | Who uses the output for which actual decision, and what additional service or use right is being purchased? | Distinguish employer reading, supplier marketing, adviser client work and the research already promised free to contributors. Define incremental value and count the same entitlement once. |
| TC02: Specific relationship and role | Is this organisation a source, substitute, public collaborator, distributor, service buyer or proposed funder in this particular exchange? | Use Nick’s documented moderator role and existing recruiter content/events as context. Verify a proposed current relationship or right separately; do not pre-fill a sponsor list. |
| TC03: Programme, cohort and entitlement lifecycle | Which dated output, cohort, member role and access period are actually observed or agreed? | Separate a series of publications, an announced event, actual participation, contractual renewal, payment and useful continued engagement. Handle changed role/employer/period proportionately. |
| TC04: Decision geography, alternatives and permitted participation | What country/operating scope and decision need does this user have, and can the relevant person participate in this format? | Compare actual UK/Irish/global support and public/internal alternatives. Event city alone does not determine research need. Separate eligible practitioner, permitted hospitality and useful research contribution. |
| TC05: Meeting mode, recognition and publication | What meeting rule applies, and what specific recognition, research use or publication has been permitted? | If adopting the named Chatham House Rule, follow its current definition/FAQ. Do not treat optional recording permission or council recognition as permission to record a Rule meeting, identify attendance or attribute others’ comments. |
| TC06: Delivery-ready value, funds and attribution | Are the required evidence, people, rights, service capacity and cash available for the benefit actually promised? | Separate research and commercial-access benefits where combined. Preserve research/member fit beside funder targeting. Model direct licence revenue, servicing/distribution value and event increment separately without invented conversion or cash timing. |

These are optional analytical checks, not an additional questionnaire, ranking or score. They do not expand the fourteen report questions.

## What changes before the next step

Refine each T02 proposition by intended user, actual use, permitted output, alternative and delivery burden. Preserve the original four hypotheses. For research-use, resolve free/paid entitlement overlap. For a partner, distinguish funding from separately defined access rights. For practitioners, define relevant peer/aftercare value and permitted participation. For the event, compare incremental value and obligations against remote/written alternatives.

Carry defined units and prerequisites into later Model A/Model B work: useful delivered benefit, contractual entitlement, actual paid receipt and continuing participation remain different states. The commissioned USD1,500 project is not future recurring cost evidence. Neither public offerings nor positive interest supply JGA prices, revenue, retention or conversion rates.

T03 actual contributions, approved collection arrangements and T04/T05 product/cost/cash work remain open. Nothing was sent, booked, purchased or published. First-pass reviews and all client/core files are preserved.
