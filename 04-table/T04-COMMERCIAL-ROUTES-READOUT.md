# The Table: commercial routes and cost-model design

31 October 2026. Internal working readout. This completes the route-design and initial UK public venue-cost screen; the T05 financial workbook and commercial recommendation come next.

The model should answer two questions: can the research support a useful paid product on its own, and what additional value and cost would an event create? Compare both on the same research and time basis. A dinner is not assumed to cause research sales.

Four gatherings do not automatically mean four reports, four customers or a continuing panel. Dates, edition cadence, pilot city, actual staffing and Texas city remain to be defined. These are cases to cost, not booked programmes.

## What could be paid for

The [reviewed T02 propositions](T02-PROPOSITION-PACK.md) become separate model lines:

1. **Research-use licence:** a defined edition and permitted use, bought by an organisation that needs it. The same research asset can support multiple legitimate licence orders.
2. **Founding research partnership:** funding for a specific edition or purpose, with an explicitly defined bundle. Count its fee once; an included licence or briefing is not another sale.
3. **Additional briefing or use preparation:** genuinely additional scoped work, where wanted and feasible. Existing deliverables, corrections and included benefits do not become new charges.
4. **Optional event-related support:** only if separately specified and appropriate. It is not necessary for the research-only case and creates no default right to contacts, attendance identities or private answers.

Practitioners still receive the full research free under the concept. Do not forecast an equivalent second sale to their employer or narrow that benefit to create revenue. These are possible commercial units, with no selected price, validated demand, signed customer or available service team assumed.

## Three financial views

| View | Decision it helps answer | What must stay visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Additional operating cash costs and contribution | What extra work and cash does this option require? | Actual selected scope; costs already incurred or excluded from this view. |
| Full repeatable resource cost | Could JGA run the offer sustainably? | Research, sales, servicing, recruitment, JGA time and event operations, including time that is not extra payroll cash. |
| Initial investment and recovery | How does the first project investment relate to later income? | Current USD1,500 commission, split USD1,000 report and USD500 Table; paid and remaining obligations separately. |

The reported USD750 upfront is a JGA/Riseklix project payment, not Table sponsorship income. The remaining USD750 follows the agreement's completion trigger. The current fee is neither a recurring edition quote nor a cost to repeat per buyer or city. Any allocation must be explicit and counted once.

Operating contribution is also different from bank cash. A programme can look positive over its full period yet need funding before customers pay. The workbook will therefore retain dated receipts, deposits, balances, refunds and cancellation exposure alongside the cost result.

## What the initial venue screen changes

The [public-cost screen](T04-PUBLIC-COST-SCREEN.md) retains five official source routes across three venue examples. It is a mechanics and feasibility screen, not a recommendation or all-in budget.

- **Capacity needs the whole party.** Hawksmoor Manchester's private room seats 10–14. It cannot hold the upper target of 15 practitioners, even before additional hosts. Larger semi-private spaces are a different privacy proposition. [Official room page](https://thehawksmoor.com/locations/manchester/private-dining/)
- **Use the document actually linked by the live venue page.** Benares' current linked guide differs materially from the older January guide found in search. The current menu is valid 1 September–15 November 2026, so its rates are not confirmed for a 2027 pilot. Its tasting menu has a 16-person maximum; a larger room does not remove that menu limit. [Official dining page](https://benaresrestaurant.com/private-dining-events/)
- **Menu price is not the event bill.** Benares' published menu, cover/service wording, minimum spend and room-charge terms need separate treatment. Corrigan's states no room-hire fee but a food/drink minimum, with the amount to be obtained. A missing minimum is not zero. [Official Lindsay Room page](https://www.corrigansmayfair.co.uk/private-dining/private-dining-rooms/lindsay-room)

Distinct people determine seats: one person who both hosts and moderates occupies one place. Twelve to fifteen practitioners plus additional people is the rule; a fixed two-host assumption is not an agreed staffing plan. No venue date, quote, employer hospitality approval, privacy/acoustic suitability or complete cost is established here.

## How the financial build will work

Use the [input and calculation rules](T04-INPUT-AND-MODEL-RULES.md) and [structured route map](T04-model-design.json) as the specification.

1. Map the eight T02 work objects to actual work, hours, cash payments and resource allocations. Separate the commissioned work from future repeat production and genuinely additional service.
2. Assemble source-labelled venue, travel, recruitment, moderation, production and operating inputs. Preserve original currencies, tax/service inclusions, applicability dates and unresolved terms. Extend international costs only after the geography is defined.
3. Introduce clearly labelled price, customer, partner and workload scenarios. Keep common A/B assumptions matched; model any event-related sales uplift separately. Unknown applicable inputs remain visible rather than becoming zero.
4. Calculate operating contribution, full-resource result, investment recovery, break-even units and dated funding needs. Reconcile common work once and each city's increment separately.
5. Stress-test zero sales, loss of a partner, lower prices, higher work/cost, delayed receipts and cancellation. Check formulas and charts against the [prepared acceptance cases](../08-qa/T04-to-T05-acceptance-cases.json).

The workbook can show what would need to be true for each route to work. Actual buyer/member evidence and operating readiness are still needed before recommending commitment or expansion. Mathematical break-even alone does not establish demand or delivery capacity.
