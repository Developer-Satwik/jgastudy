# Research and staffing costs: second-check findings

1 November 2026. Public sources and the earlier-study material were collected or checked on 31 October, with selected primary pages rechecked on 1 November. This review challenges the first research/staffing component before the next T05 cost step.

The original calculation is sound for its stated UK-contractor comparison. The review supports keeping its rates and effort assumptions as a named scenario. It does not support treating that scenario as the expected delivery bill, applying a geographic discount or removing work by an assumed AI saving.

Contractor rate, client platform charge, currency, tax/expense inclusion and payment terms are separate. The reviewed Upwork fee description is conditional; it has not been added to every route or treated as the user's plan. ## Recruitment, capacity and rework

Usable contributors are only one work driver. Extra unsuccessful contacts, cancellations, clarification and repeat review can consume time without increasing the six-to-ten report count. The original rows already include allowances; only additional work beyond those allowances should be added, once.

Camden's qualitative planning guidance provides a scoped analogy: allow time for recruitment interruptions, preparation, assurance, analysis and facilitator briefing. Its public-health context does not validate JGA hours or change this commission's scope. Its notes-versus-transcription distinction also supports costing the actual method rather than adding a transcription service by default. [Primary planning guidance](https://jsna.camden.gov.uk/guidance/qualitative-methods/talk-based-methods/planning-and-design/index.html).

Retained JGA hours still require an applicable resource rate. A salary allocation may be a real resource cost without extra cash payroll. Neither a person-hour total nor the 7.5-hour divisor proves available staff, parallel capacity or elapsed delivery time. No new deadline follows from this review.
