# The Table: draft propositions

Version 0.1 | 30 October 2026 | Internal design for user/client review; not an offer to send

## The proposal in one minute

Build a useful payroll decision research edition first. A buyer receives defined research and use rights; a founding partner supports defined work and receives stated benefits; a practitioner contributes on a practical permitted basis and receives free research and relevant peer value. An event is a possible additional format, not a prerequisite for the first report.

These drafts describe JGA’s potential future products. The signed project still delivers the complete commissioned report, feasibility assessment and agreed assets. It is not replaced with a smaller pack, and no recurring production, advice or events have been added to Riseklix’s obligations.

## TP01: Payroll Decision Evidence Pack

The proposed starting point is the finished, dated edition with a short route through the relevant evidence, supported case contrasts and practical tools for the user’s actual task. The same edition may support different uses; it is not a new bespoke study for every customer. A scoped briefing or prepared client/public-use excerpt is an optional component to define and cost. No duration, turnaround, price or future-update promise is made.

The five contract-named [buyer segments](T02-BUYER-USES-AND-RIGHTS.md) have different possible uses. Product/service strategy, a client brief and an investment research question should not be treated as the same purchase. Employer-side use is an additional hypothesis; participating practitioners already receive the research free. Preserve that benefit and do not count equivalent access twice.

**Concept wording after unaided discovery:**

We are shaping a practical research pack around payroll investment and change decisions. It would bring together the dated findings, relevant case comparisons and tools for thinking through the evidence and next steps. A short, separately scoped briefing could help your team apply the material to a decision you have described. Which part, if any, would add something to what you already use?

Change or stop this concept if the required evidence is absent, existing material already meets the need, or the customer requires representative statistics, private identities, a guaranteed result or a service JGA cannot supply.

## TP02: Founding Research Partnership

One defined edition, a bounded briefing, accurate funding acknowledgement and agreed uses form the minimum combination to examine. Its value must come from a real research, strategy or communication use. A funding partner can help agree topic/focus beforehand; it does not buy favourable findings, suppression, method/results control or an implied endorsement. JGA retains final publication responsibility under the working agreement.

Name the actual edition and funding purpose. If research is already commissioned or complete, describe that accurately and distinguish any additional release, briefing or distribution work; do not present it as a newly commissioned study. Keep production costs and partner/licence revenues separately defined, counting each cost and bundled entitlement once.

A separately defined event/access component remains possible if transparent, wanted, permissioned and deliverable. It is absent from the minimum draft. Funding does not automatically provide participant contacts, individual answers, introductions, meetings or exclusivity.

**Concept wording after unaided discovery:**

One option we are assessing is a founding research partnership for a defined edition on how payroll leaders turn operational needs into investment decisions. The proposed package would fund the research and give your team a focused findings briefing, agreed recognition as a funding partner, and clearly defined rights to use the finished work. Funding would not give a partner control over the findings. Private answers and participant contacts would not be included by default. Any event involvement or commercial introductions would need a separate, transparent scope. We are testing whether that would serve a real decision or communication need for your team.

Change or stop if the economics depend on unagreed lead access, a target audience inconsistent with the research/member fit, editorial control, or benefits that cannot be delivered. General support for the idea is not a funding commitment.

## TP03: Practitioner research and peer cycle

Preserve free attendance and free research, one industry question and optional council recognition from the original concept. The useful exchange to test is relevant evidence and peers, not simply premium dining. Not every proposed question can become a mandatory research question or receive a promised answer.

One short follow-through note, separating useful takeaways from unresolved questions, is an additional benefit idea to test and cost. It is not a promised monthly output, advice desk or new obligation in the signed project. Recognition does not by itself authorise attendance disclosure or attribution; contextual details can still identify someone.

**Concept wording after unaided discovery:**

We are exploring a small senior payroll peer group built around practical research and useful discussion. The working concept includes free attendance and free research, one industry question from each participant, and an exchange with relevant payroll leaders. One possible extra is a short follow-up separating useful takeaways from questions that still need evidence. Council recognition would be optional and separate from permission to publish a contribution. We are assessing the format and its value before arranging any event or asking anyone to commit.

Change or stop if participant/peer fit is weak, hospitality or contribution cannot be accepted, the format creates too much burden, or there is no useful return beyond the event. A permitted remote/written contribution may still be useful; it is neither event attendance nor automatically a usable report contribution.

## Event comparison

Compare the same intended research/member benefit using research-only, remote/written exchange and a small UK in-person gathering. Identify what the gathering adds before assuming all research sales depend on dinners. The proposed four-city programme remains an option to assess later, with changing cohorts rather than a longitudinal panel. No venue, date, membership or launch is confirmed.

## What needs definition before a proposal

Keep these as four grouped product decisions, not a long new participant form:

1. Actual user, decision and existing alternatives, including the contributor’s free research.
2. Specific edition, useful outputs, evidence limits and internal/client/public uses.
3. Responsible delivery owner, preparation/service limits, permissions and practical capacity.
4. Real commercial unit, complete costs, funding timing and separately agreed price/terms.

JGA’s commissioned IP rights are governed by the working agreement. The [use options](T02-use-and-entitlement-options.json) are future customer-product choices, not a new Riseklix licence or veto. Underlying participant and third-party constraints still matter.

## Test it without turning discovery into a pitch

Use the proposed [version 0.2 Table guides](../01-method/TABLE-VALIDATION-GUIDES-v0.2.md): actual recent needs/alternatives before showing a relevant short concept, research alone before asking what an event adds, then evidence/use/purchase-route/objections. Show only relevant options and record the actual version/order. All probes remain selective.

The original Table guide remains in its file and the active WP03 policy is unchanged. This proposed version requires review before actual collection; it activates nothing. Up to four substantive Table contribution events, ideally two buyers/partners and two prospective members, remain separate from six-to-ten distinct eligible report contributors. Four conversations cannot establish market size, price elasticity or a conversion forecast.

The [delivery/validation map](T02-DELIVERY-AND-VALIDATION.md) separates the work needed from evidence actually obtained. All actual customer, participant, permission, commitment, price and financial-input arrays remain empty. Current findings support a draft design; T03 validation and T04/T05 economics remain open.
