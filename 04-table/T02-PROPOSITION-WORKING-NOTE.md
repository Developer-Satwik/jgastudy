# T02: propositions to examine next

Working hypotheses, 29 October 2026. This is a bridge from T01 comparisons to the already drafted Table buyer/member guides, not tested product positioning or a new commitment to deliver ongoing services.

The most promising question to examine is whether JGA can turn bounded original payroll decision cases into a useful decision pack, then add peer exchange where it improves that value. This is an analyst starting hypothesis, not a recommendation to launch or a claim of uniqueness.

Keep three exchanges explicit: a buyer receives research and stated use rights; a partner funds defined work and receives disclosed benefits; a practitioner contributes under specific choices and receives relevant research/peer value. The event is a possible delivery mechanism with its own incremental cost and benefit.

| Hypothesis | What would make it useful | What could disprove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H01 Research use | Resolves a real decision/evidence gap with usable cases and briefing | Free/internal material already answers the need, or no owner/use exists |
| H02 Research partnership | A funder has a genuine use for independent aggregate insight | Value depends on leads, favourable conclusions or private answers |
| H03 Practitioner participation | Relevant peers and a useful follow-through output justify time | Employer restrictions, weak fit or hospitality-only interest |
| H04 Incremental event value | The room adds value beyond the same research remotely | Travel and cost add burden without distinct benefit |

The [structured hypotheses](T02-proposition-hypotheses.json) preserve tests and counter-signals. In future authorised conversations, begin with actual recent needs and alternatives, not a sales pitch listing attractive benefits. Asking if an idea sounds interesting is weaker evidence than understanding a real decision, constraints and approval route. An exploratory positive comment is not a purchase or membership commitment.

No fresh mandatory questions are added to the fourteen report questions. Use existing Table probes selectively and respect the approved collection route. The agreed limit remains up to four exploratory Table contribution events, ideally two buyers and two prospective council members. Such input can refine the design; it cannot establish market size, a representative demand rate or reliable price elasticity.

Current collection remains inactive under the five review decisions. T03 actual validation, T04 product/rights definitions and T05 cost/cash modelling remain open. Nothing in this note authorises outreach, participant-data collection, sponsor contact, venue enquiry, expenditure or publication.
