# T02: offer fit and wording supplement

30 October 2026. Optional preparation for a later authorised conversation. The first-pass three-offer pack and proposed guide remain unchanged; this supplement does not activate either.

## Match the promise to the actual payload

For a material use, establish the edition/evidence period, relevant released material, actual task/context and what the material can support. Use existing analysis/publication records where possible, not a new form for every participant. A reader path may select and interpret; it cannot create missing cases. Aggregate or partial evidence can support a bounded useful explanation. Do not require every eligible contributor to provide a named or publishable detailed case.

Record the actual work request: correction of an error/agreed-scope defect, a later-data/method refresh or a new task. Existing contract correction remedies remain included. Do not unilaterally call a defect a paid update. A legitimate added task/edition may be separately scoped only through the actual agreement. No price, support period or cadence is set here.

For a briefing, define the existing-edition material it interprets and the boundary of new analysis/evidence. For recognition or an excerpt, define the actual placement/asset/use supplied. Fulfilment of that object is separate from readership, useful impact or commercial response.

## Choose wording that matches the stage

These are internal script options after unaided needs and alternatives, not messages to send now. Set the actual edition/purpose before using a version; retain the wording shown and any material change. Do not invent a new study, completed case evidence or an unstaffed service.

Research-use concept:

“We are assessing a research pack that brings together a dated edition, a short route to the findings relevant to your decision and the limits of that evidence. A separately scoped briefing could explain the material for your team. New evidence or client-specific research would need a separate scope. Which part would add something to what you already use?”

Support for an edition already commissioned or complete:

“One option we are assessing is partner support for releasing and using the research edition. The package could include a defined findings briefing, disclosed partner recognition and agreed uses of the work. We would state exactly what that support funds. It would not give control over the conclusions or default access to private responses. Would that serve a real purpose for your team?”

A genuinely new future research cycle requires a different description of the actual new work and scope. It is not added to this commission by a concept script. Legitimate licensing/partner revenues and cost recovery remain possible; count each cost and bundled entitlement once. The current practitioner concept's free research and optional follow-through remain intact; there is no need to manufacture a new script where the wording already fits.

## Interpret only what was shown

Preserve unaided need, reaction to a proposed feature, condition, proposal request and actual agreement as distinct contextual statements. They are not a mandatory ordered funnel. Research-only interest cannot validate an unshown access package; an omitted or unshown variant remains unknown. A proposal request is not a sale or permission to publish/respond to another person.

The [blank optional aid](T02-offer-fit-aid.json) holds no actual data and is not a new participant questionnaire. The existing WP03 guide/count/permission and restricted-storage arrangements remain unchanged. No new mandatory report questions, case quotas, rights grants or collection actions follow.
