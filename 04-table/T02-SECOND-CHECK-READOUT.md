# T02 offers: second-check readout

30 October 2026. Historical, segmented-market, specific-competitor and earlier-study challenge of the three concrete draft propositions. This is design refinement, not buyer/member validation. The first-pass pack, rights/delivery maps and proposed guide remain preserved.

## The conclusion

Keep the three drafts, with a tighter link between the promised use and what can actually be delivered. The central question is no longer just whether a research pack, partner package or peer cycle sounds useful. It is which released evidence, service or entitlement helps a defined task, at a stated point in time, and what a customer or practitioner is actually reacting to.

## What the second check adds

**A useful label cannot replace relevant evidence.** The five commercial segments remain hypotheses, not five automatically served markets. Actual context, country/remit, period and available detail govern whether a particular use is supportable. Six-to-ten eligible contributors do not by themselves establish a case contrast for every task. Anonymous or aggregate material can still be useful and can count as eligible research input under the existing rules. No named-case quota or extra country coverage is added. CLM-0526, CLM-0531.

**A new briefing date does not refresh old evidence.** The edition date, evidence period and service date describe different things. ONS provides a narrow analogy for distinguishing mistakes from later-data/method revisions; its process is not imposed on JGA. Current contract defects and corrections remain covered. Genuinely new evidence or tasks require the actual scope decision, without an automatic additional fee or invented support period. CLM-0523, CLM-0524, CLM-0525.

**Interpretation and new research are different work.** ISG separates inquiry about published-study context from additional research services. A JGA briefing may be tailored, but cannot supply facts missing from the edition. A narrow answer or explicit unknown can be the right response; extra analysis is not silently included merely because a client asks it during a short call. CLM-0527.

**A benefit needs a fulfilment basis, while value needs evidence.** A funding acknowledgement on an agreed edition, a scoped briefing or an authorised use asset can be specified and delivered. This does not prove reach, sales or impact. Oakleaf already turns expert webinar material into written themes and a recording route; a note or briefing is therefore not distinctive by format alone. That does not make paid interpretation unviable. Its incremental use must be tested against actual alternatives. CLM-0528, CLM-0529, CLM-0530.

**Test the exact concept and stage.** A partner supporting an existing commissioned edition must not be described as commissioning a new study. Research-only feedback cannot validate an unshown event/access variant. Keep unaided needs, proposed-feature reactions, conditions and actual agreement distinct. Unshown or omitted information stays unknown rather than becoming a refusal or a sale. CLM-0532, CLM-0533.

## Five selective refinements

| Condition | Practical application | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OC01: Evidence actually available for the use | Match the proposed task and claim to actual releasable material, preserving role, country/remit, period, evidence object and limits. A reading path cannot create an absent case or make one setting apply to another. | Keep a narrowly useful interpretation or an honest gap where appropriate. No extra case quotas, named-case requirement, mandatory countries or removal of eligible aggregate contributions. |
| OC02: Edition, evidence, service and change reason | Separate the edition/publication date, evidence period and later briefing date. Distinguish correction of a mistake/scope defect, later-data/method refresh and a new task. | The existing contract’s correction/defect remedies remain included; no automatic extra fee, expiry, refresh cadence or future support promise. ONS is a method analogy only. |
| OC03: Briefing work boundary | Define whether the briefing interprets the existing edition or needs new evidence, analysis or client-specific research. Tailoring explanation remains possible, but a request is not a formatting change merely because it is short. | Narrow the answer, retain the unknown or agree genuine new work through the actual scope process. No unilateral charge or current-project expansion. |
| OC04: Incremental benefit and fulfilment basis | Specify what acknowledgement, briefing, excerpt/use asset or optional follow-through is delivered and what extra job it helps. Existing expert/event follow-through formats mean format alone is not a unique benefit. | A delivered placement/service is different from reach, interest, commercial response or useful impact. No need to be unique; actual relevance/value must be explored without invented outcomes. |
| OC05: Exact concept, asset stage and feedback | Use wording that fits the actual edition/funding purpose and record the relevant version shown. Separate an unaided need, feature reaction, conditional proposal request and actual agreement. | An unshown variant is unknown, not refused or validated. These are contextual distinctions, not an ordered funnel, conversion forecast or added participant form. |

These are optional analyst checks, not five extra participant questions or a scoring model. Four original hypotheses, three propositions, five contract segments plus the employer-use hypothesis, fourteen report questions and six buyer/six member IDs remain unchanged.
