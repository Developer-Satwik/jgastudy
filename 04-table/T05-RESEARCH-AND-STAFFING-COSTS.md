# Research and staffing costs: first model component

31 October 2026. One future equivalent research edition. The worksheet values a possible UK-contractor delivery route and shows JGA retained time. It is the first part of T05; event operations, sales, servicing, tax/expenses and the cash schedule follow separately.

## What the worksheet now measures

The production schedule includes research design and primary-source work, preparation, scheduling and permissions, contribution collection and coding, synthesis, the complete narrative and summary, the commercial preview, factual/rights checking, language editing, design and editable graphics, one consolidated revision, format/data QA, handover, coordination and research pricing/use options. PowerPoint or equivalent editable original graphics, Word/PDF and supporting Excel chart data remain within the agreed delivery.

Five retained JGA tasks cover scope/context, introductions, subject-matter review, consolidated decisions and publication/handover decisions. These are separate people-hours on the JGA side. A person carrying several roles does not automatically represent several employees or concurrent capacity.

All effort figures are bottom-up analyst assumptions. The lower case assumes reusable structure and readily available evidence/introductions; the higher case allows more complexity and handling. They are neither measured times nor confidence bounds. Actual effort can exceed them. Person-hours do not establish elapsed duration, staff availability or a revised delivery promise.

## How rates are used

| Work group | Published reference | Use in the component |

Sources: [CIEP](https://www.ciep.uk/knowledge-hub/suggested-minimum-rates.html), [ProCopywriters](https://www.procopywriters.co.uk/survey/2026/), [YunoJuno methodology](https://www.yunojuno.com/freelancer-rates-report), [research role](https://www.yunojuno.com/freelancer-rates-job-role/market-research-consultant), [design role](https://www.yunojuno.com/freelancer-rates-job-role/graphic-designer).

The day-rate conversion uses an editable **7.5-hour planning assumption**. Publishers do not establish that day length or availability by the hour. Minimum bookings, tax, fees and expenses can change an actual supplier bill. UK day rates and US hourly rates are separate markets; no currency conversion or Indian outsourcing cost is inferred.

CIEP figures are suggested minima, not observed averages. ProCopywriters' 573 respondents are the overall survey count, not a confirmed rate-item base. YunoJuno's mixed platform records are not a representative payroll-specialist sample or the number of observations behind each role figure.

## Reading the monetary result

The working case values producer labour at **£8,726.14 using the published reference values**. Their tax/fee inclusions and supplier booking terms remain unresolved. This is a benchmark comparison, not a proposed Riseklix fee, committed spend or full programme budget. The same build recalculates when hours, contributor count or selected rate inputs change.

JGA hours are calculated, but its five hourly cost inputs start blank. JGA time value and combined labour value therefore remain unavailable until those inputs are defined. A deliberate zero cost is different from a blank, and existing salary/resource allocation is different from an extra cash payment.

A future delivery decision can use an agreed supplier package, a correctly scoped bottom-up plan or a reconciled subcontract split. Do not add a full supplier fee and the same producer task valuation together. Do not add freelance overhead already reflected in a quoted/suggested rate a second time as employee on-cost.

## Relation to the current commission

The current USD1,500 fee remains USD1,000 report plus USD500 Table feasibility. The reported USD750 upfront and remaining completion-triggered USD750 retain their existing status. The benchmark comparison is a future sourcing/resource scenario. It is not the time already spent on this commission and is not added to the current report fee for the same work. No USD/GBP comparison is calculated without a dated, explicit FX basis.

## What is next

Cost customer/licence fulfilment, briefings, partner work and sales separately, including unsuccessful proposals where performed. Then add the UK gathering increment, travel and dated cash obligations. The up-to-four exploratory Table contributions remain separate from future dinners and report-contributor counts; genuinely shared work receives a specific overlap credit rather than being counted twice.

Before completing the whole model, choose an applicable delivery route, actual or explicitly assumed costs, event/date/city details, expense/tax treatment and receipt/payment timing. The current component creates no order, hire, actual participant record, supplier enquiry or spend. Collection arrangements and client approvals remain open under the existing process.
