## Reading the comparison correctly

These are selected business-model comparables, not six interchangeable suppliers or a market census. Direct payroll offers help assess audience alternatives; adjacent HR/L&D offers help examine funding, admission and rights. The different units should not be averaged into a market price. Organisation support, annual research access, a Council seat, a custom research project and an event sponsorship purchase different things.

A public fee shows an offer, not paid demand or a price JGA can achieve. For example, the Payroll Centre fee includes continuing support and training; using it to price one report would ignore most of the service. Fees not visible remain unknown. No enquiry, registration, quote request or purchase occurred.
