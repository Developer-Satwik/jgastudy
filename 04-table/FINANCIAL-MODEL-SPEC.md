# Financial model specification

Working specification only. No workbook, verified cost budget, sales forecast or commercial conclusion has yet been produced.

## Decisions and model scope

Compare Model A syndicated research without events with Model B event-supported research. Model the one-city UK pilot and four-location annual programme. Keep the report's commercial value separate from the event's incremental benefit.

Working reporting-currency proposal is GBP, subject to JGA review. Preserve original GBP/EUR/USD inputs and dated exchange-rate direction. Texas is a state; no particular Texas city is confirmed. Do not fill travel or venue costs as though it were.

## Proposed workbook structure

Readme; evidence and assumptions; product/licence definitions; Model A research-only; Model B UK pilot; annual city programme; staff time; monthly cash flow; scenarios; sensitivity and break-even; decision dashboard.

## Units to define first

Programme year, reports/editions, events, unique practitioners, total seats including hosts/moderation, partners, licence customers by tier, staff hours by role, invoice dates, cash receipt dates, deposits and cancellation exposure.

Cash contribution, fully loaded operating result and funding requirement are different outputs. Staff opportunity cost may not be an additional cash outflow; show the distinction. Define net/gross taxes and service charges consistently and avoid pretending this is tax advice.

## Revenue and rights

- Partnership price × partner count, with benefits and exclusivity assumptions explicit.
- Licence price × customer count by internal-use or external commercial-reuse tier.
- Do not count licences bundled in partner benefits again as separate sales.
- Keep optional future bespoke work outside the core case unless clearly separated.
- Repeat purchase and renewals are assumptions until supported; an annual programme is not contracted recurring revenue.

## Costs and timing

Include research design, collection, verification, writing, editing, design, analysis, graphics, commercial sales effort, recruitment/scheduling, hospitality, room minimums, service/taxes, moderation, travel/accommodation, administration, insurance or other necessary operating costs, JGA management and contingency.

Use the current US$1,500 commission as an identifiable first-project investment, not proof that future editions can always be produced at that fee. Recurring labour must not disappear from the model.

Classify each cost as current public rate, written indicative quote, contracted rate, historical comparator or analyst assumption. A per-person menu is not necessarily a private-room minimum. Record capacity for 12–15 practitioners plus hosts and other required seats.

## Core calculations to implement and verify

- Cash contribution = recognised modelling revenue minus direct attributable cash costs, with definition disclosed.
- Fully loaded operating result additionally includes the model's staff/overhead allocation without double counting.
- Remaining funding gap = relevant fixed costs minus partner/other net contribution already included.
- Break-even licence count = ceiling of positive remaining funding gap divided by positive net contribution per licence. Handle zero/negative unit contribution explicitly.
- Closing cash = opening cash + cash receipts − cash payments.
- Peak funding requirement = largest cumulative cash deficit, subject to stated starting cash and timing.
- Annual event totals reconcile to individual city inputs; common report costs occur only as specified.
- Model A/B use consistent core research assumptions. Event-related benefits remain incremental assumptions, not automatic attribution of all sales to dinners.

## Scenarios and stress tests

Conservative/base/optimistic cases with independently editable inputs. Test zero/one fewer partner, delayed payment, fewer research sales, lower price, higher hospitality/travel, more staff/recruitment hours, cancellation exposure and delayed launch. Show which assumptions change the decision.

Before a pilot commitment, compare non-refundable cash exposure with confirmed and received funding. Before expansion, require evidence from the UK pilot plus local cost, audience and operating feasibility. No venue or supplier commitment is made by the workbook itself.

## Acceptance checks

Every input has a source or labelled assumption ID. Formulas recalculate; no hidden hard-coded totals, circular references or broken links. Sensitivity movements have the expected direction. Test zero sales, loss of a partner and delayed receipts. Charts reconcile to tables. The workbook remains usable offline and carries its own definitions and evidence notes.
