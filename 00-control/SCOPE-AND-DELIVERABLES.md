## Interpretation

The practitioner target is 6–10 distinct eligible contributors. The up-to-four Table conversations are separately tracked, with permitted overlap. Original third-party source rights and participant confidentiality limit redistribution of supporting material. A report being fit for commercial use does not guarantee sales.

Formal dates, final report length and the primary audience/geography are agreed at the project checkpoints. One consolidated editorial revision is included; correction of factual, technical and scope defects is separate.
