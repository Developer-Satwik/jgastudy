HR & Payroll Pulse 2026

A Reality Check for Europe

Drawing on new insights from the SD Worx Research Institute, HR & Payroll Pulse 2026 presents the latest evidence on how organisations are navigating workforce planning, payroll reliability, pay transparency, employee experience and the real impact of AI at work.

Download the report

What you’ll learn from HR & Payroll Pulse 2026

This report offers leaders a grounded understanding of how work is functioning today. It reveals the moments where foundations strengthen trust and the points where small gaps can quickly scale into operational risk. With evidence from employers and employees across 16 European countries, it provides a practical, research led view of how work is delivered and where improvement has the greatest impact.

- Where organisations are feeling the greatest operational pressure and how leaders are responding.
- How employee trust is shaped by accuracy in payroll, clarity in pay and consistency in day‑to‑day work.
- Why workforce planning has become a priority and how capability gaps affect readiness.
- How employees across Europe are experiencing work, from wellbeing and workload to fairness and security.
- How AI is changing roles, expectations and confidence inside organisations.

What this year’s findings tell us

Organisations across Europe are pushing to modernise work, yet progress and experience do not always align. The Pulse data shows where intention is strong, but results are uneven.
