# Initial source discovery

28 October 2026. These are planning checks and a starting queue, not completed report findings. Original statistics and commercial conclusions have not been extracted or approved for publication.

## Primary methodology checked

- [Government Analysis Function questionnaire guidance](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/questionnaire-design-guidance/). Relevant to clear wording, respondent burden and testing. Page marked under review.
- [MRS B2B guidance](https://www.mrs.org.uk/standards/mrs-guidelines-for-businesstobusiness-research-insight-and-other-data-processing-activities). Relevant professional guidance; no membership/accreditation claim.
- [Quality in Qualitative Evaluation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/quality-in-qualitative-evaluation-qqe-html). Current government HTML presents an appraisal framework. Its update date does not make all underlying methodology new research.
- [Accessible charts checklist](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/charts-a-checklist/). Use for readable, accessible outputs and source tables.
- [Green Book 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book--2/the-green-book-2026). Adapt sensitivity-testing principles; this project is a commercial cash/resource model, not a government social-benefit appraisal.

## Proposed six-model shortlist

The final six are not yet fixed. Candidate mechanisms and original routes:

1. Payroll Influences: partner-developed payroll research.
2. [NelsonHall](https://research.nelson-hall.com/): analyst intelligence and licensed research distribution.
3. [Hackett Payroll Applied Intelligence](https://www.thehackettgroup.com/payroll-applied-intelligence/): continuing payroll decision support.
4. [i4cp membership](https://www.i4cp.com/solutions/membership): research, tools and practitioner community; inspect [licence terms](https://www.i4cp.com/member-terms-of-use).
5. [PayrollOrg CPO forum](https://cpo.org/): senior payroll community and executive discussion.
6. [GDS Group](https://gdsgroup.com/): sponsor-supported executive experiences, as an adjacent mechanism rather than a directly comparable confidential research product.

Agents reviewed selected official routes during planning. Each final comparable still requires a uniform full review. No published or achieved commercial price is assumed where not established.

## Known access and interpretation cautions

- Search snippets and landing pages are not substitutes for unseen reports.
- Old linked reports remain historical even when their catalogue is crawled today.
- Free supplier research is a real substitute, but its existence does not prove every buyer need is met.
- The early JGA project proposal's public-source figures remain unverified candidates until extracted and checked in this workspace.
