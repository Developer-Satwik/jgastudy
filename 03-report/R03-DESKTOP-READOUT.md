# Which changes could address the payroll need?

29 October 2026. R03 desktop synthesis, revised after the user-requested second challenge. Original practitioner integration and final publication checks remain open. No procurement choice, product test or external contact occurred.

## Main conclusion

Choose the change against the task, outcome and context established in R02. Compare what it changes, what it requires, what remains with each party and how the result would be verified. Technology mechanism and operating ownership are separate dimensions; neither a connector nor an outsourced label is a complete operating arrangement.

The [eight-path option map](R03-OPTIONS-AND-TRADEOFFS.md) covers process/control, existing configuration, integration, AI assistance, platform replacement, managed execution, mixed components and specialist capability. These are combinable options, not a ranking or a maturity ladder. No route is automatically best or cheapest.

## What the research established

### Integration changes the handoff; it does not remove it

Primary technical examples distinguish source/blank semantics, mapping, locks, job acknowledgement, partial errors, events and authorised access. This makes the evidence requirement concrete: did the right population and period reach the intended state, were exceptions resolved, and was the downstream outcome checked? CLM-0206–0212.

HMRC's GfC22, published 22 October 2026, adds current identity/transition context and distinguishes particular already-created discrepancies needing HMRC correction. The report should explain the migration and resolution dependency, not reproduce an undifferentiated resend instruction. CLM-0213. [Official guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/help-to-prevent-payroll-errors-and-paye-rti-disputed-charges-gfc22/help-to-prevent-payroll-errors-and-paye-rti-disputed-charges-gfc22).

### Delivery must be mapped component by component

Official guidance and detailed supplier definitions show retained work alongside delegated execution. Cintra's documented combinations separate processing payroll from making payments; Fourth's definition includes separately opted-in payment submission and customer checks. These are scope examples, not achieved-service evidence. CLM-0214–0217.

Software help, operator execution, employer approval and direct employee resolution require separate entitlement. A hybrid can target a constraint but introduces coordination. A time-bound specialist can supply expertise but still needs authority, review and knowledge handback. Current capability and no-immediate-replacement routes remain legitimate comparisons. CLM-0219–0222. [Cintra workflow](https://help.cintra.co.uk/docs/in-house-vs-outsourced-payroll).

### Automation and AI need task-level definitions

Configured rules, workflow, data movement, anomaly support, generated explanations and action-taking assistance have different requirements. SAP's configurable payroll templates provide a non-generative control example; ADP's dated release describes variance assistance with practitioner review and supplier-stated availability. Neither demonstrates realised benefits for JGA's cohort. CLM-0231–0234.

Record availability, actual use and assessed benefit separately. Government AI guidance supports matching the tool to the need, testing, intervention and lifecycle support; its public-sector policies/legal passages are not adopted as current private-employer law. CLM-0229–0230. [AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government/artificial-intelligence-playbook-for-the-uk-government-html).

No supplier time-saving number is used as a performance benchmark. A broad current agent overview includes development language; a specific dated availability announcement cannot establish uniform deployment of every described action or completion of a later planned rollout. These distinctions prevent a speculative adoption story.

### A live platform and a demonstrated result are different

The selected Scottish Government evidence contains controlled cutover and sampled-pay positives, together with dated reporting/support limitations and benefits not yet demonstrable at the audited stage. Its later closure account combines retrospective events, management's assessment/progress by publication and future plans. It does not independently establish conditions in October 2026. CLM-0223–0227.

The separate Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body reports positive internal-audit assurance following its HR/payroll replacement. This preserves a functioning case without implying zero errors or comparative product superiority. CLM-0228. The underlying evidence and exact locators are in the [case memo](research-notes/r03/cases/CASE-EVIDENCE.md). The organisations and their HR/payroll implementations must not be conflated.

Together, these cases support separate checks for essential continuity, usable records/reporting, support, adoption and assessed benefit. Go-live can be a meaningful achievement while further work remains. Assurance is bounded by its scope, sample and date. Two public employers do not establish a success/failure rate or a typical private-employer result.

## What this means for the report's readers

For payroll leaders: describe the outcome and compare viable changes to the actual task. Check what work moves, what new review/support is needed, what remains and what must stay reliable during transition. No immediate purchase can be sensible where the current arrangement is adequate or an identified prerequisite is unresolved; a material gap still needs an owner and response.

For suppliers: demonstrate relevant inputs, states, exception routes, retained duties and usable outputs. Specify version, scope, territory, access and execution rather than rely on broad seamless, fully managed or AI labels. Evidence of closure matters more than a demonstration alone.

For JGA: connect capability with operating authority and knowledge. Recruitment, development, temporary expertise, process changes, revised services and technology can be alternatives or complements. The report should let the evidence determine the response rather than favour a commercial route.

## Counterevidence, holds and limits

- Existing scope and configurable capability can address some needs; replacement is not presumed necessary. Public-sector sourcing guidance is a design lens, not a private procurement obligation.
- Beta-labelled async documentation does not prove general availability. Product state definitions and examples are not observed incident frequencies or throughput guarantees.
- Two dated Cintra articles differ on UI/status/calculation behaviour. CLM-0218 holds universal behaviour claims; no product defect or its resolution is inferred.
- Current retained Staffology page metadata differs from cached reader headers. The retained body's date is recorded; no capability-change trend follows.
- Dated historical service tiers do not become current package rights. Geography and usable export/handback require case-specific confirmation.
- Official guidance, technical definitions, supplier releases, management reviews and external audit are distinct classes. Existing numerical holds remain held; no new pooled adoption rate, vendor score or ROI was created.
