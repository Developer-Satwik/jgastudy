## Questions addressed

1. Which studies cover needs, technology, outsourcing, AI and transformation? See the source-family map in the readout and appraisals below.
2. Does decision-related research already exist? Yes: G2/Paycom examines buying logic; Vistra examines project changes/delays; PPCI includes investment themes; Audit Scotland documents an approval and implementation case. This prevents a broad novelty claim.
3. Whose experience is represented? Payroll teams, providers, professionals, mixed HR/Finance roles, multinational employers and public-sector cases differ. They are not one interchangeable population.
4. What limits comparison? Different questions, bases, dates, units, geography, recruitment and funding, plus text/chart or label conflicts.
5. What original input could help? Evidence and decision transitions from verified senior practitioners, including successful implementation and rational renewal as well as adverse outcomes.
6. What exists around confidence and strategic value? PPCI is a relevant existing product. Its reviewed edition is distinct from the publisher's partner-developed reports.
7. What commercial formats exist? Free reports, partner research, indices and analyst/benchmarking products are visible. The full six-model comparison and demand validation remain open.

## Research design implications

- Recruit actual relevant decision involvement, not only a senior title.
- Anchor the guide to a recent case and record outcome/stage explicitly.
- Distinguish requested, planned, approved, funded and delivered changes.
- Ask about sponsor, budget ownership, approval and veto separately.
- Examine evidence that affected the choice, alternatives and implementation conditions.
- Include reasonable retention/no-change and successful delivery.
- Keep employer evidence separate from commercial buyer/vendor interpretation.
- Use 6–10 contributions for explanatory depth, not representative market rankings.
