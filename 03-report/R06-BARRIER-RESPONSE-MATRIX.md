29 October 2026. Internal analytical aid; independent integrated review passed for desktop synthesis. Not a validated taxonomy, causal remedy, required questionnaire or supplier-conversion score. See the [desktop findings](R06-DESKTOP-READOUT.md).

## Start with four separate facts

Record the actual option, dated disposition of the specific decision object, implementation and benefit. A retained platform, changed contract route and staged organisational change can coexist. A project can contain successive or different component decisions. Preserve source words, date precision and knowledge; do not infer pending, rejection or no formal approval from silence.

These are optional analysis rows, not a list to read to every contributor. A possible response is not an adopted action or observed effect. No condition is presumed addressable, no remedy is guaranteed and a reasoned stop or continuation can be legitimate.

## Record the object and chronology

For a material event retain: case/object/scope; event date and position as of; source/knowledge; original and any supported normalised disposition; selected/retained option; what changed/continued; stated reason; alternatives; remaining condition/owner/review where known; actual action; delivery/benefit; later event and missingness. The [structured map](R06-outcome-map.json) keeps a blank template. No confidential complete committee, budget or contract pack is required.

A postponed rollout is not automatically deferred investment approval. The West Sussex March/October history is one programme and a later self-service notice is not full payroll assurance. A cancelled NICS payroll component did not erase other functioning components; that 2001 event is historical context outside recent fieldwork. A cancelled NZ advance notice is not an established cancelled tender or upgrade.

## Keep buyer evidence in its own lane

A hypothetical trigger, future preference, reviewed arrangement, reported ease and stated premium justification are different measures. They do not establish a funded proposal, actual switch/renewal or causality. Overall survey samples/country counts do not substitute for item-valid bases or UK-specific outcomes. Moorepay numeric conflicts and earlier holds remain.

Use the existing unaided Q006–Q013 routes and fourteen core questions. Do not demand disclosure, fill unknowns from market anecdotes or force a decline story. Retaining software can coexist with investment in people, process, controls or another service component.
