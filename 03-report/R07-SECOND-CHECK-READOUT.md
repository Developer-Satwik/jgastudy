# R07 second challenge: what changes a priority into the next action

29 October 2026. Internal integrated desktop challenge; independent integrated review passed. Requested after the first R07 pass. No original practitioner evidence or external contact.

## Result of the challenge

The first-pass distinction between stated priority, commitment, resources and result still holds. The additional lenses sharpen five conditions: the origin of a priority, its sequence alongside ongoing work, actual usable capability, the underlying work before choosing a response, and whose outcome/action is involved.

This makes the report more practical. A useful explanation is not merely “integration was a priority but was delayed.” It should identify the concrete task, what was already known or available, the actual dependency and next decision. This example describes the analytical approach, not a collected case or evidence that all delays are justified.

## Historical lens: recurring themes do not measure the same employer's progress

CIPP2020 expectations about transactional work and skills differ from CIPP2024 current AI/RPA use and perceived pressure. The populations, task definitions, measures and linked respondents do not establish a matched ambition-to-investment history. No subtraction, progress gap, causal story or funded share is adopted. Reappearing “automation” could refer to different work or an ongoing priority; the actual explanation must be established. CLM-0410, CLM-0411.

LFB's existing2025 programme report provides a narrower concrete contrast: phase2 depends on phase1 and is scheduled alongside ordinary work. It concerns specific HR modules in one combined HR/payroll programme. This is declared sequencing, not proof of low importance, no funding, an optimal plan or later completion. A passed prospective2026 date is not a new observed outcome. No new employer history is added. CLM-0412, CLM-0413.

## Market lens: an included feature is not an implemented improvement

FreeAgent's feature declaration and setup route illustrate that advertised inclusion and the relevant account/access/configuration conditions are different facts. No actual account, filing, quote or total-cost claim is tested. A next action could be establishing existing capability rather than assuming replacement; that remains a case hypothesis, not a finding of unused features. CLM-0414, CLM-0417.

Moneysoft separates update prompts/checks from a June2026 statement about a future BiK update. A roadmap does not prove shipment, local installation, useful operation, employer resources or legal readiness. BrightPay's own-employer and multiple-client routes also show why the provider's work and a client's priority can have different owners and beneficiaries. Neither route is evidence of observed misalignment or market prevalence. CLM-0415, CLM-0416, CLM-0418.

## Portfolio, affected-user and alternative-response lenses

Government project-delivery guidance considers existing work, capacity, dependencies and review. Government digital guidance uses performance/user evidence and connected service responsibilities for improvement. Both are normative contexts, not private-employer payroll requirements or observed results. The useful adaptation is selective: clarify what must happen first, what is known about the affected task and whose action controls the next step. No full portfolio, rigid review timetable, stakeholder census or additional interviews are required. CLM-0425–CLM-0428.

## Changes applied under the unchanged instrument

| Selective refinement | Why it matters | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin and material revision | Where the history matters, establish whether the stated priority is new, continuing, re-scoped or re-timed, its last evidenced state and the actual reason for a material change. | Repeated themes are not matched employer histories; a passed target date does not establish later delivery, persistent delay or failure. Do not require a complete project archive. CLM-0410, CLM-0411, CLM-0413. |
| Sequence beside ongoing work | Where a milestone depends on other work or capacity, record the actual dependency, whose action matters and the next relevant review or decision. | Later order is not proof of lower importance, rejection or no funding. A government management framework is not an observed private-payroll rule; no forced portfolio rank or optimal sequence. CLM-0412, CLM-0413, CLM-0425, CLM-0427. |
| Actual capability, account and service scope | For a feature/update priority, establish relevant task, account/service scope, known inclusion/access, released versus future availability, configuration/use and who must act. | Supplier declarations, entitlement, release, installation and useful operation are separate facts that can coexist across tasks. No product trial, universal capability, zero total cost or inferred new purchase. CLM-0414, CLM-0415, CLM-0416, CLM-0417, CLM-0418. |
| Needed work and capability before a chosen route | Keep the actual work, role/remit, performing party and known resources separate from a proposed hire, learning, process, retention or tool response; distinguish employer/team and geography units. | Recruiter advice, hosted account, service promise and salary inputs are not funded employer demand, available candidates or validated remedies. Constructed prompts preselect routes and do not validate them. CLM-0419, CLM-0420, CLM-0421, CLM-0422, CLM-0423, CLM-0424. |
| Whose outcome and whose next action | Where another function, provider, client or affected user matters, distinguish the contributor’s stated value from evidence about that task/outcome and the condition another party actually controls. | Do not assume misalignment, user satisfaction, contractual responsibility or permission. No supplier/employee cohort expansion, additional interview obligation or full stakeholder census. CLM-0418, CLM-0426, CLM-0428. |

The five conditions supplement, rather than replace, the first-pass six priority dimensions, six context dimensions, four comparison claim purposes and blank templates. Existing Q005 still asks for up to two organisational priorities over12–24months, unaided first. There is no extra compulsory question, ordinal status ladder or score. Actual practices and source expectations are kept separate; not known, not measured, not applicable and not disclosed remain different.

## What remains unestablished

We do not have permissioned original cases showing the actual relative priority, access/configuration, changed resources, alternative response, affected-user outcome or next-decision reason. Six to ten purposive contributions will support careful case explanations, not representative sector/size/country percentages. Published advice and service declarations cannot fill those arrays or expand the unapproved proposed cohort.

No future policy statement, supplier promise, old target date or source silence is promoted into a delivered outcome. Existing Moorepay/Deloitte/UI holds remain, including the new first-pass branding/customisation conflict. No prices, statutory rates, salary values, numerical forecasts or Table inputs are added.
