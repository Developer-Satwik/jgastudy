# R07 priority evidence and contextual comparison method

29 October 2026. Internal analysis design, supported by QQE context/quality guidance and the existing questionnaire. No participant data, new questionnaire, confidence score or completed subgroup analysis.

## What a priority record means

Establish whose priority is described: participant, payroll team, employer, group programme or a public respondent population. Record the specific work/outcome and time horizon before assigning a theme. The same term can describe a wish, current operating work, a separate proposed project or a continuing obligation. Evaluation, planning, approval, funding and delivery need their actual basis; no status is inferred from enthusiasm or source silence.

The existing Q005 asks about expected organisational priorities over 12–24 months and up to two priorities with selective clarification. Funding is separate. This design does not add a mandatory status questionnaire, ask every participant for a confidential budget or assume that every improvement needs new funding. Approved/being delivered can coexist; a recurring operating budget and a special programme allocation are different contexts.

## How to compare without overstating

QQE asks whether case selection supports intended comparisons, describes achieved and missing coverage, preserves source context, explores negative cases and makes wider inference traceable. We apply those principles to the proposed small purposive cohort; no representative market or subgroup estimate follows. METH7-C01–C02.

Distinguish four claim types: observed contrast in the collected cases; a source-supported contextual mechanism; a hypothesis to test in later cases; and a group difference not established. These are different claim purposes, not an ordinal evidence score. A mechanism can explain why context matters without showing a sector gives it greater priority or has funded it.

Compare actual responsibility/service boundary, work, horizon, question and evidence type before attributing a contrast to headcount, sector or location. A pooled multinational survey, a single public employer and an implementation declaration cannot form a clean country comparison. Missing or sparse constituencies should be identified, without claiming that unrepresented groups have no need or that groups are identical. METH7-C04.

## Preserve the original account

Keep unaided and prompted themes distinguishable. Similar labels may refer to different tasks; a case can have several contexts and a theme can appear in both successful and stalled cases. Recurrence in 6–10 purposive contributions may guide explanation but does not become a national priority ranking. Counterexamples and alternative explanations remain visible rather than being averaged away.

For the eventual report, retain enough context to assess applicability while respecting confidentiality and re-identification risk in a small professional population. Broad descriptors can still identify an individual. This is an analytical aid under existing Q002/Q005 and recent-case questions, not an expanded sample or a completed coding framework for final fieldwork. METH7-C03–C04.

## Remaining evidence

Original eligible, permissioned cases must establish actual priority status, authority, resource arrangements and consequences. Unknown, not measured, not applicable and not disclosed remain separate. No interview, named case ranking, forecast or Table financial input was created.
