# R07: dated planning signals and organisational commitment

29 October 2026. Internal desktop research. Two new primary guidance routes, five canonical reuses and seven bounded claim/design records. No original practitioner, financial input, product trial, numerical forecast or external contact.

## Main finding

An external change can make a payroll task relevant without establishing that an employer has approved or funded a project. Conversely, work needed for an already operative change should not automatically be presented as a future investment ambition. Separate the signal's date, scope and status from the organisation's own decision, resources and delivery evidence.

## Read the policy version, not just a headline

The [HMRC BiK manual](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/draft-guidance-and-legislation-to-aid-preparation-for-reporting-benefits-in-kind-in-real-time) still calls itself draft/interim. Its index describes an option for globally mobile employees through a service to be available in November 2026; the phased chapter specifies benefit groups and exclusions. Those details matter more than a broad “all employers need new payroll” interpretation.

The live chapters retain some earlier future-update and voluntary-period text. For example, the preparation chapter discusses the old April 2026 voluntary registration window alongside later November 2026 arrangements. As of 29 October, that earlier window is not an instruction that a reader can still register for it. Likewise, a forecast technical-guidance delivery date is not proof that the promised detail is now available. Do not invent a policy-change chronology or silently resolve cross-page drafting differences. Check the applicable current detail at final publication or action time. SIG7-C03.

The chapter's data-flow, training and employee-communication points show that preparation extends beyond software. Its cash-timing discussion is a prospective context, not a new tax amount or employer cash-flow calculation. Benefits provided, payroll frequency, third-party/group data and affected employees can alter the relevant work. These are conditions to establish, not measured size/sector differences. SIG7-C02.

## Separate already operative work from future preparation

The [HMRC SSP transition page](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sickness-absences-that-start-before-and-end-on-or-after-6-april-2026) was published before the April 2026 change and retains a preparation heading. The [Acas current SSP page](https://www.acas.org.uk/checking-sick-pay/statutory-sick-pay-ssp), updated 30 June 2026, explicitly describes the changes as effective on 6 April 2026. This contrast helps prevent old forward-looking wording being re-labelled as a future 2027 ambition.

The source text preserves eligibility and transition conditions. We do not calculate payments, apply an eligibility rule to an individual, adopt statutory rates/earnings examples or independently assure the whole consolidated legal position. Employment law and payroll regime applicability need the actual jurisdiction and case; an official guidance example does not make every country or workforce directly comparable. SIG7-C04.

HMRC's instructions to review policy, check provider preparedness and communicate do not show that any employer did so. A requirement, a recommended preparation action, an internal plan, approval, allocated resources and an accurate operational result are distinct evidence claims. SIG7-C05.

## Funded does not mean fully delivered

The existing Synergy original is reused for one bounded status contrast, not another employer case. Its programme account reports an earlier investment approval and executed contract; the finance case distinguishes a ring-fenced envelope and departmental responsibilities from potential later shortfalls and forecast benefits. No amount, payback date, optimism factor or arithmetic is adopted. The public summary's stated positions are not independent financial assurance or actual later benefit. Existing numerical and NAO scope limits remain intact. SIG7-C06.

This supports recording funding separately from a priority label. A contributor may know a task is in a plan but not know whether the relevant budget/resources were committed. They can report that limit without supplying confidential accounts. Do not infer “unfunded” simply because an amount is undisclosed, or presume that an approved programme resolves every local funding need.

## Application under the unchanged core

For up to two priorities under Q005, first obtain the unaided answer, then establish idea/evaluation/plan/approval/delivery and funding separately where relevant. Add the external signal's source/date, applicability and operative/interim/future status as analyst context. Establish dependencies and what would make the priority move forward or become less important. Q002 supplies the actual operating/geographic context. No new compulsory question, statutory calculation or ranking score is introduced. SIG7-C07.

For practitioners, use a dated, applicable trigger to define the next specific task and evidence needed. For suppliers, explain how an offer addresses actual required work without treating a deadline as proof of a funded purchase. For JGA, distinguish market context from original employer commitments and preserve alternatives to recruitment.
