# R04 Decision authority and pathways

Desktop research and integrated review completed 29 October 2026. Original practitioner integration and final-use checks remain open.

## Decision this informs

Who turns a payroll need into an organisational choice, which decisions they control, and how evidence, conditions and delegated authority connect across a particular dated route.

## Desktop answer

Use a specific decision object, not a title or whole programme labelled as one approval. Need, sponsorship, evaluation, funding, supplier choice, contractual commitment, implementation, release and continuing service ownership can be combined, shared or unknown. No universal committee or CFO veto is established.

The evidence-led [readout](../R04-DESKTOP-READOUT.md) and [pathway](../R04-DECISION-PATHWAY.md) combine source-scoped governance definitions, rechecked buyer-study methods and six deliberately selected published employer cases. These are secondary examples, not original JGA contributors or a representative private-employer sample.

## What changed in the analytical design

- Twelve optional functions are separate from actions and decision rights. They are not twelve required posts or interview questions.
- Recommendation, advice, required prerequisite, binding condition and formal stop remain distinct; record who could clear/escalate a condition and what followed.
- The event record preserves decision scope/date, knowledge basis, missingness, actual disposition and subsequent implementation/result state.
- Approval, signature, functional signoff, go-live and benefit are not synonyms. Procurement records need their own applicable date/regime and wording.
- Legitimate incumbent retention, delegated changes and no-formal-approval cases remain available. Contradictory accounts and date conflicts are retained.

The [structured map](../R04-decision-map.json) contains the vocabulary, a blank event template and published-case provenance. Its original participant observation array is empty. Existing Q001 and Q006–Q013 support the analysis without changing the fourteen-question core or client checkpoint files.

## Evidence and limits

Canonical CLM-0253–0278 and source-specific memos support the distinctions. Government standards are normative and source-scoped; listed-company guidance is not a rule for all employers. Supplier/adviser stories are attributed accounts with undisclosed rights, not independent causal proof. Existing G2/HR.com/KPMG material does not supply an authority-prevalence finding. ICO's reviewed page is under update review; exact legal use needs a final check. GovS 002 and Purplebricks retain parsed reader extracts rather than downloaded raw originals. Council source bodies were inspected; local raw captures were unavailable. Selected public dates and the Rotherham signature discrepancy are preserved.
