FAQ

No such thing as a bad question.

01

What should I look for in payroll software as a first-time employer?

Look beyond price. The essentials are HMRC-recognised RTI submissions, automatic pension handling, and access to real payroll experts when something doesn't look right. Before your first payday, you'll also need to register as an employer with HMRC. With Payfit, specialists prepare and check every pay run before you approve it, so you're never relying on guesswork.

02

How much does payroll software for entrepreneurs actually cost?

Pricing typically scales with headcount, so a one or two-person company pays a fraction of what a growing team does. What decides the real value isn't the monthly fee, it's whether RTI submissions, pension contributions and CIPP-qualified support are included as standard, or charged as extras once you actually need them.

03

Should I run payroll myself or outsource it?

Running payroll yourself means carrying full responsibility for PAYE, National Insurance and pension accuracy with no safety net. Outsourcing to a bureau or accountant often means slower turnaround and less visibility. If you're weighing up payroll outsourcing against in-house payroll, Payfit sits between the two: specialists prepare and check your payroll, and you keep full visibility and the final approval.

04

How quickly can I get my first payroll run up and running?

Most founders worry this will eat a week they don't have. In practice, your dedicated implementation consultant sets everything up with you, and Payfit AI is available around the clock for questions in between, so your first pay run doesn't wait on your calendar.

05

What if I miss a deadline or make a mistake on my first payroll run?

Late or missing submissions can trigger an HMRC penalty from £100 a month for businesses with up to 9 employees. This is exactly why Payfit's specialists check each RTI submission before it's sent, rather than leaving you to catch errors after the fact.

06

Will Payfit set up my workplace pension automatically?

Yes. As soon as an employee earns over £10,000 a year (£192 a week) and meets the age criteria, they must be auto-enrolled into a workplace pension. Payfit handles the enrolment and the monthly contributions automatically, so it's one less threshold for you to track by hand.

07

When should I add HR features like leave, expenses or performance reviews?

As soon as you hire beyond yourself, spreadsheets start creating more admin than they save. The benefits of bringing HR processes together show up quickly: adding leave, expenses and performance management alongside payroll means every change reflects on payslips straight away, so you're not reconciling two systems every month-end.

08

Do I need payroll experience to use Payfit?

No. That's precisely the point of having UK payroll specialists prepare and check each pay run alongside Payfit AI answering your day-to-day questions. You approve with confidence, whether payroll is brand new to you or not.
