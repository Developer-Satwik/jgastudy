## Judgment

The twelve-function map holds. Its useful next refinement is to identify the organisation and level at which a right applies, then distinguish the mandate as described from the action actually taken. Software access, practical control, contractual delegation and retained employer responsibility can differ.

The sources do not establish a simple market rule such as small employers buy directly while large employers require committees. A founder-targeted product page does not establish every customer's buying process, and a global transformation case does not establish UK prevalence. Preserve both simple/delegated routes and cross-function cases without imposing either.

## What changes in the analysis

### Identify whose authority and at what level

For a relevant action, establish whether it belongs to the employer, group, region, local entity/country, shared-service organisation, supplier or adviser. Preserve the actual service component. A right concerning one country, implementation wave or operational task does not automatically authorise a group supplier choice or a contract variation.

This is a selective context field around existing decision scope and authority domain, not a new prescribed organisation chart. The organisation performing the work may differ from the body accepting the service or retaining an obligation. Do not ask for legal-entity names or confidential structures when broad descriptions suffice.

### Separate described mandate from practice

The first-pass map already separates knowledge bases. Where a mismatch appears, retain both the participant's description of who should decide and the action they actually observed. A provider effectively determining readiness is not by itself evidence of a formal delegation. Conversely, a formal role description does not prove its holder participated in that event.

This adds a useful comparison within a case, not a manufactured compliance judgment. Ask what happened, what the contributor directly knows, and whether any remit or practical arrangement changed. Preserve unknowns. No private contract or delegation letter is required.

### Keep operational permissions distinct

An application's admin role may allow a person to process or approve a pay run. That does not establish their corporate ability to buy the platform, change its contract or accept risk outside the application. The Payfit declaration is useful precisely because it supplies the operational meaning while leaving purchasing rights undisclosed.

Employer responsibility and buying authority also differ. The HMRC recheck preserves a UK PAYE boundary; it does not name an internal budget holder or signatory and is not organisation-specific legal advice. Final exact legal use should be rechecked.

## Suggested integration

1. Keep all twelve functions and the unchanged fourteen-question core.
2. Add selective holder context to the authority/event template: employer or external party; group/regional/entity/country level; relevant service component. Unknown/not applicable remain valid.
3. Where relevant, preserve mandate as described, action observed/reported and any discrepancy or change. Do not overwrite one with the other or score credibility by title.
4. Keep software permission separate from organisational delegation and contractual power. Existing Q001/Q006/Q009/Q013 can clarify this without an additional compulsory question.
5. Do not turn these market contrasts into recruitment quotas, a new SME respondent cohort, a buyer hierarchy or a provider-quality comparison. Use the unaided case first.

## Counterevidence and limits

A direct first-person decision account can remain useful even without a full committee map; missing signatories do not disqualify it. A larger programme can still have proportionate or local delegated decisions. Neither simplification nor centralisation is automatically correct. The inspected evidence cannot measure the frequency of either arrangement or show that a function's title predicts power.

The Chazey account reports improvement as well as problems, but this strand does not independently verify outcomes or adopt an economic claim. Its commercial perspective is retained. Current product marketing is likewise not a configured-access test or a guarantee of compliance/results.
