## What changes

The existing three financial views and nine domains remain useful. Add two selective conditions across them: whose resource/cash/benefit is being discussed, and which dated version of the choice supports the next decision. A positive aggregate case does not establish every participant's affordability; a prior approval does not establish unchanged current assumptions.

## Apply without expanding the instrument

For a material case, clarify the unit bearing the cost/work/risk and the unit receiving the benefit. Where different, record any actual allocation or funding arrangement and whose authority matters. Do not infer conflict, prescribe equal sharing or invent a transfer. This reinforces E02–E04 and E06–E08 and the existing R04 rights map. R5H-C06.

Record the appraisal date/version, option and service boundary, relevant subsequent changes, and the next reconsideration point. A dated estimate may remain useful; a revision is not automatically proof that the earlier decision was irrational. No automatic expiry, mandatory full reapproval or new question follows. This reinforces E01, E04–E05 and E09. R5H-C05.

For history, preserve what decision-makers could reasonably have known then and what later evidence added. Do not retrofit an eventual result into the earlier forecast or turn an incomplete historical baseline into a corporate valuation policy.
