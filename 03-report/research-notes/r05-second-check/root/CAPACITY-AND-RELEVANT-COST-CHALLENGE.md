# R05 challenge: usable capacity and the actual financial decision

29 October 2026. Internal analytical refinement, not employer financial advice or observed payroll evidence.

## What changes

The first pass separated paid time from cash release correctly. Add a selective check for whether the required skill is available in the required window, and what work is displaced. An annual balance of hours cannot establish that the relevant people are available for a migration, reconciliation, payment deadline or exception. This timing application is our analytical inference, not an empirical payroll result from the educational source.

ACCA's [relevant-cost concepts](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/student/exam-support-resources/fundamentals-exams-study-resources/f5/technical-articles/relevant-costs.html) distinguish decision-caused future cash effects, unchanged commitments/allocations and alternative use. Its labour example has different assumptions about existing available hours, additional people and displaced output. No classroom amount, staffing requirement or contribution formula is reused. R5R-C01–C02.

## Implication for the nine domains

Use E02, E04 and E06 together: relevant resource; availability/skill/window; actual extra cash if any; displaced obligation or alternative activity; owner and evidence. An existing salary does not prove new cash cost, free operational availability or an unimportant resource. An unavoidable payment still belongs in funding/lifecycle context even if it does not differ between the specific options under comparison.

Do not monetise missed payroll/control work using product-margin examples. Consequences may remain operational/nonmonetary unless a defensible actual financial basis exists. Do not assume all released minutes are interchangeable, a staffing reduction follows or a new hire is the answer. Obtain the unaided case and use this only where relevant under Q008–Q013; no new compulsory question.
