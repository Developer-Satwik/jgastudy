Project Services, collaborative by design
Enterprises are under pressure to accelerate digital transformation, modernise legacy systems and strengthen cybersecurity – all while competing for scarce cloud, data, cyber and digital skills.
Hays Project Services mitigates this pressure by meeting the growing demand for scalable, flexible and results driven technology project and resourcing services. Underpinned by our collaborative approach, we provide agile SOW solutions, ensuring you achieve your programme goals, deliver target benefits and maximise budgets.
Our in-house Project Management Office (PMO) assures consistent delivery of project objectives across all delivery models.
Delivery models: increase capacity, embed transparency
Explore our Project Services portfolio and find the best delivery model for your goals:
Resource Augmentation
A flexible model that is ideal for resolving capability gaps during surges in demand
Interim SOW
For agile engagement of technology and project leadership experts within a structured SOW and capped costs
Managed Resourcing
Provides the flexible, scalable and accountable SOW talent model enterprises need as priorities shift and workloads change
Managed Projects
An outcome-based model where our PMO owns project delivery, team management, and execution of defined deliverables
How Project Services can benefit your organisation
Increased delivery capacity
Augment internal teams or outsource project delivery to suit your priorities, timelines, and risk profile.
Rapid access to scarce skills
Mobilise specialist capability across cloud, data, cybersecurity, and digital disciplines.
Predictable cost and delivery control
Maintain clarity through structured statements of work, competitive rate cards, and PMO‑led governance.
Agility as priorities shift
Stay agile with a ready-to-deploy managed bench and “as a service” (AAS) squads.
Stronger internal capability
Build long‑term value through structured knowledge transfer embedded in every engagement.

Our perspective on structured SOW delivery and outcome‑focused models, informed by our real‑world delivery experience.
How structured SOW delivery models bring clarity, ownership and accountability
As project workloads increase, organisations need clearer structure, defined ownership and better visibility of progress.
In this video, Molly Ellis, Project Services Sales Director, outlines how statement‑of‑work delivery models clarify scope, responsibility and performance measurement – helping organisations maintain momentum, and access the right expertise at the right time.
Outcome‑based delivery: driving meaningful results
Outcome‑based delivery continues to grow, but misconceptions can restrict where and how these models are applied.
In this video, Molly Ellis addresses the most common myths and explains how outcome‑focused approaches improve visibility, structure and confidence across complex, multi‑workstream delivery environments.
What you can expect from Project Services
Our in-house Project Management Office (PMO) supports an end-to-end engagement approach, from initial discovery through to transition and knowledge transfer. Governance, reporting and documentation are embedded throughout to enable enterprises to retain intellectual capital and build long term internal capability.
Requirements analysis
We work with you to translate project objectives into clear delivery requirements, covering scope, priorities, constraints, risks, dependencies, and governance needs. This informs delivery planning, resourcing, and capability alignment across the project or programme.
Agile resourcing
Detailed analysis of work demand ensuring you procure the correct skill sets, tasks and specific effort required to maximise budgets.
SOW creation
We establish a statement of work that sets out agreed scope, deliverables, timelines, and governance, ensuring clarity and alignment before delivery begins.
Onboarding and offboarding
Enable productivity from day one through effective onboarding and ensure a seamless transition to “business as usual” via structured offboarding and knowledge transfer.
Progress reporting & reviews
Gain clear delivery visibility through ongoing SOW reporting and regular service reviews, supporting knowledge capture and the early identification of risks, mitigation, delivery performance, and financial position.
Delivery operations support
Our ongoing service delivery approach reduces internal overheads and lowers the overall cost of project delivery.
