# R05 second challenge: salary intelligence, delivery evidence and the actual commitment

29 October 2026. Internal desktop research. All six recruitment comparators and JGA assessed through selected original public material. Four new routes and five explicit reuses; no figures, quotations, participant evidence or new prompts. The previous recommendation counts are not reused.

## What changes after this check

R05's nine domains and three financial views remain useful. Three refinements make their application more precise:

1. **Match a benchmark to the claim.** Record the edition, underlying period, role/remit, location, pay basis and contributing population before using a salary figure. A starting salary, existing employee's pay, day rate and an output-based engagement are different cost inputs. None alone establishes total employer cost, available funding or realised benefit. Unknown relevant employment, recruitment, overlap or handover costs remain unknown; no standard loading factor is supplied.
2. **Identify the wider decision boundary.** A payroll proposal may be one part of an acquisition, new-country launch or organisation-wide programme. The payroll component's readiness, cost and outcome should remain visible alongside the wider decision. This does not automatically make payroll subordinate or prove that commercial teams always control the date.
3. **Specify what is actually purchased and accepted.** Recruiting expertise, augmenting a team and commissioning defined delivery are different commitments. Establish applicable scope, retained work, outputs/acceptance and handover before comparing their economics. A claim to provide capable people or a managed model is not evidence of available capacity or delivery in this employer's circumstances.

Apply these selectively under E01, E02, E04, E06, E08 and E09. They are not additional compulsory questions, a hiring recommendation, a demand forecast or a supplier scorecard.

## Prior prompt research: use it to challenge us

The [limited bridge](prior-study-bridge.json) selects S04-C, S05-C, S09-C and S10-C: four exact questions, each with eight saved answers across four tools and two repeats, giving 32 saved full-answer texts. Hashes and exact index text were checked. The full study's existing partial/pending source-list limits remain attached. No question was rerun and no recommendation total was transferred.

| Constructed question | Useful analytical contrast | Anchoring to exclude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S04-C: payroll transformation director | What relevant evidence separates general recruitment claims from experience with the proposed operations/systems/change remit? | The prompt already specifies a director and explicitly asks for evidence. It cannot establish that a director is needed or that employers usually request this proof. |
| S05-C: post-acquisition group payroll director | Identify the actual integration option, wider business boundary and assignment-level experience. | The permanent leadership appointment and integration framing are researcher choices, not observed employer priorities. |
| S09-C: provider-transition head of payroll | Distinguish a public job/service declaration from demonstrated comparable delivery or vendor-governance experience. | A transition and Manchester-based hire are assumed. Advertisements and generated claims are not completed placements or available expertise. |
| S10-C: interim cover and succession | Examine overlap, exact engagement scope, realistic handover and relevant cost/time assumptions. | Six months, director-level cover and a permanent search are written into the question. No universal handover duration, engagement or tax-status rule follows. |

All four C questions ask about evidence or verification. Repeated due-diligence advice in the answers is therefore partly elicited by the prompt design. It must not be presented as independent corroboration of buyer priorities. Generated cost, salary, tax-status, candidate-availability and supplier-outcome claims remain unadopted. Obtain the practitioner's unaided recent case first, then use relevant optional clarification under the existing fourteen core questions.

## Implication for the eventual report

The commercial value lies in helping a reader identify the **evidence that fits their actual commitment**, including the boundary between a payroll component and the wider programme. It does not lie in publishing a generic recruiter list or attaching a salary guide to a financial forecast. Preserve the useful contrast between a public proposition, actual assignment evidence, the employer's decision and observed delivery/benefit.
