# R08 second challenge: what the account supports and what remains contested

29October2026. One canonical methodological reuse, freshly rechecked. Four bounded guidance/application records. No new payroll source dataset, original contributor, score or validated framework effect.

## The missing qualification

The six-view draft carefully separates requested, supplied, accepted and believed influential evidence. It should make the next distinction equally usable: a decision-maker accepting a piece of evidence is not independent confirmation that every claim it makes is accurate or applicable. A contributor's report, a supplier declaration, an observed event and the researcher's interpretation need their own scope and knowledge basis.

The [QQE guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/quality-in-qualitative-evaluation-qqe-html) asks whose appraisal is reported, its basis, document origins/status and how conclusions were reached. It also allows divergent appraisals, alternative explanations and negative cases. Its indicators are aids to informed judgement, rather than a compulsory checklist. R8R-C01–C02.

## Selective application to R08

For a claim that materially changes the interpretation, retain its source, task/object, date/version where known and the contributor's direct knowledge or other basis. State what the evidence supports and what it does not. If another source corroborates it, establish the source's actual independence rather than count repeating pages or model recommendations. No external validation is invented from acceptance or a citation. A statement that lacks corroboration is not automatically false; the appropriate test depends on the claim. R8R-C03.

Where accounts differ, first examine whether they refer to the same component, period and criterion. A platform can be live while a separate task remains unavailable; a programme owner and user may assess different outcomes. If a relevant disagreement remains, preserve it as unresolved with alternative explanations instead of manufacturing consensus. This is an illustrative distinction, not a collected dispute. R8R-C04.

The draft writing pattern should therefore say “the account reports” or “the available evidence supports,” as appropriate, rather than automatically “the account establishes.” An optional blank evidence-qualification note behind the canvas is sufficient. It does not require a new question, a document demand, another interview, a numerical confidence rating or a larger cohort.

## What this does not establish

The guidance does not certify the framework, prove practitioner usefulness or rank a statement's credibility solely by source type. Nor should the framework become so cautious that a scoped partial brief has no value. Keep the known account, basis, boundary and justified next step concise, and preserve not known/not measured/not applicable/not disclosed.
