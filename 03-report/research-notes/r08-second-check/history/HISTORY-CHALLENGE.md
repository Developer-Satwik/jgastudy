# R08 historical challenge: a decision then and an explanation later

29 October 2026. Targeted internal desktop challenge. Three canonical reuses, zero new routes, four bounded claim/design records. No new employer, practitioner, numerical estimate or later outcome.

## Result

The six-view framework is largely confirmed. It already separates decisions from delivery/results and allows retention, component change, postponement and a partial brief. History does not justify a seventh view or a more complicated instrument.

One useful precision remains: **when explaining an earlier decision, separate what was known and feasible at that event from what a later account says with hindsight.** V02's “at this time” could otherwise be read as the current research date, while V03–V05 describe an older event. The source/report date, selected event date and the contributor's retrospective knowledge should not be silently substituted for each other. H8S-C03.

## One continuing programme is not one fixed decision

The retained [LFB2021 authorisation notes](https://www.london.gov.uk/decisions/dmfd106-replacement-human-resources-and-payroll-solution) describe options and receiving/timetable constraints at that time. Fresh full-page access now returns403, so this is an explicit reuse of previously inspected selected original-reader notes, not a claimed new original. The [LFB2025 reset report](https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/media/jrqngpy0/lfc-25-098-replacement-hr-and-payroll-system-novemberr-2025-update-signed.pdf), PDF2–4, narrates earlier procurement, sponsor changes, release options and later constraints in the same programme. H8S-C01.

The later report itself is partly retrospective. It explains a no-go-live decision using data, assurance, risk and workaround considerations, then describes reset measures and alternatives within the remaining window. It does not independently prove every contemporary belief, the optimality of the earlier option, supplier fault or the outcome after a future go-live date. The2021/2025 documents are different events/perspectives in one employer history, not separate market observations.

## A contrasting audit account does not provide a universal threshold

The retained [Scottish Government annual audit](https://audit.scot/uploads/2025-10/aar_2425_scottish_government.pdf), PDF/printed43 and46, describes migration signoff, conditional backing and final go-live approval. The final meeting recorded known issues and assurances; the auditor's later appraisal comments on particular processes. Those are different claims and perspectives. H8S-C02.

Set beside LFB, the accounts show that outstanding issues can feature in different reported release decisions. They are not matched risks, comparable severity measures or a test of which employer made the right choice. The source-specific judgement/conditions must remain attached to each event. No readiness percentage, minimum parallel-run rule, numerical risk threshold, current2026assurance or payroll-only outcome is transferred. H8S-C04.

## Practical refinement under the existing canvas

Where it matters, describe:

- the selected event and decision object;
- the source and perspective of the explanation, including whether it is retrospective;
- what is actually established about knowledge, options or authority at that event;
- later material information as a separate event or explanation, leaving unestablished details unknown.

This is a clarification of existing scope/date/knowledge fields under Q006–Q013 and V02–V05. It is not four new compulsory questions, a demand for complete minutes or a judgment that a person ought to have known something. If no contemporaneous evidence is available, record the account as retrospective rather than treating it as false or complete. No later outcome is imputed from an elapsed target.

## Suggested constructed challenge, not executed here

Supply a hypothetical older event where a constrained continuation was selected. Then supply a later account describing a newly available alternative or revised explanation, without proof that it was available earlier. The framework should preserve both dated records, avoid rewriting the earlier feasible option set and avoid declaring the old decision either optimal or irrational. It should still allow a current next decision when supported. This is a proposed internal test only, not an actual case or a passed framework validation.
