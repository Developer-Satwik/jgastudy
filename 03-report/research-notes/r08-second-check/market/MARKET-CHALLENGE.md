# R08 second challenge: accepted work, recurring operation and retained duties

29October2026. Targeted internal market/service challenge. Three canonical primary routes re-inspected, no new route or independent dataset. Four bounded declaration/design records. No account, product trial, payroll execution, private contract or practitioner observation.

## What this adds to the compact canvas

R08 already distinguishes action-specific authority and decision, commitment, implementation and benefit. The market lens finds one useful clarification: **an operating approval or accepted handoff has its own task/run/period; it is not automatically the payroll investment decision or proof of continuing operation.** This belongs as a selective explanation under the existing views, not a seventh view or extra questionnaire.

There can be several different objects called “approval”: approval to change a provider, acceptance of reports for a particular payroll, and authority to make a payment. A provider taking a task does not by itself establish that all retained work or employer responsibilities have moved. Keeping these distinctions visible makes the next evidence need more concrete without manufacturing a new project.

## Selective framework refinement

Under V03/V05, ask the analyst to retain whether the supplied approval/acceptance concerns a change decision, an operational run/task or a payment authority, where that distinction affects the explanation. Under V04/V06, keep the known remaining work, continuing responsibilities and next actual evidence need. Use the contributor's knowledge and preserve unknown or withheld details.

This confirms and sharpens the first-pass action/object/date rule. It does not require every participant to supply period records, a complete RACI, confidential contract or proof about every future run. It also does not automatically create a new investment case or an obligation to keep monitoring forever. An account that does not establish a further action may remain a useful partial brief.
