Payroll Capabilities

Our Payroll division is a genuinely dedicated payroll consultancy, which offers an unrivalled service. Delivering both interim and permanent assignments, we work across a variety of levels, from Payroll Clerks and Administrators through to Payroll Managers, Global Payroll Implementation Managers, Global Payroll Directors and Auto Enrolment Specialists. We have the capability to source talent across the UK and internationally.

Recent placements include:

  • Global Head of Payroll — FX Trading
  • Payroll Manager — Construction
  • Payroll Administrator — Accountancy
  • Interim Payroll Ofﬁcer — Retail Bank
  • Interim Head of Payroll — Asset Management
  • Head of Payroll and Beneﬁts— Insurance
  • Global Head of Payroll — Real Estate
