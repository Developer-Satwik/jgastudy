# R08 second challenge: evidence must match the decision object

29 October2026. Eight canonical source reuses cover the six comparators and JGA. No new source route, original practitioner, market figure or model rerun. Five bounded claim/design candidates.

## Result

The six-view canvas survives this lens. It already distinguishes need from hiring, authority from seniority, approval from delivery and perceived influence from causal proof. The useful refinement is narrower: **make clear what specific object and claim each piece of evidence supports.** A firm's service description, an old placement, a proposed assessment, a named candidate's verified fit, acceptance/start and the later payroll result are different evidence objects.

Where this distinction matters, V04 can record the actual work/remit/time/object, who checked the supplied evidence if known, and what remains unverified or introduced as an assumption. V02 and V05 can then avoid treating general firm credibility as current task-level readiness or completed engagement. This is a selective aid, not an additional compulsory question, candidate-data collection demand or a new scoring system. C8S-C05.

## Constructed challenge for parent integration

Supply a hypothetical brief in which a payroll leader has accepted a recruiter as worth discussing based on a firm case and a generic assessment description. No named candidate, current assessment, candidate agreement/start, actual contract or payroll result is supplied. The canvas should retain the supplied firm-level material and its source, keep the specific person/engagement/result unknown, and permit a relevant evidence need without declaring the candidate ready or the remedy successful. If an answer adds a city, employer-size band or selection weight, mark it as an introduced assumption rather than a case fact.

This is an unexecuted challenge design for the parent to apply to the integrated draft, not a completed practitioner scenario or validation result.
