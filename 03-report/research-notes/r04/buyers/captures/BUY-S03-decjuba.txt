0008: Background
0009: Decjuba is a leading fashion retailer with over 150 stores and a workforce of approximately 1,800 employees, scaling up to 2,000 during peak seasons. Claire Nagle, Payroll Manager, has been with the business for over four years and led the transition to a modern time and attendance system. With a history of using various platforms across retail brands, Claire brought deep experience and insight into the selection process.
0010: The Challenge
0011: Decjuba was previously using a legacy system that had reached end-of-life and was no longer supported. The business needed a modern, scalable solution that could handle the complexities of the General Retail Industry Award, frequent legislative changes, and the operational demands of a multi-site retail business.
0012: Claire and her team sought a system that:
0013: Was easy for store managers to use
0014: Delivered accurate pay outcomes
0015: Supported complex rostering
0016: Enabled proactive compliance management
0017: With increasing scrutiny around wage compliance and legislation such as wage theft laws, Decjuba needed a solution that could help prevent issues before they occurred.
0018: The Solution
0019: After a thorough tender process, Decjuba selected OAHI based on Claire’s prior experience with the platform and its proven reliability in retail environments. Key reasons for choosing OAHI included:
0020: Trust in the product
0021: from previous implementations
0022: Commercial viability
0023: and cost-effectiveness
0024: Strong implementation support
0025: and technical expertise

0027: Claire appreciated that OAHI was not only reliable but also backed by a team that understood retail deeply. She shared:
0028: "OAHI has tools that analyse our data and help us identify issues before they occur. That’s critical for compliance."
0029: OAHI’s tools allowed Decjuba to modernise its payroll operations, build award-compliant work rules, and streamline rostering and budgeting processes. Claire also highlighted the accuracy of OAHI’s forecasting tools, which helped the business manage wage budgets with confidence.
