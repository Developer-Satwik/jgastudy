0002: Payroll at the tipping point: The case for C-suite elevation
0003: Payroll often accounts for 40–60% of operating expenses, yet many organizations have not modernized how it is governed or led.
0004: This report examines why payroll has reached a tipping point and why stronger executive ownership is now required.
0005: Download the full report
0006: Download PDF
0007: Share
0008: New global research from KPMG and UKG, based on a survey of 319 senior leaders, shows that payroll remains fragmented, under‑resourced, and vulnerable to financial leakage, compliance risk, and operational breakdowns. While most organizations report having a global payroll strategy, few have achieved the standardization, controls, and insight required to operate payroll at enterprise scale.
0009: The case for elevating payroll leadership
0010: The research points to a clear conclusion. The current operating model is insufficient for the scale, risk, and complexity payroll now carries.
0011: A future‑ready approach includes:
0012: Elevating payroll to a
0013: C‑suite‑level function
0014: with clear ownership and authority.
0015: Strengthening governance and internal controls across regions and vendors.
0016: Standardizing processes as a prerequisite for AI and automation.
0017: Expanding measurement beyond operational metrics to support predictive insight and enterprise decision‑making.
0018: Many organizations may pursue this evolution through phased leadership models, including the emergence of a
0019: Chief Payroll Officer
0020: role.
