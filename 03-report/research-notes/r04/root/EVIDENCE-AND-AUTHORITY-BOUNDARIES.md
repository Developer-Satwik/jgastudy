# R04: what a role or decision record actually establishes

29 October 2026. Internal desktop analysis. Two public original guidance pages plus the existing qualitative case design. No organisational legal advice, procurement instruction or new practitioner observation.

## Judgment

The unit of analysis is a specific decision and its scope/date, not a person's title or a whole programme treated as one approval. A function may recommend, advise, fund, impose a condition, approve, sign, release or own continuing results. These actions can sit with different people, be combined, occur in parallel or change over time.

Do not infer decision rights from involvement, attendance, a senior title, supplier access or the fact that an issue could delay delivery. A practical dependency, professional advice and formal power to stop are different things. Establish what the participant directly did or observed and what remains their understanding.

## Adviser versus accountable organisational decision

ICO's DPO page describes advice, monitoring, access to senior decision-makers and retained controller/processor responsibility. This is a useful example of why expert involvement and ultimate organisational responsibility differ. It does not identify a payroll procurement approver or establish a universal absence/presence of veto authority. The page is explicitly under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act; exact legal use requires a fresh final-use check. The report does not reproduce appointment/compliance rules as organisation-specific advice. [Original](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/guide-to-accountability-and-governance/data-protection-officers/), Tasks; Support/access; Is the DPO responsible for compliance?

For the actual case, ask whether the role advised, identified a requirement, formally conditioned approval or had delegated stop authority, and how that is known. Do not recommend bypassing an unresolved requirement or treating a professional opinion as automatically equivalent to funding approval.

## A milestone's meaning depends on the applicable record and regime

Cabinet Office guidance updated 17 August 2026 distinguishes an intention-to-contract award notice under the Procurement Act from a later contract-details notice. It also explains that the same award-notice label had a different function under earlier legislation. This is a record-interpretation warning: verify the actual procurement's date, regime, source wording and status. Do not transplant this sequence into earlier/differently governed cases or ordinary private-employer decisions. No statutory timetable is extracted as a payroll decision benchmark. [Original](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-contract-award-notices-and-standstill-html), paragraphs 1–7 and 52.

An announced selection, authority to award, an entered contract, budget permission, acceptance of a work product and permission to go live can therefore require separate records. The actual case determines which occurred. A published notice generally cannot establish every internal approver, informal influence or benefit outcome.

## Evidence-reading questions for the pathway

| Evidence type | What it may support, if explicit | What must not be supplied by inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delegation/terms of reference | Defined responsibility, authority scope, limits and escalation. | That every practical decision followed it or that a title carries the same rights elsewhere. |
| Dated recommendation or committee resolution | What was proposed/decided, by whom or which body, and stated conditions. | Contract signature, completed delivery or measured benefit unless recorded. |
| Contract/procurement record | Actual scope, entered agreement or published award stage as the record/regime defines it. | The whole internal buying committee, informal influence or operating readiness. |
| Assurance/audit account | Scope, assessed conditions, recommendations and dated assurance. | Budget, signature or go-live authority merely because a reviewer participated. |
| Employer/supplier case narrative | Attributed involvement, rationale and reported chronology. | Independent verification, unnamed signatories, market prevalence or causal certainty. |
| Practitioner contribution | First-hand action/experience and the participant's understanding, labelled separately. | Confidential formal rights, other people's motives or documentary confirmation not actually supplied. |

These are proposed reading rules, not new evidence of a particular organisation. Contradictions should be retained: a formal mandate, a person's recollection and observed practice need not be identical. The research can record functions and broad limits without requesting names, private budgets or confidential delegation documents.

## Minimum analytical record

Record the decision object, approximate date/as-of point, route and actual disposition. For each relevant function, record its action, claimed authority, scope/condition, basis of knowledge and outcome. Keep observed action, formal authority as described, participant interpretation and unknown separate. No credibility score is manufactured from job title.

Funding permission, option/vendor selection, contractual commitment, configuration/release and continuing service ownership can be distinct decisions around one case. Do not impose all of them as mandatory gates. Existing Q001/Q006–Q013 cover direct involvement, decision, authority, evidence and what followed; use relevant probes without expanding the fourteen-question core.
