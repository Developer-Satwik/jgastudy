# R06 second challenge: the talent decision, the other party and continuing capability

29 October 2026. Internal desktop review. Two new primary routes and five canonical reuses cover all six recruitment comparators and JGA. Four bounded claim/design records. No employer interview, candidate approach, live vacancy submission, price estimate or new prompt.

## What changes

R06 already separates option, dated disposition, implementation and benefit. The talent lens adds two useful checks inside that structure:

1. **The employer's decision is not the other party's agreement or arrival.** Approving a search or making an offer does not establish that a suitable person accepts, contracts, starts or remains available. A later pause may concern that specific commitment, while the underlying payroll need persists. Establish the actual event and directly known reason rather than infer a hiring freeze, rejection or loss of interest.
2. **Ending a temporary engagement is not the same as retaining useful capability.** Identify who receives knowledge and ongoing ownership, what handover actually occurred, and whether the remaining team can use it. A document or planned meeting is not proof of effective transfer, and hiring a permanent successor is not the only possible response.

These are optional analytical applications, not new interview questions, universal recruiting stages or a guarantee that a talent solution resolves a payroll constraint. They preserve retention, development, revised remit, process improvement and adequate existing-team routes. C6S-C04.

Prompt, full-text hash, date/status, index text and coverage were checked. No question was rerun and no recommendation count was reused. | Constructed question | Useful analytical contrast | What its answers cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S07-D: replacement before departure | Separate employer search/offer, candidate start and realistic handover. | The prompt already assumes resignation and a permanent replacement before departure. It cannot show a real buyer preferred hiring over retention/revised remit, or that the deadline is feasible. |
| S09-A: Manchester provider-transition leader | A provider transition and the leadership engagement are different objects with different decisions. | The provider change, permanent Head of Payroll and location/work pattern are preselected. Output cannot establish the need to switch or hire, actual approval, or the reason a proposal stalled. |
| S10-C: interim/permanent overlap | Availability, ongoing coverage and receiving-team handover are separate conditions worth clarifying if relevant. | Six months, director level and parallel permanent search are written into the question. Its explicit request for verification elicits due-diligence advice, not independent buyer priorities, a standard duration or proof of available talent. |

All three prompts begin after a researcher has chosen a staffing response. They do not compare an actual buyer's unprompted retention, process, development or no-change alternatives. Generated handover plans, tax-status guidance, weighted scorecards, candidate claims and commercial terms remain unadopted. The semantic check read the exact prompts and the three ChatGPT repeat-1 answers to identify these elicited assumptions; the remaining selected texts were checked for integrity/coverage, not freshly validated as advice.

## Implication for R06

For a specific talent-related case, preserve the employer's commitment, the relevant counterparty's acceptance/start and what capability/ownership remained afterwards. Clarify whose action or agreement affected progress, rather than assume the payroll buyer alone controlled the outcome. Establish actual handover and next ownership where relevant; a planned handover is not evidence of effective transfer.

This strengthens the existing option/disposition/delivery/benefit and possible-response/actual-action/effect boundaries. It does not add a mandatory stage, a candidate interview, a required permanent successor, a staffing ratio or another questionnaire. A stop, revised remit, existing team or retention may remain the right response. Original permissioned practitioner cases must establish the actual explanation and outcome.

No new forecast, salary/rate, behavioural prevalence, supplier ranking or validated causal remedy is supplied. Nothing was sent to Nick or anyone else.
