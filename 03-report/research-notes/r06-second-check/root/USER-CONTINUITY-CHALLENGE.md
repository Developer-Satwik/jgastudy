# R06 second challenge: completion for whom?

29 October 2026. Internal analytical refinement. One new primary guidance route and one canonical employer route re-read. No end-user fieldwork, product trial or legal advice.

## What changes

Ending a supplier relationship, starting a portal and completing a contract are different from usable continuity for affected people. The first pass separated delivery from benefit; add a selective check for the exact user, task, channel and historical evidence that remain usable when one service changes or stops. An export or new login does not by itself establish this.

[GDS retirement guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/retiring-your-service) distinguishes a need ending from another service meeting it. It discusses communication, API changes, assisted channels and continued information protection. This is public-service guidance, not a mandatory private-employer workflow or payroll observation. Its displayed 2025 update concerns a contact email, not an established substantive policy change. Government redirect or SSL deadlines are not transferred. R6R-C01.

The reused West Sussex 13 October 2026 self-service notice simultaneously describes current payslip access, older payslips absent from Oracle and support/query routes. It instructs staff to retain copies, but does not establish that copies were obtained, everyone could access the service, an alternative archive existed or harm occurred. R6R-C02.

## Apply proportionately

Where material, identify the affected user or cohort and task: current operational access, historical evidence genuinely needed, receiving service/help owner, and support appropriate to the channel. Payroll administrators, workers, integration users and former employees can have different needs. Do not assume every case involves every group or extend this study to interview all users. The fourteen-question core is unchanged; relevant clarification sits under Q012/Q013 after the unaided case.

Public declarations describe the declared arrangement, not observed experience. Keep service-owner completion, contractual exit, record availability, practical access and service outcome separate. Neither archiving everything indefinitely nor destroying all records after exit follows. Actual retention, permission and contractual conditions require source-specific review. R6R-C03.
