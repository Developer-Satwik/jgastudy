Locators executive summary; paragraphs1.3,1.5,1.7 and2.3.
The record authorises the Commissioner to commit spending on developing/rolling out a replacementHR/payroll solution. It describes historical continuation/workarounds due to investment requirements, a maturing/deprecated product and a transition extension. Collaborative alternatives were considered but receiving capacity, timetable and affordability were stated constraints. A replacement procurement was preferred. These are the author's stated reasons/forecast options at that time, not independently verified future outcomes or a universal rule.
No monetary amounts, standard contract duration, SaaS saving or supplier risk assessment is adopted.
