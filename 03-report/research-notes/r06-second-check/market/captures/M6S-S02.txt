IRIS. Look forward 
Introduction 
This guide has been created to support our customers as they prepare for the End-of-Life (EoL) 
of Earnie Payroll (including IRIS Payroll Basics) software. As of the beginning of the 2026/2027 tax 
year (6th April 2026), you will no longer be able to process payroll in Earnie (including Payroll 
Basics). As long as you are on v24.30 or later, you will continue to access Earnie, for compliance 
and reference purposes, even where your license has expired. You will not be able to run 
payroll unless with a valid licence.  
Why this change is happening 
As part of IRIS Software Group’s ongoing commitment to innovation and delivering the best 
payroll solutions, we are consolidating our offerings to focus on modern, cloud ready platforms 
that support current and future business needs. 
What you need to know 
End of payroll processing 
• The last payroll run that is compliant and supported will be up to 5th April 2026 
• After 5th April 2026, you will no longer be able to process payroll 
• Whilst users will be able to access the product after the licence expiry date, users cannot 
run a payroll without a valid licence. 
Producing P60s post-EoL 
• For customers closing Tax Year 2025-2026 in Earnie (including IRIS Payroll Basics), you 
will need to install the final EoL version due in March 2026, in order to produce 2026 
P60s. 
Read-only access 
From 6th April 2026, Earnie (including IRIS Payroll Basics) will remain available but non-compliant, 
this means you will still be able to access the system after your licence has expired  but the 
subsequent tax years will not be supported for payroll compliance purposes.  
 
You will be able to: 
• View historical payslips and employee records 
• Access year end documents (e.g. P60’s) 
• Export reports and data

Risk disclaimer 
IRIS will cease to monitor the application for security and vulnerabilities from the 5th of April 
2026, and the application will likely become more vulnerable shortly thereafter and eventually 
may become in-operable. Customers and Users who continue to host and access the software, 
do so at their own risk and IRIS accepts no responsibility or liability for the software .  
 
As a result of this, our recommendation is that any information required for legislative purposes 
is downloaded and be stored securely elsewhere.   
Your responsibilities before 5th April 2026 
To ensure compliance and continuity, we recommend taking the following actions before the 
End-of-Life date: 
Export critical reports and data 
• P60s, P11s, RTI history (FPS/EPS) 
• Employee payment history 
• Pension reports, submissions and enrolment status 
• Payroll Journals if the costing module is activated and audit trails  
Complete final submissions 
• Ensure all final payroll adjustments (bonuses, terminations, holiday payouts, etc) are 
completed 
• As with a normal Payroll Year End please ensure that all RTI Submissions for tax year 
2025/2026 are submitted by 5th April 2026 
Prepare for transition 
If you are moving to another IRIS solution, such as Staffology Payroll which is our cloud-based 
payroll software that has enhanced security, improved performance and continued support then 
please co-ordinate with your Account Manager for migration and onboarding support.  
Frequently asked questions 
Will I still be able to access Earnie after April 2026? 
Yes. You will retain read-only access to historical data after 5th April 2026 
Can I still generate reports or Year End documents? 
Yes. You can run and export reports.
