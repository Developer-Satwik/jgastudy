Any Questions?
We’re Always Here to Help
When will BrightPay desktop reach end-of-life?
BrightPay desktop reached end-of-life at the beginning of the 2026/27 tax year (April 6, 2026). From this date, the desktop version will no longer receive updates, support, or legislative compliance.
You can read our FAQs about the move here .
Why is BrightPay moving fully to the cloud?
BrightPay is transitioning to the cloud to ensure innovation, efficiency, and future readiness. The move allows BrightPay to focus on delivering modern, scalable, and secure payroll solutions that meet evolving compliance requirements and business demands. Cloud technology offers enhanced functionality, automation, and flexibility that desktop systems cannot match.
How do I check my BrightPay desktop licence?
You can access your BrightPay desktop license details in your 2025-26 payroll software by clicking the blue triangle in the top-right corner of the main company screen.
What are the benefits of running payroll in the cloud?
Running payroll in the cloud offers several benefits, including:
Flexibility: Access payroll data from anywhere with an internet connection.
Enhanced security: Data is securely hosted on Microsoft Azure servers with automatic backups, reducing risks of data loss or breaches.
Real-time collaboration: Multiple users can work on the same payroll file simultaneously, improving teamwork and reducing errors.
Automatic updates: Always stay compliant with the latest payroll legislation without manual upgrades.
Does BrightPay Cloud have all the features of the desktop version?
BrightPay Cloud is quickly reaching feature parity with the desktop version. While most features are already available, some functionalities are still in development and will be released in the near future.
Click here to see our feature comparison table.
Why start your transition from BrightPay desktop to BrightPay Cloud now?
BrightPay Desktop is now non-compliant with current UK legislation for the 2026/27 tax year, so it’s essential that you transition to BrightPay Cloud as soon as possible to continue processing payroll.
Can I book a migration slot with a BrightPay Cloud migration agent?
Yes, our team is available to help you with your migration. You can book a migration slot with us here .
*Please note that this slot is dedicated solely to supporting your data migration from BrightPay Desktop to BrightPay Cloud. Your organisation must be set up in BrightPay Cloud before this consultation. Once you book your migration slot using the link above, you will receive a confirmation email outlining the steps required to create your organisation.
How many employees can I have in BrightPay Cloud?
BrightPay’s Cloud is currently restricted to 300 employees (including leavers). If your employees’ number is around 300 or more, please complete this form immediately so we can discuss your options.
Is the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) module available on BrightPay Cloud?
We have developed a separate solution – BrightCIS which provides CIS functionality and compliance in the cloud.
