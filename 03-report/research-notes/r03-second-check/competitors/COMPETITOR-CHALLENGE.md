# R03 second challenge: expertise, project delivery and the earlier study

29 October 2026. Internal desktop analysis. No prompts rerun, clients contacted, products tested or prior-study/client/canonical files changed. This pass adds three bounded primary routes and five candidate claims, while reusing the previous six-firm inspection where sufficient.

## Judgment

R03's eight-path map holds, but needs a clearer engagement-mode overlay. An individual supplied to an employer-directed team, a consulting-led project with defined deliverables, enduring operational leadership and a provider running payroll are different arrangements. A combination can be useful, but the contract, retained authority, output acceptance and knowledge handback must be established in the actual case.

The current map's P08 title is limited to time-bound expertise. That can underrepresent a genuine enduring leadership need. Broaden the wording to include the appropriate duration and operating role, without presuming a hire or assigning decision authority from job title. Keep project and operating capacity visible together. Do not add a ninth path just to mirror another commercial label.

## Precise proposed R03 refinements

1. Broaden P08's time-bound title to permit enduring leadership/capability where the case supports it. Duration, task and authority remain separate descriptors. No assumed hiring demand.
2. Across P05/P07/P08, distinguish individual capacity/advice, employer-directed team, scoped consulting-led project and ongoing payroll-service execution. A firm can offer more than one mode; the actual engagement determines its obligations.
3. Add required work product, acceptance owner and handback to the existing output/ownership questions. This sharpens R03 result evidence without beginning R04's budget/approval study.
4. Distinguish programme/change capacity from the recurring operations and controls that continue during migration. One resource plan may need both; do not assume one senior hire personally performs every specialist task.
5. For acquisition/provider contexts, establish what actually needs changing and what must be retained. Consolidation, harmonisation and replacing all systems are possible choices, not synonyms for integration or guaranteed improvements.
6. Preserve adequate arrangements and development/process alternatives. A recruitment or consulting offer's existence does not prove it is needed, effective, affordable or available for the case.

Use the existing Q002/Q004/Q012/Q013 selective probes where relevant; no compulsory question or sample target is added. Actual authority and funding pathways remain R04, full investment economics R05, and actual participant experiences remain uncollected.
