Understanding SAP SuccessFactors HCM Product Release Cycles

Objective

After completing this lesson, you will be able to describe the product release process for SAP SuccessFactors HCM products.

SAP SuccessFactors HCM Release Cycle Overview

Releases in a public cloud system are essential to ensure that the software stays up to date with the latest features, security enhancements, and performance improvements, maintaining optimal functionality and security for users.

Since SAP SuccessFactors HCM is a public cloud solution, SAP proactively applies software updates and patches during defined maintenance windows, and customers are notified in advance of this information.

Knowing SAP SuccessFactors HCM products’ release dates is especially relevant during cutover activities, as no transport between Preview and Production tenants can occur during the five-week "freeze" period.

In the long run, these dates should not only be incorporated into IT yearly task calendars but also into HR yearly task/process calendars for:

  • Managing Change Management activities: Such as communication and enablement for the adoption of new capabilities.
  • Testing and Validation: Evaluating new features and deciding which to enable immediately versus later. Aligning system improvements with HR strategy and annual cycles (e.g., performance review periods, merit increases, onboarding seasons).
  • Risk Mitigation and Strategic Planning: Aligning product enhancements with other HR initiatives (e.g., talent development programs, compliance deadlines, etc.). Providing input for budgeting and resource allocation if consulting or training support is needed.

Access upcoming release dates and related resources via SAP SuccessFactors HCM Product Release & Road Map Information.

How to Prepare for SAP SuccessFactors HCM Releases

The following are recommended preparation activities to help you get familiar with upcoming SAP SuccessFactors HCM releases and identify the next steps for your organization:

  1. Register to attend the  Release Sessions. These webcasts deliver helpful answers to questions related to the latest iteration of the What’s New Viewer. (Note: Registration is needed to access the on-demand recordings & presentations).
  2. Review the  What's New Viewer in the SAP Help Portal to see changes to SAP SuccessFactors HCM products. Use filters to narrow results by your areas of interest, explore the available updates, and review the recommended adoption steps. You can also filter by "Release Version" to see updates based on when a change or delivery was introduced into the product.
  3. Familiarize yourself with known issues and required actions, including:
    1. Notable Fixed Issues: Resolutions to defects and customer-reported issues as part of the latest release.
    2. Known Issues: Navigate to the Issues section of the linked page. Review Knowledge Based Articles (KBAs) containing dynamic search links to the top-reported known issues at the back of the latest SAP SuccessFactors HCM product release.
    3. Innovation Alerts: Product-related changes that impact or require action for customers. This can include migrations or deprecations in features or functionality.
  4. Have a plan to adopt the latest innovations that can add value to your organization’s current and future processes or close existing gaps. Implementing SAP SuccessFactors HCM releases requires close coordination between HR (the business owners and process experts) and IT (the technical enablers). HR ensures that business needs, compliance, and user adoption are addressed, while IT ensures system stability, integration integrity, and security. Together, they make each release a structured cycle of planning, testing, adoption, and support, maximizing value while minimizing disruption.

For more information on how to build and structure your company’s release strategy and process, visit the SAP SuccessFactors: Build your Adoption program to maximize results event page. Here, you can access live and on-demand webcasts covering key programs that SAP SuccessFactors HCM customers should consider activating within their organizations to support their cloud technology and achieve best results.

Implementing a set of relevant business transformation programs will ensure that technology, people, and processes evolve together. This maximizes adoption, reduces resistance, aligns HR with business goals, and ultimately drives measurable ROI from SAP SuccessFactors HCM products.

What Tasks Do I Need to Perform?

During some release cycles, there will be mandatory tasks (or must-know tasks) that your administrators or configuration experts will need to perform for specific business processes.

To find the list of mandatory tasks associated to each release cycle:

  1. Go to the What's New Viewer (a section contained within the SAP SuccessFactors Release Information Help Portal).
  2. Use the filter named Action to filter by "Required". This will provide you with the list of mandatory tasks relevant to the latest release.

Conclusion

Bi-yearly product releases ensure that SAP SuccessFactors HCM systems receive the latest features and functionalities, enhancing performance and mitigating potential security risks. They provide access to the newest innovations and improvements, which boost productivity, efficiency, and overall user experience.

Implementing SAP SuccessFactors HCM product releases requires close collaboration and coordination between HR (the business owners and process experts) and IT (the technical enablers) teams.
