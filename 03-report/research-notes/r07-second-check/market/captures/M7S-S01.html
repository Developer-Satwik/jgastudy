  • Manage accounting and payroll together
  • Generate, manage and print payslips and P60s
  • Add as many employees as you need as your business grows, at no extra cost

Easy-to-use, HMRC-recognised payroll

Staying compliant with PAYE can be a tedious task. But with FreeAgent, you can effortlessly run monthly payroll and submit your Real Time Information (RTI) payroll directly to HMRC. Find out more in our 60-second video.

Play video

One software that does it all (and grows with your business)

Make life easier (and save on costs) with integrated payroll. FreeAgent will automatically calculate PAYE and National Insurance contributions for you. It also supports Employment Allowance claims, student loan deductions and handling statutory pay.

Growing your team? Effortlessly add new employees to your payroll whenever needed.

Manage payslips and P60s

Generate, view, and edit payslips via FreeAgent each month. Automatically send notifications to your employees to let them know when their payslips are ready.

Then, at the end of the tax year, use FreeAgent to generate P60s. Made a mistake? No biggie, you can easily make corrections to payslips from previous tax years.

FreeAgent automatically calculates pension contributions

Set up default pension contribution settings for each of your employees and FreeAgent will automatically calculate pension contributions when you're generating payslips.

Here's the lowdown on payroll in FreeAgent:

  • Generate and manage payslips and P60s that you can easily print or share
  • Stay compliant and submit RTI directly to HMRC
  • Easily switch your payroll to FreeAgent anytime
  • Automatically calculate employees’ pension contributions
  • Export payroll reports, including a monthly breakdown of payroll figures that have been sent to HMRC
  • Add and remove employees from your payroll
  • Handle student loan deductions and statutory pay
  • Claim the Employment Allowance
  • Enable HMRC notices - i.e. changes to your employees’ tax codes or student loans - to show and be managed within FreeAgent
  • Add notes and your company logo to your payslips
  • Automatically send notifications to employees when their payslips are ready
  • Make payroll corrections to payslips from previous tax years
  • Send final payroll submissions and close your PAYE scheme if needed
