# R07 second challenge: available capability and whose priority

29 October 2026. Three targeted supplier routes and one explicit canonical reuse; five bounded declaration/design records. No product trial, customer account, tariff estimate, employer observation or market ranking.

## What this lens changes

R07 already separates preference from organisational commitment. The market check adds a practical condition: **a feature can be included in an offer without being available, configured or used in the actual account and task; a supplier's roadmap is not an employer's delivered improvement.** A priority may concern making current capability usable rather than buying a new product. That is a possibility to establish in a case, not evidence that a particular customer has an unused feature.

The distinction also has an owner. A bureau's work across clients and a client's own payroll work need not be the same improvement, despite using a common label such as “automation.” Establish the actual scope, owner and beneficiary before attributing the priority or resources to an employer. Do not assume disagreement or expand the proposed practitioner population to suppliers.

## Apply selectively under the unchanged questions

Where an unaided priority concerns a feature, software update or workflow, use existing Q002/Q005 and recent-case probes to clarify only what matters:

1. What actual task and account/service scope does the improvement concern?
2. What is known about relevant entitlement, access, availability and setup/use?
3. Is the statement an already released capability, a future supplier plan, or an actual organisation's commitment?
4. Who must act, and whose work or outcome is expected to improve?

These are optional analytical checks, not four extra core questions or a sequence every case must pass. States can coexist across tasks. A contributor can know an improvement is important but not know the contractual or software details; preserve that limit. Do not require a confidential contract or infer missing functionality merely because access has not been tested. M7S-C04–C05.

For practitioners, the value is a clearer next action: a capability check, configuration, process change or a genuinely new option can have different implications. For suppliers, explain the actual work and conditions instead of equating feature interest with a purchase. For JGA, capability ownership may matter without proving the need for a hire.
