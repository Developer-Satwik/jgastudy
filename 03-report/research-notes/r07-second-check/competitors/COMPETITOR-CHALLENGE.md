# R07 second challenge: capability, scope and the chosen route

29 October 2026. Internal desktop review of all six recruitment comparators and JGA. Three new routes, four canonical reuses and six bounded claim/design records. No original practitioner contribution, quantified market estimate, live candidate approach or new model test.

## What this lens changes

A payroll priority should specify the work or capability needed before it is described as a vacancy, training purchase or technology project. Existing colleagues can develop or redistribute capability; a role can combine several functions; an external search can be one part of a response. These routes may coexist. None of the inspected material establishes that one is always best or that a particular employer has funded it. C7S-C01–C06.

This adds a selective **capability, route and actual remit** check under existing Q002/Q005/recent-case probes. It does not add a compulsory question. Preserve the original account and ask whose work/capability is changing, which tasks/territories it covers, who will perform it and what actual action/resources are known. Do not infer a shortage or prescribe a hire from a strategic-priority label.

## How to use the refinement

For a relevant priority, record the actual payroll task/capability and remit, then the chosen or still-open response. Distinguish organisational workforce, payroll population and payroll-team size; likewise distinguish headquarters, work location and jurisdictional responsibility. Identify current, developed, redistributed and externally obtained capability only when the account supports it. These are selective descriptions, not a staffing ratio or universal option checklist.

This works within R07's existing priority/resources/context record and R03–R06's route, authority, financial and outcome distinctions. It does not widen the proposed cohort or add fields that every contributor must answer. Sparse information remains unknown rather than replaced by a recruiter proposition or a generated scenario. Original permissioned cases are still needed to show what the organisation actually prioritised and did.
