Scope: government portfolio/programme/project expectations, not observed employer payroll priorities.
Sections3.3,5.3.3–5.3.7,7.4: the framework includes ongoing operational/improvement work. Portfolio choices consider existing work, dependencies and resource constraints, with periodic review. Monitoring distinguishes outcomes to date from future expectations. Replanning may follow capacity limits; development, retention and reassignment are possible resource responses.
Analytical application: where relevant to one payroll priority, clarify what it depends on, its sequence alongside ongoing work and its next review; do not infer low importance, absent funding or a mandatory new hire. No full portfolio document, monetary amount or private-employer rule is required.
