# R07 second challenge: priority beside other work and affected users

29 October 2026. Internal analytical challenge. One new normative government route and one canonical reuse. No original practitioner, numerical estimate or private-employer mandate.

## What the first pass needed to make clearer

Recording the status and resources of a single priority is useful, but can miss what else needs to happen first. Equally, a leader's desired outcome can be different from evidence about an affected user's task. Both are selective context questions, not reasons to expand the instrument.

The [project-delivery standard](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/library-products/government-functional-standard-govs-002-project-delivery/) considers ongoing work, dependencies, capacity and review. It permits development, retention and reassignment among resource responses. R7R-C01. These are government management expectations, not observed payroll choices.

The [digital standard](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/government-functional-standard-govs-005-digital/government-functional-standard-govs-005-digital-html), updated1October2026, connects performance/user evidence, ongoing improvement and service dependencies, including digital and non-digital work. R7R-C02. It does not show an employer adopted any method or achieved a benefit.

## Selective refinements

- If sequence or competing work is material, record what the priority depends on and its next review or decision. Do not infer that later work is unimportant, unfunded or rejected. A full portfolio, exact ranking or new compulsory question is unnecessary. R7R-C03.
- If another party's task or decision matters, identify the actual affected work, the contributor's knowledge and the condition that party controls. Do not invent conflict or claim user evidence that was not collected. No additional interviews or stakeholder census is proposed. R7R-C04.

These conditions supplement the existing Q005 and recent-case routes. They do not validate a score, forecast, outcome or prescribed recruitment response. The blank priority/context/comparison records remain blank.
