## The strongest report contribution

The existing research supports a disciplined explanation of a particular payroll decision: what work/outcome matters, which ways were feasible, who could act, what evidence/resources/delivery conditions applied, what is actually known about the result, and what the next useful decision is. It does not establish a formula for securing investment or show that the proposed framework improves approval rates.

The broad themes are already covered by other research. A traceable case-to-decision explanation is JGA's value hypothesis to test, not a demonstrated unique market position. FND-001–006. The original practitioner evidence still needs to establish the actual account and its interpretation; the current findings are internal research-design findings, not those contributors' results.

## What must remain a hypothesis

1. **Framework usefulness.** We have not tested whether practitioners understand/use the six views or whether a shorter version would work better. A future comprehension check may be helpful within agreed access and scope; no such testing is reported now.
2. **Evidence that changed a decision.** Q010 asks about evidence requested. Q011 asks about evidence the contributor believes mattered. Requested, produced, accepted, attributed influence and independently established causal effect are different claims. We have no original cases proving a particular evidence package caused approval.
3. **Action effectiveness.** A plausible response is different from the action actually adopted and its observed result. No source-supported possible remedy becomes a promise to remove a barrier.
4. **Commercial differentiation.** The case-to-decision contribution may be valuable, but it is not evidence of paid demand. The Table still needs its specific buyer use, substitutes, funding/approval and credible next-step evidence.
5. **Transferability.** A context mechanism or small case contrast is not a size/sector/country priority estimate. Keep achieved/missing coverage, contrary cases and alternatives visible; do not turn the proposed 6–10 purposive contributors into subgroup percentages.

These hypotheses are explicitly recorded in the structured proposal. Original-practitioner and framework-use arrays remain empty.

## Collisions to resolve in integration

**Programme and source double counting.** Synergy, LFB, Scottish Government and West Sussex recur because different documents or modules reveal different components/events. They do not become a new employer every time cited. Likewise a press release, report, method page and chart may represent one dataset. Use canonical route and case identities; do not count six views as six independent confirmations.

**One progress label hiding different facts.** Distinguish a proposal decision, contractual commitment, release permission, actual operation and assessed outcome. The same organisation can retain software, change the commercial route, postpone a component and continue operations. Normalisation requires an explicit object/date, not a whole-project maturity label. CLM-0261–0262, 0365, 0371, 0408.

**Value, money and capacity double counting.** Whole-life resources, future incremental cash and funding/timing are different views of an option; overlapping costs are not added again because they appear in two views. Time released does not automatically release cash; paid time does not establish usable critical-window capacity. Wage principal is not automatically a transformation cost. CLM-0298, 0302, 0328, 0334, 0343.

**Whose priority and whose next action.** Personal study intent, employer support, provider roadmap and affected-user value are not interchangeable. Establish the relevant task/account/remit, direct knowledge and controlling party. Do not invent conflict when responsibilities differ. CLM-0391, 0408, 0417–0418, 0428.

**Historical repetition and future dates.** Repeated themes are not matched employer progress; a planned date is not an observed result. Source access is not new fieldwork. Keep conditional policy/current-service statements dated and scoped; recheck applicable detail before publication. CLM-0371, 0405, 0410, 0413.

**Predetermined response.** The earlier AI study's prompts often select a hire, role or geographic remit before asking a question. Use them to challenge researcher assumptions and locate leads, not as original buyer demand, supplier performance or evidence a specific route was necessary. CLM-0424.

**Charts creating false comparability.** Do not make a synthetic investment funnel, national priority chart or decision score from incompatible measures. CLM-0051/0052, 0167/0168, 0218 and 0392 remain held and are excluded as positive framework support. Preserve unknown, not measured, not applicable and not disclosed separately.

## Report story priorities

Keep Nick's two-part report and conclusion, “From Ambition to Investment.” Within the proposed outline, lead with the practical decision, not a long catalogue of technology trends:

- Part one can explain the payroll work/outcomes and why the gap mechanism matters before choosing a response. Show adequate service, retention and process/learning routes as well as unmet needs.
- Part two can explain the organisation's actual decision and evidence: authority, feasible alternatives, resource/funding/delivery conditions and dated result. Clarify that evidence requested is not necessarily evidence accepted or influential.
- The conclusion can give a compact framework and an example of the next supported decision/action. It must accommodate proceeding, revising, waiting, retaining and stopping without claiming to predict which will succeed.
- Use the deeper module matrices as analytical support, not a requirement for every contributor to answer every domain. Preserve the14-question version0.2 and its no-case/refusal/unknown routes.
- Prefer a labelled case comparison or original connected-view diagram over invented percentages. A few cleared examples should demonstrate the distinction that matters; repeated examples should not imply prevalence. Diagram links should not depict inevitable one-way passage from need to spend.

This is a story hierarchy for integration, not a newly approved publication outline or final report copy. Needs, choice, evidence and outcomes must ultimately be populated from permissioned original accounts where the commission calls for them.

## Public report, private Table advice and restricted material

The public report can contain cleared context, method, permissioned practitioner synthesis and the practical framework, with leader/supplier implications that do not prescribe recruitment. The Table assessment must separately hold buyer/member reservations and commitment, comparables, proposed licences, JGA's costs/revenues/cash assumptions and a proceed/revise/pause recommendation. Public research themes and an attractive framework do not prove people will pay for a research licence or attend/contribute usefully.

Identity/permission linkage, unredacted participant/buyer accounts and confidential commercial facts belong only in the approved restricted arrangement. No new collection/storage arrangement is created by this synthesis. A public citation or a locally retained source is not blanket final reuse approval.
