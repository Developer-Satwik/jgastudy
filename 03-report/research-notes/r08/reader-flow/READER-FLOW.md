The existing checkpoint proposes two report parts and eight chapters, ending in “From Ambition to Investment.” That structure remains subject to Nick's review; the proposed approximate 20–24 page target is not a fixed limit or approval.

## The reader promise

Proposed plain-English positioning:

> Understand the work that needs to improve, what supports a credible decision, and the next step that fits the case.

This is a promise about the purpose of the analytical aid, not a guarantee of approval, successful change or a validated prediction. It accommodates a need that never became a proposal, a pending case, a successful renewal, a stopped component and an operating change awaiting assessment. Investment can involve existing operating resources or a smaller improvement; it need not mean a new system, provider or hire.

The report's contribution to test is the connection among concrete needs, decision evidence and workable responses. It should not claim familiar themes such as automation or skills are discoveries, that buying barriers were previously unexplored, or that the publication is uniquely valuable before practitioner/commercial review. FND-001–006.

## A compact canvas with optional depth

Use six linked views around one case or priority. A small context strip carries the actual remit/service unit, as-of date and knowledge basis. An evidence marker distinguishes published context, practitioner account, researcher interpretation and missing information. These are reading aids, not a score or maturity ladder.

| Linked view | Plain-English instruction |
| --- | --- |
| What needs to improve? | Name the actual task, who is affected, what already works and the desired outcome. |
| Which responses are feasible? | Compare the relevant ways to respond, including using current capability or keeping a functioning arrangement. |
| Who can decide or unblock it? | Identify the authority, funding responsibility, assurance or other condition relevant to this action and date. |
| What would justify and support it? | Connect the intended outcome to appropriate evidence, resources, affordability and a workable delivery basis. |
| What is actually known to have happened? | Record the proposal decision, commitment, delivery and benefit assessment separately, using their own dates and evidence. |
| What is the next appropriate step? | State the remaining evidence or condition, whose action is relevant and when review makes sense; no change can be a legitimate outcome. |

A reader can start with whichever view matches the situation and return to another when new evidence changes it. Numbered view IDs are references only. Avoid a single arrow ending in “buy,” traffic-light grades, totals or completion percentages that imply predictive validity. Unknown, not measured, not applicable and not disclosed remain distinct.

View 5 should visibly keep **proposal decision**, **commitment**, **delivery** and **assessed result** separate. Approval is not go-live; go-live is not complete authorised operation; operation is not proof of the intended benefit. A last evidenced state is not automatically current. View 6 must permit a further evidence check, a condition controlled by another party, a later review, continuation or a reasoned stop. FND-035,057–060,062–065.

Put the relevant selective detail behind each view in the chapter narrative or a worksheet. Do not expose all 12 authority functions,9 business-case domains,9 outcome rows and many context conditions as required fields on one page. Different cases need different detail. A short decision note is a better output than a synthetic score:

> As of [date], [specific work/outcome] concerns [scope]. The known decision is [state], while delivery and result are [separate known facts or unknown]. The relevant remaining condition is [evidence/dependency], with [role/party if known] responsible for the next action or review.

This is a blank wording pattern. Do not fill it with invented facts, names or confident language unsupported by the source. A participant need not provide confidential documents or a full committee/portfolio history to support a useful account.

## Preserve the two-part story

The executive summary should eventually answer three plain questions: what mattered in the evidence, why the actual decisions differed, and what readers can do next. It cannot yet present “the strongest barriers,” a national priority ranking or final practitioner conclusions. A source-supported provisional synopsis and an empty final-summary structure can be prepared now.

Part One begins with actual work and desired outcomes, then shows available response routes and known commitment. Part Two explains the decision, evidence, practical conditions and actual choice. Chapter 8 brings those threads together; the framework does not replace the substantive chapters.

## Different readers, a common evidential basis

For payroll leaders, focus on naming the concrete work, understanding actual authority/resources and finding the appropriate next action. Preserve what already works and the possibility of no material gap.

For finance, HR and technology colleagues, show the affected task/outcome, feasible alternatives, evidence fit, affordability, continuity and conditions. Do not turn a salary, saved hour, supplier label or technical status into a complete business case.

For suppliers, explain the condition an offer might address and what remains to be established. Do not call every dissatisfaction, feature wish or unanswered need a funded opportunity. A possible supplier response is not an adopted action or proven remedy. FND-028,044–054,060,066,074–076.

For JGA, retain the distinction between capability needed and a particular commercial response. Mentoring, process change, retention, using current tools, specialist support and hiring remain possible routes depending on the evidence. The report should build credibility through concrete and contrary cases rather than lead every conclusion to recruitment. FND-075.

## Graphics that clarify without manufacturing precision

Prepare four editable structures: a need/consequence/outcome matrix; feasible response comparison; separate dated decision/delivery/result lanes; and the compact framework canvas. A source/date timeline is optional if useful to the final narrative, with no cross-edition progress curve. Numeric charts require genuinely comparable data and valid base/units before drafting; a qualitative theme count is not a national benchmark.

D08 still requires editable graphics and underlying Excel data in the finished package. This note only specifies their use; no completed figure, chart data, Excel workbook or final designed asset is claimed. Empty observation/chart arrays remain empty. The earlier 352-answer AI study is a different project and cannot become payroll buyer-frequency data.

## Separate publication from The Table advice

The public report may include practical practitioner and supplier implications. The confidential commercial package must hold Table buyer/member reservations, partner negotiations, proposed licence prices/rights, staffing/venue/financial assumptions, cash exposure and the recommendation for Nick and Mark. Do not insert a Table launch pitch into the report conclusion, imply buyers committed, or treat publication usefulness as willingness to pay. FND-011 and agreement section 5.

The executive summary and two-page preview will need the final evidence and cleared claims; a preview can explain planned value before fieldwork but must remain labelled as such. The portable Word/PDF/PowerPoint/Excel handover remains the contracted route. A website, user account or dependence on Riseklix hosting is unnecessary.

## What can move now and what cannot

The source-grounded terminology, blank canvas, chapter evidence map, report outline and explicitly constructed demonstrations can be prepared now. Actual cross-case findings, quotes, strongest/weakest contextual conclusions, measured benefit claims, final executive-summary headlines and claims of practitioner validation must wait for eligible permissioned contributions and coding. Methods must describe actual achieved coverage, not simply the intended 6–10 target.

Before final publication, complete client scope/structure review, currentness/factual/rights and confidentiality checks, integration of contrary cases and final rendered-file QA. Retain final source-specific holds rather than selecting a convenient number. This reader-flow proposal does not close those dependencies or count as their approval.
