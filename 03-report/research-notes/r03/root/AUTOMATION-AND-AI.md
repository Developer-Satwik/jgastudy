# R03: automation and AI at the task level

29 October 2026. Internal desktop analysis. No product trial, model benchmark, participant data or private payroll input. The distinctions below are an analytical vocabulary, not a validated industry maturity scale.

## Start with the task and permitted action

"AI in payroll" is too broad to identify what changes or who remains accountable. Separate calculation, movement of records, identifying exceptions, explaining information and authorising changes. A single product can combine several mechanisms; no label alone establishes the quality of the result.

| Mechanism for case clarification | What it may change | Condition or trade-off to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Configured rules and controls | Apply defined checks, calculations or routing consistently. | Rule coverage, configuration, effective dates, test cases and the owner of an alert. A flagged variance is not necessarily an error. |
| Workflow or robotic task execution | Repeat a specified sequence or move work between steps. | Stable inputs/interfaces, permissions, exceptions and recovery. A successful run does not establish business correctness. |
| File/API integration | Move data between defined systems. | Identity and meaning, completeness, timing, rejected records, duplicate handling, reconciliation and ownership. Connectivity does not authorise pay. |
| Analytical/anomaly support | Identify unusual cases or patterns for investigation. | Appropriate baseline, false positives/misses, review capacity and confirmation of the actual issue. An unusual payment can be correct. |
| Generated answers or summaries | Help users understand records, policies or exceptions. | Access permissions, source currency, factual checks, user understanding and escalation. An answer can be fluent and still wrong. |
| Action-taking assistant or agent | Propose or execute steps across a workflow. | Exact action/authority, approvals, logs, stop/recovery and meaningful intervention. Do not infer unrestricted autonomy from a marketing label. |

These mechanisms are neither mutually exclusive nor a progression from primitive to advanced. They can operate within existing software, a replacement platform, an employer team or a managed service.

## What current originals establish

The UK Government's AI Playbook, published 10 February 2025, supports choosing a tool for a defined need, considering established alternatives, testing and monitoring, and meaningful human control. It is public-sector guidance, used here as a transferable design lens. Its legal passages are not adopted as current private-employer legal advice. Relevant sections: Principles 4–6; Defining the goal; Human oversight. [Original HTML](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government/artificial-intelligence-playbook-for-the-uk-government-html).

SAP's public PCC lesson describes configurable validation templates, process steps, roles and payroll-manager information. This is a concrete counterexample to treating all automated control as generative AI. It demonstrates declared implementation content, not achieved accuracy or a complete ready-made configuration for every employer/country. [Original lesson](https://learning.sap.com/courses/sap-successfactors-payroll-control-center/using-sap-best-practices-for-pcc-introduction_d7ff1f10-7fbb-4a35-830d-a27b9ffec64f), Pre-Configured Content and Standard Delivery.

ADP's 13 April 2026 release describes a payroll-variance agent, natural-language queries and practitioner review/approval. It states availability for enterprise clients in more than forty countries while describing further mid-market rollout as planned. That is a dated supplier availability claim, not verified usage or completion of the later plan. Its time-saving language combines early-adopter reporting and expert estimation without a disclosed evaluation base; no numerical savings benchmark is adopted. [Original release](https://uk.adp.com/about-adp/press-centre/adp-integrates-new-ai-agent--to-elevate-global-payroll-accuracy-and-efficiency.aspx).

ADP's live agent overview describes simple, advanced and autonomous forms as being built. It illustrates a broad product direction; it does not independently verify that every described action is deployed in every product/market. Its HR-policy outcome statistic is not a payroll-control result and is not adopted. [Live overview](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/ai-solutions/ai-agents.aspx).

## Availability, use and result are different records

For a product example, record the dated publisher claim and its product/territory scope. For a practitioner case, distinguish awareness, evaluation, trial/pilot, limited live use and wider live use only where known. Record the task, actual users, exceptions and permitted action separately. These are descriptors, not scores, and uncertainty is retained.

Benefits need their own evidence: what was expected, what changed, how assessed, over what period and at what resource cost. Availability does not prove adoption; adoption does not prove benefit; a go-live does not establish stable controls. Existing source-specific survey figures remain contextual, with their original bases/holds. They cannot be combined into a new adoption curve.

## Evidence before recommending wider use

Proposed checks, not tests performed by this project:

- Does the output/action address the identified need against representative normal and exception cases?
- Is sensitive information restricted to the appropriate user and approved environment?
- Can a person understand, correct, stop or escalate a material issue at the necessary time?
- Does review work displace the claimed time saving, and what happens to genuinely released capacity?
- Are changes, failed transfers and unresolved cases visible to the responsible owner?
- Can the system be updated, recovered or withdrawn without losing essential pay/control work?

These are optional analytic prompts under the existing guide, not an extra compulsory questionnaire. They do not constitute a technical certification, security audit or procurement decision. R03 supplies option conditions; R04–R07 and original cases will establish authority, investment evidence, decisions and priorities.
