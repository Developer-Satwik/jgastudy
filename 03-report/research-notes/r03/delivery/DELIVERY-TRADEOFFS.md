# R03 delivery and retained-work trade-offs

29 October 2026. Internal desktop assessment. No provider ranking, procurement recommendation, practitioner input or final publication approval. Original cohort and scope remain unchanged. No external contact occurred.

## Main conclusion

The practical choice is a combination of tasks and authorities, not one in-house/outsourced label. Start with the R02 outcome, locate the work and exception owner, then compare the least extensive viable change with alternatives. A service can change who executes a task without removing the employer's retained duties or proving an improvement.

The option map below is analytical: plausible mechanisms and case-specific conditions to test. No row establishes typical savings, reduced headcount, market demand or a guaranteed result. Options can be combined, and adequate arrangements can justify no immediate change.

## What the inspected originals add

- HMRC's current [choose-payroll guidance](https://www.gov.uk/paye-for-employers/choose-payroll), SRC-0078, describes software-operated and provider-operated routes while retaining employer responsibility for PAYE tasks. This supports an accountability boundary, not advice on a specific organisation's legal position.
- The current [Sourcing Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-sourcing-and-consultancy-playbooks/the-sourcing-playbook-html), SRC-0077, recommends evaluating in-house, market and mixed delivery at service-component level, with proportionate testing and transition planning. Its public-procurement requirements are not private-employer mandates.
- Fourth's registered 2026 definition, SRC-0094, distinguishes knowledge guidance from execution for self-managed packages, retained inputs/checks/approval, and a separately opted-in BACS service. Its declared terms do not establish actual achieved service. Relevant PDF pp10, 17–18 and 22 were inspected; p22 was rendered and visually checked in this pass.
- Cintra's current [in-house/outsourced guide](https://help.cintra.co.uk/docs/in-house-vs-outsourced-payroll) and older [cycle guide](https://help.cintra.co.uk/payroll/docs/understanding-the-payroll-cycle) separate payroll processing from making payments. An in-house customer can use bureau BACS; a bureau-processed payroll can leave payments elsewhere. The terms are supplier-specific, not industry definitions. Both retain customer review/approval at relevant stages.
- Phase 3's current [managed-services page](https://phase3.co.uk/what-we-do/managed-services/) declares support ranging from individual processes to full operation. Its older 2021–2022 definition, SRC-0097, illustrates separately described input, month-end and fully managed activity. The dated tiers are not assumed to be today's entitlements. PDF pp3–8 were inspected; p5 was rendered and visually checked. Promotional accuracy/volume claims were excluded.

| Response path | When it may address a diagnosed need | Potential advantage to test | Dependencies/new burden or failure condition | Evidence needed before choosing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retain the arrangement; clarify process, owner, cutoff or control | Capability is adequate but information, authority or exception routing is unclear | Resolve a specific handoff without migration or a new platform | Owner must have capacity and authority; a revised instruction alone may not change behaviour or an external dependency | Baseline/known case, clear owner, completed control, observed recurrence and consequence closure |
| Configure or use more of the existing software/service | Required task is supported but configuration, entitlement, permissions or training is the gap | Preserve functioning components and avoid unnecessary replacement | Configuration may introduce downstream effects; an available feature may be unsuitable or an extra-charge module; support may advise rather than execute | Current version/package, representative task tests, reconciled outputs, authorisation and support escalation |
| Replace software with employer-run processing | An evaluated capability/fit limitation remains after simpler options are considered | Different workflow, functionality or information access | Employer still needs operational expertise, cover, data/configuration ownership and maintenance; transition and integration can displace live work | Fit to actual pay/exception types, migrated balances/IDs, parallel/reconciliation evidence, exit/recovery readiness and retained staffing |
| Bureau or managed execution | Defined recurring tasks or cover can be placed with a provider under an agreed service | Access to execution capacity or specialist support | Employer input, checking, policy, approval and oversight may remain; the label does not define every query/payment/recovery duty | Actual schedule and scope, responsibility map, representative end-to-end test, confirmed exceptions and achieved response/resolution |
| Hybrid or co-sourced components | A specific part of the cycle needs support while other work is appropriately retained | Target a constraint while retaining useful control/knowledge | More boundaries can create duplicated or omitted work; each interface needs an owner and route for change | Component-level ownership, handoff acceptance, separate payroll/payment authority, support entitlement and end-to-end closure |
| Time-bound specialist, temporary cover or capability development | The need is an absence, project skill, knowledge gap or temporary workload rather than unsuitable core software | Bridge the relevant task or build capability without assuming a permanent new delivery model | Access, authority, documentation, cover and handback must work; advice is not owned execution, and a new person cannot change an external institution alone | Defined tasks/deliverables, competency/context fit, authorisation, quality review, cover/handback and observed result |
| No immediate purchase or replacement | Current outcomes and controls are adequate, or a proposed change lacks useful fit/readiness evidence | Preserve continuity and capacity while a prerequisite or further evidence is addressed | Deliberate deferral must not conceal a known material unresolved problem; identify the condition that would trigger reconsideration | Adequate present outcome/control evidence, reason for deferral, responsible review point and relevant trigger |

These paths are not a required escalation ladder or an assertion that the least extensive option always wins. An urgent material gap may require a larger change; a time-bound response may coexist with permanent redesign. Full approval mechanics and quantified lifetime economics belong to R04/R05, while R03 identifies the conditions those later assessments must examine.

## The two-axis payment example

Cintra's dated documentation supplies a concrete illustration of why a single binary is inadequate:

| Who processes payroll | Who makes payments | Relevant distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employer | Employer/bank arrangement | Software workflow is not the entire bank/payment process. |
| Employer | Bureau | Payment support can be delegated without outsourcing processing. |
| Bureau | Employer/bank arrangement | Returned/approved payroll output is separate from making the payment. |
| Bureau | Bureau | Customer review and authorisation can remain despite delegated execution. |

This is a documentation example, not an observed distribution of employers or a universal process. Absence of an in-product approval banner does not establish absence of organisational authorisation. Confirm the actual current workflow and applicable service agreement.

## Source-freshness conflict to preserve

The Cintra in-house article was published 7 July and updated 24 September 2026; it says full-access SaaS users can now calculate within Cintra People and view status symbols. The cycle article is dated 15 May/23 June 2026 and says symbols are shown only for bureau-managed payrolls and refers in-house continuation to Cintra iQ. We do not silently combine those UI claims. The processing/payment separation is supported by both, but exact access, calculation, status and authorisation behaviour requires current configuration/version verification. The difference is not proof of product failure or its resolution.

The Phase 3 G-Cloud14 listing, SRC-0096, also has broad UK/ROI wording alongside a UK-only service constraint. The old attached definition is explicitly 2021–2022. Do not infer jurisdiction coverage, current package entitlement or contract terms without confirmation. A promise of data export is not proof that a receiving process can reconcile or use it. The integration strand addresses those technical conditions.

## Minimum retained-work map

For a relevant case, locate rather than assume who:

1. Defines and approves pay policy/rules and supplies complete authorised changes.
2. Configures, processes, validates and reconciles the result.
3. Reviews output, authorises release and confirms transmission/receipt.
4. Owns employee explanations and exception escalation, including external institutions.
5. Maintains access, records, continuity, change control and exit/handback information.

Separate operating execution, authority and accountability. One person/provider may occupy several roles, but a commercial label cannot establish that. Support for a payroll administrator is not automatically direct support for employees, and an acknowledgement target is not resolution.

## What demonstrates a useful result

Go-live, a feature being enabled, an outsourced contract and a completed hire are delivery events. For the diagnosed outcome, seek the known baseline, actual affected work, observed result, period and unresolved consequences. Check whether effort disappeared or moved to managers, payroll, finance, employees, the supplier or an external authority. Released capacity is not automatically lower expenditure.

Use the existing core questions and selective probes. No new mandatory question, sample target, confidential employee record request or scoring system is added. Preserve adequate arrangements and contrary cases. The map supplies conditional choices for R03; only permissioned actual cases can establish materiality and realised outcomes for the JGA cohort.
