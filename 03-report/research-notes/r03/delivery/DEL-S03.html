    Managed Services

    Let us take care of the everyday administrative and transactional tasks

    We offer outsourced services for payroll, finance and HR systems administration and reporting. All services are delivered by our dedicated in-house consultancy team ensuring the best experts are looking after those processes most precious to you.

    Find out more

    Phase 3’s managed services can take the pain away from running daily processes, leaving you to focus on more strategic objectives.

    High quality Flexible services Award-winning

    Ensuring high quality

    The Phase 3 service centre boasts a 99% accuracy rating, well above industry standards. Our customers choose us for everything from fully managed services to outsourcing single processes. As we are whole-of-market specialists we can work with whatever your current solution is, no matter how complex or simple.

    Flexible and accurate services

    Our managed payroll services operate across the UK with over £30m of BACS processed on a weekly/monthly basis with a 99.5% accuracy rating. We tailor our services to suit your business, whether that’s by supporting your in-house team with month end processes, or completely running your payroll function on your behalf.

    Award-winning services

    Over the years Phase 3 have been recognised by the Chartered Institute of Payroll Professionals Awards, Personnel Today Awards, The Rewards, and the HR Excellence Awards. We also hold the CIPP Payroll Assurance Scheme accreditation.

    We hold accreditations for Cyber Essentials, ISO27001 for information security and ISO9001 for Quality management. Phase 3 are implementing the Investors in People accreditation and have undertaken SOC1 Type 2 auditing.
