About Phase 3
Phase 3 are independent and whole-of-market specialists in the application of 
people technologies, providing Consulting, Services and industry Insights to UK-
based customer organisations, partner product suppliers and to the professional 
community. 
We apply our expertise to help our customers choose, implement, develop and 
understand their preferred technology for HR, payroll and business intelligence and 
to develop increasing organisational capabilities with people technology. 
Our belief is that it is the people behind the technology that is the greatest 
enabler to success, which means that the core Phase 3 values are those of talent, 
objectivity, credibility and knowledge-sharing. 
Well-established and proven over a 17-year history, the company is based in 
Manchester. Phase 3 Consultants work nationwide and as a close team, valuing 
one another’s individual expertise so that it is compounded for those we advise. 
Day-to-day Phase 3 are delivering systems selection and implementation projects 
and long-term bite-sized consultancy, running managed services and delivering 
training and events.

Service Level 1: Month End Pay Sphere
Month End Pay Sphere – for those clients who have in house payroll teams who 
would like the assurance that month end processes are entirely accurate, audited 
and compliant.   
This service includes:
 z Processing of payroll in line with payroll best practice
 z Gross to net calculations
 z Auto Enrolment assessment and eligible Jobholder Enrolment
 z Costing interfaces and recompilation
 z Review of all statutory payments and deductions
 z Payroll reconciliation
 z Production of all best practice payroll reports
 z Quality Assurance document for each pay period covering any notable 
exceptions, differences to previous pay periods, assessment of 
payroll accuracy, issues/risks, pension assessment, compliance and 
recommendations for future months
 z Submission of all necessary returns (including Real Time Information, 
Pension Provider Extracts, Remittance to third parties)
 z Tax Y ear End processing
 z Issue (electronically) of all payroll correspondence for distribution internally
 z Pension reconciliation
 z Voluntary Deduction Reconciliation
 z Any additional payroll interfaces (e.g. T emporary Input, Checking reports)
 z Quarterly review meetings to discuss payroll controls
 z Documentation stored off site, securely with audit accessibility for audit or 
other key staff
 z Full assurance for legislative compliance

Service Level 2: Payroll Input Sphere
Payroll Input Sphere – for those clients who don’t have the time or the team to 
complete payroll input. 
This service includes:
 z Processing of all New Starters, Leavers, Contract Changes, Restructures, 
Transfers, Additional Positions  with 100% of input checked
 z Entry of T emporary input such as overtime/bonus/additional payments
 z Processing of new and amendments to employee Permanent elements such 
as salary sacrifice, allowances or other voluntary deductions
 z Bulk processing of salary changes (such as annual reviews, increment 
processing)
 z Changes to bank details
 z Changes to names or addresses
 z Changes to organisation structure including reporting lines
 z Response to all third party requests for information e.g. Mortgage 
Applications, T enancy Applications, Earnings assessments from DWP
 z Amendments to Tax Codes and National Insurance Categories in line with 
legislation
 z Start/end notifications for Student Loans
 z Start/end notifications for Court Orders
 z Creation of voluntary pension contributions (Such as AVC, ARC etc.)
 z Changes to voluntary pension contributions
 z Processing of overpayments
 z Absence return processing (for where self-service is not in use)
 z Processing of advances for underpayments
 z Monthly reports on transaction KPIs
 z Provision of monthly transaction data for issuing of documentation
 z Monthly MI to core payroll function detailing numbers and types of changes 
to allow payroll reconciliation

Intergrating our services
The payroll services are delivered by a team of expert consultants not administrators. 
Y ou will be assigned a named consultant (who is part of a larger team) who will be 
responsible for your day to day operations. Should you require any developmental 
consultancy this can be delivered by the same team either through Bite-sized or with 
our integrated on site team. This ensures a continuity of personnel and a  
thorough understanding of you, our client. 
 
Our prices are based on the number of employees and payrolls, this is a fixed price 
with no hidden costs. Each menu can be tailored to meet your needs ensuring a 
service which is relevant and responsive.
