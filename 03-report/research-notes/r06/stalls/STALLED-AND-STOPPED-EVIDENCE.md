# R06: postponement, component cancellation and narrow notice status

29 October 2026. Internal desktop evidence. Two selected decision histories plus one narrow publication-status context, using four original public HTML routes. No original JGA contributor, numerical estimate, product trial or external contact.

## Main contribution

A pause or cancellation needs an object and a date. Postponing a payroll implementation, cancelling one component and cancelling an advance notice are different events. None alone establishes whether the underlying need disappeared, whether another option was chosen or whether a remedy worked. The source's reported reason must remain separate from our explanation.

## Implications for the outcome/response matrix

1. Record the specific object: proposed option, contract, release date, component, procurement or notice. A stopped option may coexist with continued payroll need or another functioning component.
2. Preserve dated positions. An earlier approval can be followed by delay, re-scope or cancellation. A later scoped access announcement adds information without retrospectively proving the earlier decision or its effect.
3. For a postponement, establish the remaining condition, owner and next review or date where known. Readiness and school-calendar timing may be reported reasons, but do not turn them into a universal implementation window or proof of risk reduction.
4. For a stop, establish what ceased and what continued. Exit can interact with equipment, other services or contracts; inspect the actual dependency rather than calling every continued relationship inertia or lock-in.
5. Keep a suggested response, accepted action and observed result separate. A proposal to re-scope or improve exit terms is an analytical possibility until supported by the case. STL6-C06.

These are selective clarifications under Q006, Q007, Q012 and Q013. They do not add core questions, require confidential contracts or create a mandatory workflow. Start with the contributor's unaided account and respect unknown/not disclosed.

## Evidence gaps worth retaining

This strand does not establish the prevalence of any outcome, a current private-employer rejection rationale or that one response reliably converts rejection into approval. Public records expose selected programmes and procurement labels, not all internal deliberation. The current West Sussex communications are official statements rather than independent benefit evaluation. NIAO's historical summary provides a stronger external audit account within its own date and scope; the linked full PDF was not inspected or relied upon.

The GETS case is a useful warning against classifying a whole buying decision from a portal label. Its reason and later route remain unknown. No actual tender, upgrade abandonment or UK market conclusion should be inferred.

For practitioners, clarify what is being stopped or deferred and which obligations remain. For suppliers, distinguish the customer's actual decision and unresolved condition from a sales-stage label. For JGA, include rational staging and scoped cancellation without converting selected failures into a general anti-technology or pro-hiring narrative.
