FAO: headteachers and school business professionals
Employees can access Oracle to view and manage a range of employment information through self-service, including:
Viewing personal information and employment details
Updating bank account details
Viewing payslips (from October 2026 onwards)
Once logged in, we recommend that colleagues:
Check their personal details are correct: if any information requires updating and you are unable to make the change yourself, please continue to follow the existing process through HR Shared Services or your payroll administrator
Confirm their bank account information is accurate and, if required, update through Oracle self-service
Familiarise themselves with Oracle and the information available to them
Please remind staff to retain copies of any historic payslips they may require, as payslips from before Oracle go-live are not available within Oracle.
Learning and support
The Oracle Learning and Support page provides access to guidance documents, eLearning, and support materials. We ask that staff review the Oracle access and navigation guides before raising a query, as these cover many of the most common questions and tasks.
Additional information, FAQs and programme updates can also be found on the Oracle page on West Sussex Services for Education. For issues accessing Oracle, please email oracle.external.access@westsussex.gov.uk. This mailbox creates a support ticket directly with the technical team. For any other queries, please email hrsharedservicesschools@westsussex.gov.uk. Thank you to everyone who has supported the transition to Oracle Fusion.
Giulia Di Lorenzo, Programme Coordinator and Engagement Lead
