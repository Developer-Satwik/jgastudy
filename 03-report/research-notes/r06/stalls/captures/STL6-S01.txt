FAO: school business professionals
As you know, the county council is continuing its work to implement Oracle Fusion for HR and Payroll, replacing the current SAP system. Once live, Oracle Fusion will allow employees to view electronic payslips and P60s, access payroll information held by the Council, and update personal bank details.
Further to the email shared with you on Monday 9 March, the programme has reviewed the previously planned April implementation date and confirmed that Oracle Fusion for HR and Payroll will now be implemented in October 2026. This revised timeline reflects the scale of the change, impacting over 20,000 users, and the need to ensure a safe, stable system with a reliable and accurate payroll, particularly for schools and term-time employees.
As previously communicated on 23 February, all Oracle users will require a unique, secure corporate email address to access the system. Employees who cannot be provided with a compliant email address will not be able to access Oracle Fusion and will continue to receive paper payslips. Please ensure this requirement is being progressed within your school and that the earlier communications have been shared.
We will continue to keep you updated with clear timelines, programme information, and guidance materials ahead of any required changes. In the meantime, please refer to the Implementing Oracle page on Services for Education for more information.
Giulia Di Lorenzo, Programme Coordinator & Engagement Lead
