# R06: retention, renewal and the unit of change

29 October 2026. Three selected primary public employer decision contexts. Five bounded claims/analytical applications. Local raw requests returned 403; original public bodies were inspected through the web reader and paraphrased selected notes retained. No interview, contract, supplier trial, numerical estimate or completed switching result was created.

## Main finding

“Staying” and “switching” need an object. An employer can continue a payroll platform while changing the commercial route, or change its organisational service boundary without public evidence of a new platform. A renewal approval is a decision, not a delivery result. These distinctions sharpen R06 without adding another required question.

## What this adds to the analysis

**Define the unit before classifying the choice.** Consider platform, service/task scope, commercial route/counterparty, shared organisational boundary and retained work as relevant. Keeping one does not mean keeping all. Do not manufacture a full component map for a small decision. RET6-C04.

**Interpret continuity within the actual choice set.** A stated timing or transition constraint can make continuation reasonable at that point without proving complete satisfaction, permanent inability to switch or irrational inertia. The record can also contain service merit and constraints together. Do not infer an unreported future tender, forced lock-in, psychological bias or supplier-rescue remedy. RET6-C05.

**Keep statuses separate.** Public approval, authority to procure, contract signature, start of work, usable service and observed benefit require different evidence. A website's Implemented label can describe completion of the decision record; it does not establish payroll go-live. Likewise, a published purpose may describe a proposed arrangement even where a meeting separately confirms recommendations agreed.

## Practitioner, supplier and JGA implications

For a practitioner case, obtain the unaided explanation first, then clarify what changed or stayed, the relevant decision date, alternatives and feasible next step. Existing Q006/Q007/Q012/Q013 already cover this. A lack of material shortfall, unknown outcome or withheld detail remains valid.

For suppliers, a credible alternative must fit the actual service boundary, timing and retained work. These selected cases do not establish that any particular offer would have changed the decision, or that continuity concerns can simply be sold away.

For JGA, report retention on merit, constrained continuity and mixed choices without forcing a satisfied/dissatisfied binary or implying that every unresolved need calls for a new leader. Earlier R05 funding/capacity distinctions remain relevant; no recruitment recommendation follows from these examples.
