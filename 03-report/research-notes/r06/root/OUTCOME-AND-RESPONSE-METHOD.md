# R06 outcome and response method

29 October 2026. Internal analytical design, not a validated behavioural taxonomy.

## Four separate records

Identify the actual option (including retained, mixed and people/process responses), the dated disposition of a specific proposal/decision object, implementation, and observed/expected benefit. One project may have several components or successive decisions. Retaining a platform while changing route is not contradictory. Approved, live and benefited are different claims.

Use the existing Q007 dispositions: approved; declined; deferred; withdrawn/abandoned; pending; no formal approval required; not known; prefer not to disclose. A public source that is silent about an approval does not establish pending status or exemption. A continued service does not alone establish a recent deliberate renewal. Preserve original wording, date precision and the contributor/source's knowledge.

The pre-proposal route in Q006 is separate: a need not raised, an older context, an uncertain case and a declined request are not all indecision. A recent substantive milestone can qualify in an older programme; routine continued operation alone cannot. Existing fourteen core questions stay unchanged.

## Why stopping needs a fair reading

GDS discovery guidance supports investigating the real problem, constraints and alternatives before a commitment to build, including a reasoned stop. This is government guidance, not an observed payroll cancellation or a universal judgement that stopping is correct. The R06 application is to establish what was stopped, what need remained and what alternative/next review existed. ROOT6-C01/C04.

## A specific transition condition

HMRC's current RTI guidance identifies payroll-ID linkage when software/provider arrangements change. It supports one concrete continuity/data-control condition, not a blanket instruction to wait until year-end or a measured probability of failure. Do not infer the reason for any actual buyer's delay from general guidance. ROOT6-C02.

## A bounded positive operational observation

OEP's January2025 management report records the staff-record transition and successful December2024 payroll processing, while refinement remained. The cover/date and paragraph37 on PDF page8 were visually checked. The report does not establish the selection rationale, all accuracy tests, cash benefits or later performance. ROOT6-C03.

## Response matrix discipline

For a stated material condition, record the actual reason and basis, whose decision or work it affects, whether the condition is temporary/addressable/structural/unknown only when supported, response actually adopted, and what happened next. Separately label a proposed response and what evidence would be needed to assess it. Never turn a proposed remedy into observed successful mitigation or presume every constraint should be overcome to win a sale. ROOT6-C05.

This assists relevant clarification under Q008–Q013, not a second interview or demand for confidential contracts. Unknown, not measured, not applicable and non-disclosure remain distinct. Public cases are selected context, not new JGA practitioners or estimates of buyer behaviour.
