## Working interpretation

These sources weaken a generic claim that the market lacks payroll-frustration, AI, strategic-value or investment material. They do not rule out a more specific useful contribution.

A testable JGA angle is the concrete transition from an identified need to a funded, delayed, rejected, abandoned or renewed decision: what evidence changed the outcome, who could approve or veto it, what implementation conditions were non-negotiable and why an unsatisfactory incumbent was retained. That angle is a research hypothesis, not a claim of uniqueness. Root's parallel G2/Paycom, Vistra, KPMG/UKG and ADP reviews must be compared before the landscape conclusion.

Keep variation in source population visible. A mega-enterprise cost benchmark, a mixed-stakeholder sentiment index, a recruitment-oriented efficiency study and a UK/Ireland AI landing page answer different questions. Do not pool their percentages or present associations as causes.
