# R05: what makes a benefit claim usable?

29 October 2026. Internal evidence-design research. No payroll product was tested, employer forecast calculated or practitioner account collected.

## Judgment

A credible business case explains the link from the proposed change to the result, which assumptions matter and how the result can be assessed. A published benefit, fluent demonstration, enabled feature, passed test and observed outcome answer different questions. Evidence should fit the actual claim and decision; a larger pile of documents is not automatically stronger evidence.

Two primary guidance routes sharpen this design:

- The current Magenta Book connects early evaluation planning to the business case and distinguishes performance tracking from examining additional effect and attribution. Its approach is proportionate and government-scoped; it does not require every employer to commission an experiment. R5ROOT-C01–C02.
- NIST's Measure playbook emphasises evaluation in relevant operating conditions and limits to generalising results. That supports task/context-specific scrutiny of an AI claim, not certification of a payroll product, a payroll accuracy threshold or proof of financial savings. The page says AI RMF 1.0 and the playbook are being updated; final precise use needs a version check. R5ROOT-C03.

## A selective claim record

For an important cost, benefit or readiness claim, establish:

| Field | Useful distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Claim and decision | What result is asserted; which option, commitment or release it informs. |
| Actual context | Relevant task, service, employer scope, population/period, volume and quality requirements. Not every dimension is needed in every case. |
| Current basis | Supplier declaration, employer forecast, planned control, executed test, reported outcome or evaluated contribution. These are labels for provenance/state, not an ascending power score. |
| Comparison | Realistic baseline/alternative, what changed, other relevant changes and unknowns. A simple before/after observation does not by itself isolate the effect. |
| Conditions | Adoption, configuration, data, integration, people, review/exception work and other dependencies necessary for the claim. |
| Measurement | Available baseline, intended indicator, source/definition, owner and relevant assessment period. Missing or undisclosed information stays labelled. |
| Uncertainty | Range/assumption when actually supported, applicability limits, disagreement and what information could alter the decision. No invented probabilities. |
| Actual use | What was requested and what reportedly influenced the decision, preserving the participant's knowledge basis. Evidence requested is not automatically evidence provided, accepted or influential. |

This is an analyst worksheet around existing Q008–Q013, not eight additional interview questions or a demand for confidential financial records. A directly experienced account can be valuable even when figures or formal documents cannot be shared. Preserve approximate, unknown, not applicable and prefer-not-to-disclose states.

## Specific use for an automation or AI proposal

Where AI is actually part of the option, identify the task, system/version or service scope as known, permitted actions, relevant operating conditions and output quality. Consider review and correction work alongside direct processing time, and distinguish pre-release evidence from performance after adoption. A test performed with selected clean inputs may not establish how the same task works with live exceptions, changing data or different users. R5ROOT-C03–C04.

This is a selective analytical application, not a new compulsory AI procurement audit. It supplies no error tolerance, sample size, model ranking, savings estimate or implied authority to use personal/confidential data. The non-AI or smaller-process alternative remains available. Quality and meaningful oversight cannot disappear merely because a task becomes quicker.

## Implications for R05 and later work

The economics strand should define what is being valued; this strand records how far the evidence supports it. The delivery strand should distinguish plans from executed readiness checks and the state of the service. Together, they prevent a forecast or cutover milestone being relabelled a realised benefit.

R05 will not prove that an employer should buy, nor that any proposal with uncertainty must be rejected. It will expose which assumptions and missing evidence matter to the next decision. R06 will examine actual outcome explanations; practitioner input will establish what evidence was available and persuasive in each case. Our small qualitative study is not an impact evaluation or payroll ROI benchmark.
