75: BrightPay is cloud-based payroll software designed for UK and Irish employers and small to medium enterprises who need accurate payroll processing without complexity. 76: Whether you manage payroll in-house or work alongside an accountant or payroll bureau, BrightPay keeps payroll accurate, organised, and aligned with UK payroll requirements.
