Simple, compliant payroll software you can rely on

BrightPay is cloud-based payroll software designed for UK and Irish employers and small to medium enterprises who need accurate payroll processing without complexity. It supports compliant payroll workflows, automatic legislative updates, and secure access to payroll data, helping businesses run payroll confidently and efficiently.

Whether you manage payroll in-house or work alongside an accountant or payroll bureau, BrightPay keeps payroll accurate, organised, and aligned with UK payroll requirements.

What It Is

BrightPay is a cloud payroll platform that supports everyday payroll processing for employers. It combines automated calculations, automatic legislative updates, secure cloud hosting, and controlled multi-user access in one easy-to-use system.

BrightPay helps businesses pay employees accurately, meet statutory obligations, and maintain clear payroll records throughout the year.

Who It’s For

BrightPay is built for UK businesses that want reliable payroll without needing specialist payroll knowledge.

It’s ideal for:

  • Small and medium enterprises running payroll internally
  • Employers managing regular or variable payroll schedules
  • Businesses working with external accountants or payroll providers
  • Organisations needing secure, shared access to payroll data
  • Growing businesses that need payroll software that can scale

BrightPay adapts to your business size while supporting consistent, compliant payroll processes.

Team members can access BrightPay from anywhere, at any time. Whether you’re in the office, working from home, or even abroad, all you need is an internet connection to work on payroll in the cloud. Unlimited users can access the software and work on the same payroll file together, and changes will be visible in real-time.

Benefits & Outcomes

1. Stay compliant without payroll stress

BrightPay updates automatically when payroll legislation changes, helping employers keep payroll aligned with requirements without manual intervention or guesswork.

2. Save time every pay run

Automated calculations and clear workflows reduce manual processing and repetitive admin, allowing payroll to be completed efficiently each cycle.

3. Pay employees accurately and on time

Clear calculations and controlled processes help reduce errors, ensuring employees are paid correctly and consistently.

4. Work confidently with your accountant or payroll provider

BrightPay allows accountants or payroll professionals to access the same up-to-date payroll data, reducing duplication, errors, and end-of-period delays.

5. Scale payroll as your business grows

BrightPay supports additional employees, users, and payroll complexity as your business evolves, without needing to change systems.

Why Bright?

BrightPay is designed for compliance-driven work, even when payroll isn’t your core expertise.

Compared to basic payroll tools, BrightPay offers:

  • Compliance built into payroll workflows
  • Automatic legislative updates
  • Clear, structured payroll records
  • Integration with accounting software and pension providers
  • Real human support when you need guidance

This means payroll stays manageable, accurate, and dependable as your business grows.

How It Works

BrightPay supports a simple, structured payroll process:

  • Employee details and payroll data are entered securely
  • Calculations update automatically in line with legislation
  • Payroll is reviewed and processed
  • Payments are made to employees and HMRC
  • Payslips and reports are stored securely
  • Records remain accessible for reporting and review

The result is a straightforward payroll process that supports compliance without unnecessary complexity.

FAQs

Is BrightPay suitable if I don’t have payroll expertise?

Yes. BrightPay is designed to be easy to use, with clear workflows that support employers without specialist payroll knowledge.

Does BrightPay update when payroll rules change?

Yes. BrightPay updates automatically when payroll legislation changes.

Can my accountant access my BrightPay data?

Yes. BrightPay supports shared access, allowing accountants or payroll providers to work from the same payroll data.

Is BrightPay cloud-based?

Yes. BrightPay is cloud-based, allowing secure access to payroll data from anywhere.

Can BrightPay scale as my business grows?

Yes. BrightPay supports additional employees and users as your business expands.
