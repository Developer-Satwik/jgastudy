# R05 commercial evidence: compare the actual service and full cost

29 October 2026. Internal desktop strand. Three new supplier-document routes, a reused Xero pricing route and reused official sourcing context. No price quotation, product test, market-average estimate or practitioner contribution.

## Judgment

A headline monthly or per-person amount is not the cost of solving a payroll need. Define the actual option, service and charging unit, then separate recurring charges, transition work, retained work and future exit/renewal obligations. Unknown cost is not zero. A public tariff is a dated supplier declaration, not a negotiated quote or verified total cost.

## Evidence to establish in an actual proposal

The following is a proposed checklist. It does not assert that each supplier charges every item or that every small change needs a large procurement exercise.

| Cost/evidence question | What to record where relevant |
| --- | --- |
| What is bought? | Product/module, service component, country/entity, processing/payment/support responsibilities, outputs and included access. Distinguish software from managed execution and an implementation engagement. |
| What is the charging unit? | Base fee, organisation/employer, people paid versus active/highest-recorded people, frequency/pay run, country/entity, transaction, user or usage band. Record inclusions and how changes in population trigger charges. |
| What is the price basis? | Dated source or actual quote, currency, tax treatment, volume assumption, minimum, promotion/expiry, recurring price and applicable term. Do not turn an empty calculator, discount or old licence into a current quote. |
| What sits outside the base? | Ask whether relevant setup/migration, configuration, interfaces/API, reporting, payment, additional entities, support tiers, statutory/exception work or changes are included, separately charged or unknown. Do not assume a connector includes implementation or ongoing monitoring. |
| What work remains internally? | Data preparation/approval, operational checking, provider oversight, employee/manager support, training, testing, transition capacity, cover and continuing administration. Separate additional cash cost from existing time/opportunity cost. |
| What changes over time? | Quantity growth or churn, recurring usage, renewal/repricing, indexation if relevant, payment timing, minimum commitments, notice/change/cancellation terms and service entitlement after a change. Keep cash timing separate from cost recognition. |
| How does the option end or continue? | Renewal/retained option, outgoing and incoming work overlap, handover/data/configuration/knowledge, formats and access after termination, exit assistance and who supplies/costs it. A file export is not proof of an operational receiving service. |
| Who accepts the scope? | Link the cost line and acceptance object to the specific R04 decision/right. An operating user, invoice approver, output acceptor and signatory may differ. |

Use the same horizon and scope to compare the option with retaining or improving the current arrangement. Include its foreseeable continuing work and relevant renewal costs. A bundled accounting package already held may have a different incremental cost from buying it afresh; establish the actual case rather than allocate the whole bundle arbitrarily to payroll. This is an analytical application, not a claim about a particular Xero customer.

## Whole-life and transition context

Reused SRC-0077, [Sourcing Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-sourcing-and-consultancy-playbooks/the-sourcing-playbook-html), sections 3 and 13, describes whole-life comparisons across in-house, market and mixed delivery, transition/mobilisation, and planned exit/renewal including staff burden and information handback. This is public-sector design guidance, not mandatory private-employer procedure or a current approval rule adopted here. Its live page now shows 30 September 2026; preserve that capture separately from earlier registered versions.

For the report, compare total relevant resources and service outcomes. Do not choose a supplier from the smallest displayed rate, assume risk is transferred because marketing says so, or add speculative penalties as guaranteed savings. A guarantee needs its actual contract, scope, conditions and remedy; this review does not establish enforceability or a cash recovery.
