# R02 challenge: prior study, recruitment competitors and talent-related needs

28 October 2026. Internal research. All six earlier comparator firms plus JGA were checked at a bounded service/case level. No prompts were rerun, no contact was made and no original study/client/canonical files were changed.

## Judgment

R02's core conclusion survives: start with the operating outcome, task, handoff, owner and evidence. The useful refinement is to split the capacity lens into permanent operating coverage, temporary continuity cover, specialist change delivery and accountable leadership/retained oversight. These can coexist and require different evidence. They do not imply that hiring is always necessary or that an advertised service solves an observed need.

Recruitment competitors are different from payroll software, processing bureaus and managed-payroll providers. A recruiter can supply a person; the employer may still retain data, approval, control and provider-management obligations. Recruitment-process outsourcing is also different from payroll-processing outsourcing. These categories must not be merged in the report.

## The earlier AI study: a disciplined bridge

The prior pack contains 352 saved answers to 44 selected questions in 11 situations across four consumer tools, with repeated runs. Its six named comparators were Portfolio Payroll, Frazer Jones, Oakleaf Partnership, Middlemore, Hays and Robert Half. The exact local files, hashes, dates, selected observations and permitted transfer are recorded in [prior-study-bridge.json](prior-study-bridge.json).

The replacement, growth and interim-plus-permanent situations are useful contrasts for practitioner cases. Different observed recommendation patterns suggest routes worth checking; they establish neither the prevalence of those needs nor comparative recruiter capability. There is no new calculation of market share, buyer demand, skills shortage, service performance or hidden model reasoning. Repeats and tools are not independent employers.

The prior study's highly cited JGA page routes identify available evidence surfaces. They do not prove which pages or operating needs matter commercially. Its source-capture limitations remain in the bridge; the source ledger is not silently made complete. Older generated group affiliations, fee claims and capability descriptions remain unadopted.

## A concrete current request, with clear limits

Oakleaf's [12 October 2026 independent-school interim vacancy](https://www.oakleafpartnership.com/jobs/interim-payroll-and-pensions-manager-pr-016244) requests payroll processing, statutory submission, reconciliation, pension-domain knowledge and audit/control work in one temporary role. It supports asking what tasks and specialist context actually need coverage. An advert does not show a hire completed, prove shortage, measure understaffing or establish what happens without filling it.

## What to change in R02

1. Expand the sustainable-skills row with four optional distinctions: ongoing operating cover, temporary continuity cover, time-bound change expertise and accountable leadership/retained vendor oversight. Keep them as case descriptors, not a new scored model.
2. Establish what the person would own and be authorised to change. Additional hands, specialist advice and a leader with decision authority are different responses. This is a research interpretation to test; it is not demonstrated demand.
3. Where relevant, ask what remains with the team during change and how knowledge/ownership passes back at the end. This can use existing selective probes; do not add compulsory questions or increase the agreed interview burden.
4. Retain UK ordinary replacement/growth cases alongside complex/global transformations. Earlier prompt situations demonstrate that the study deliberately included both; only actual practitioner input can establish their current materiality.
5. Preserve successful and adequate arrangements. A specific skill can be hired, developed, borrowed temporarily, obtained through a changed service or rendered unnecessary by a better process. R03 will assess options; R02 should not preselect one.

## What must still come from real cases

The actual task/backlog or project, specialist knowledge required, timing, existing cover, decision authority, handover, recurrence, consequence, source of evidence, available alternatives and observed result. The current competitor pages cannot supply these for JGA's intended cohort.
