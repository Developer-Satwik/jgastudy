# Payroll operating needs and business consequences

28 October 2026. R02 desktop synthesis. This is an evidence-informed analytical map, not a ranking of market demand or a table of new practitioner findings. Measures below are suggestions to investigate where already available; no values have been collected from JGA contributors.

## The outcome distinctions

Evaluate correct/timely employee payment, correct statutory reporting, reliable records/reconciliation and understandable employee service separately. A case can work well on one dimension and need improvement on another. Responsibility may span payroll, HR, managers, finance and providers.

## Identify the kind of gap before the response

For every concrete need, distinguish not-known-to-be-available capability, unsuitable fit, excluded scope, affordability/value, data/readiness, capacity/skills, unclear ownership, not evaluated/proposed, or an adequate arrangement. Explanations can overlap. A participant's belief that something is unavailable is not an audit of the market.

Potential responses include clearer ownership, changing the input/control process, adjusting configuration or service scope, better evidence/communication, additional capability or a different technology/service. These are conditional options for later investigation. R03 will examine trade-offs; R02 does not choose suppliers or establish that a purchase is warranted.

## How to assess materiality without an invented score

Ask what is actually affected: people or records, timing, recurrence, worker consequence, control exposure, effort and displaced work. Record whether the account is measured, estimated or an impression, and what remains unknown. A rare serious payment issue and a frequent routine query need different treatment.

Do not manufacture weights or rank the profession's needs from these sources. Evidence of actual harm or a time-critical obligation warrants investigation in the relevant case; frequency, scale, remedy and ownership still require context. A published staffing ratio or response target does not supply a universal threshold.

## Quantities to keep separate

- Gross overpayment, amount recovered, amount outstanding and written-off loss.
- Remedial advance/supplementary payment principal and the resource cost of administering it.
- An internal charge or policy rate and a measured economic cost.
- A service target, achieved response, achieved resolution and corrected employee outcome.
- Time released, use of the released capacity and an actual reduction in expenditure.
- Self-reported agreement, audited performance and absence of an adverse incident.

This protects both the business case and the practical recommendation. No quantified retention ROI, national error rate, universal staffing norm or cost-per-error estimate is established by this module.

## Source use

The canonical source and claim registers retain original routes, populations, dates, locators and numerical holds. The two new employer examples are public-sector cases, not a representative private-market sample. Service documents establish declared scope; guidance establishes mechanisms/responsibilities; surveys describe answers from their stated populations. Original practitioner input will add the case-specific need, consequence, response and result.
