# R02 second check: historical continuity, changed conditions and residual work

28 October 2026. Internal research challenge. Five candidate claims and three new primary source routes accompany this memo. No canonical records or client documents were changed; no external contact occurred.

## Judgment

R02's nine-area outcome map survives the historical challenge. It needs two refinements: extend ownership beyond employer/provider to external institutions where relevant, and distinguish prevention of new incidents from correction of existing records and consequences. The central opportunity is explaining the operating mechanism and the case-specific result, rather than declaring an entirely new problem or repeating historical transformation forecasts.

The earlier general historical review is [preserved here](../../challenge-history/HISTORY-CHALLENGE.md). This pass tests R02's specific needs and consequences. It does not repeat that review or begin the R03 choice-of-response assessment.

## What changed our understanding

### External resolution can be part of an operating need

HMRC's historical review records successful modernisation alongside difficult reconciliation and correction. This qualifies a failure narrative while supporting investigation of dependencies outside the payroll team. Use the original's dated account, not its monetary programme headline as a current employer benchmark. Candidate HIS-C01.

The selected CIPP cases add practical routes involving employers, agents, software and HMRC. The publisher also carries HMRC's statement that most RTI data is correctly allocated. Those accounts identify mechanisms worth testing, without measuring how often they occur. Candidate HIS-C03.

R02 refinement: record who can detect, authorise, change and confirm a correction. Those can be different parties. A need may be real even when neither the payroll employer nor its chosen provider controls the final resolution. Avoid falsely assigning every failure to the available commercial actor.

### Prevention, record correction and consequence closure are separate

One CIPP NHS case reports a software fix while already affected records still needed manual correction. Treat this as a reported example, not proof of permanent prevention or all NHS performance. Candidate HIS-C02.

R02 refinement: for an actual case, distinguish stopping recurrence, clearing old exceptions and closing any employee/account consequence. Measuring only new incident volume can miss a legacy queue; measuring only unresolved legacy cases can obscure a successful prevention change. This improves the analysis without prescribing a purchase.

### Useful information depends on its purpose

ONS/HMRC describes statistical QA, transformations and revisions for employment/earnings outputs. That is a separate use from paying a person correctly. Candidate HIS-C04.

R02 refinement: preserve the intended user, decision, timeliness and transformation boundary for each requested report. Data adequate for one purpose may need additional preparation for another. A data-quality or dashboard claim needs its actual purpose and result, rather than an undifferentiated claim that payroll data is reliable or unreliable.

## Historical conclusions to resist

- Similar themes in 2017 and 2025 demonstrate recurrence in the published conversation, not unchanged frequency, no progress or deterioration. Candidate HIS-C05.
- A need surviving digitisation does not prove technology had no benefit. Continuity and improvements can coexist with expensive exceptions.
- A broad predicted shift from transactions to strategic work does not show what time was actually freed or how it was used.
- Familiarity with a requirement can reduce transition effort, but new data flows, task ownership or case types may create fresh demands. Separate implementation disruption, recurring operating burden and residual backlog.
- Future regulatory preparation is a readiness scenario. Do not recycle an outdated timetable or infer that a software replacement follows automatically.

## Precise recommendations for the integrated R02 synthesis

1. Extend the ownership sentence to payroll, HR, managers, finance, providers and relevant external institutions. Keep the expansion conditional on the case.
2. Add prevention / legacy correction / consequence closure to the correction-capacity row and distinguish these in case interpretation.
3. Add a short historical panel showing recurring needs and changed conditions, without numerical trend bars from incompatible studies.
4. Within existing selective probes, ask which party could actually change the affected record and whether the change prevented recurrence, cleared existing cases or both. Do not add mandatory questionnaire items.
5. Preserve positive arrangements and measurable improvements. Do not headline a deterioration claim, provider shortage or quantified ROI from this material.
6. Carry the three temporal categories into later analysis: transition cost, recurring work and residual consequences. Their relevance and values still need actual cases.
