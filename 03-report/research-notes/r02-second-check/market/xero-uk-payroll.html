Automate your payroll calculations

Xero works out pay, tax and National Insurance (NI) from each employee's tax code, NI category and pension details, plus student loans and directors' NI. 92% of UK small businesses surveyed say they run payroll faster with Xero.* *Source: Xero survey of 254 UK small businesses, May 2024.

Handle workplace pensions on autopilot

Set up automatic enrolment in Xero by adding your duties start date, pension provider, and contribution rates. Xero works out contributions and sends them to Nest or The People’s Pension, and alerts you on the pay run screen when re-enrolment is due. That’s one less job on your list!

  • Set up automatic enrolment
  • Xero shows each employee's eligibility and enrolment status, and alerts you on the pay run screen when re-enrolment is due.
  • Send contributions to your provider
  • Contributions go to Nest or The People's Pension through a direct connection.

Wondering how statutory pay adds up?

Family leave and sick days need careful sums. Xero calculates statutory sick, maternity, paternity, shared parental, adoption, and bereavement pay, plus small employers' relief. It handles Construction Industry Scheme (CIS) deductions and IR35 workers too, and your team can check payslips in Xero Me.

  • Calculate statutory pay for leave and sickness
  • Apply small employers' relief where it's due
