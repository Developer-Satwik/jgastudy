# R08 second challenge: a decision brief must keep its evidence basis visible

29October2026. Internal preparatory desktop second challenge; independent integrated review passed. No new source route, original participant or market estimate. Seventeen bounded guidance/application records extend canonical reuses; they do not create seventeen new studies.

## Result

The six-view framework remains useful as an evidence-informed working structure. The challenge narrows two ambiguous points: “at this time” now identifies the selected decision/event time rather than silently importing later knowledge, and accepted evidence is explicitly different from verified or independently corroborated claims. The brief-writing pattern now says “the account reports” or “the available evidence supports,” according to its actual basis.

Five optional conditions add precision without expanding the questionnaire: event-time knowledge, the object/period of approval, evidence fit to the person/service/outcome, qualification beyond acceptance, and divergent accounts. Practitioner usefulness, causal influence on approval and commercial value remain untested.

## Historical and counterfactual lenses

LFB's earlier account and2025reset concern different events in one continuing programme. The later account cannot replace what was known or feasible at the earlier event. A Scottish Government audit separately reports migration signoff, conditional backing and final go-live with known issues and assurances, followed by audit appraisal. These scoped judgments do not yield a universal risk threshold, an optimal release decision or a current2026operational result. CLM-0429–CLM-0432.

The framework now makes this timing distinction explicit. A later source may legitimately change today's interpretation, but it does not prove an earlier decision-maker knew the same facts. Neither decision is scored as right/wrong from hindsight. Existing target-date and programme/component controls remain.

## Market and operating-service lens

Cintra's documentation describes recurring submission/report/payment actions according to its declared route. These are not the same as the investment/change decision. Its cycle page now carries an October2026update label, but cross-article visibility/payment-stage wording remains inconsistent; CLM-0218 stays held. No UI, customer account, payment or automatic sequence was tested. CLM-0433, CLM-0435.

HMRC's source-scoped provider guidance distinguishes purchased support from retained employer inputs/records and responsibilities. This is not a full legal audit or a determination about an actual contract/employer. The analytical implication is selective: identify which task/run/period an approval or accepted handoff concerns, then preserve unknown payment, ongoing work and next-period readiness. One accepted report set cannot settle all those facts. CLM-0434, CLM-0436.

## Method, affected-party and disagreement lenses

QQE supports purpose-fit judgement, source provenance, whose appraisal is reported, divergent criteria and traceable interpretation. It is not certification of this framework or a mandatory indicator list. A contributor's report need not be dismissed merely because no other source corroborates it; equally, acceptance cannot stand in for independent verification. CLM-0442–CLM-0444.

Where accounts differ, first inspect object, time and criterion. A platform owner and an affected user could describe different tasks or outcomes. If a material disagreement remains, preserve it with alternative explanations rather than average it away. This is an analytical possibility, not a claim that participants disagree. No recontact, stakeholder census or numerical confidence rating is required. CLM-0445.
