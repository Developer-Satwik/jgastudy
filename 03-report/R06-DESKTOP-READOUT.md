# R06: why payroll choices stay, change, pause or stop

29 October 2026. Internal desktop research and independent integrated review completed. Original practitioner integration, client scope review and final publication checks remain open.

## Main conclusion

The useful explanation is not simply why a buyer failed to switch. Establish **what was being decided, what changed or stayed, the dated decision and delivery positions, the stated reasons and what happened next**. Retention can be a good option, a constrained continuation, or a mixture. A proposed change can be approved while rollout is delayed; a component can be cancelled while another delivers useful service. Neither a supplier sales-stage label nor survey intent supplies that history.

The practical output is an [outcome-specific barrier and response matrix](R06-BARRIER-RESPONSE-MATRIX.md), supported by the [structured event map](R06-outcome-map.json). Nine optional analysis rows do not create new interview questions, a numerical score, an employer workflow or a supplier-conversion recipe. Existing Q006/Q007/Q012/Q013 already separate recent case, disposition, option and condition; Q008–Q011 supply trigger, rights and evidence.

## Staying and changing need an object

The selected primary public cases expose several materially different choices:

| Dated context | What is supported | What is not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warwickshire, 15 October 2026 | Public meeting agrees restricted recommendations; public purpose retains platform while changing proposed commercial route. | Signed/executed contract, full restricted rationale and later outcome. Public call-in labels differ. CLM-0344. |
| Sandwell, 14 October 2026 | CombinedERP renewal resolution explicitly considers lapse/replacement and continuity/time. | Complete execution, independent benefit or a universal replacement duration. CLM-0345. |
| Cumberland, 13 March 2025 | Procurement permission within service disaggregation, with exhausted extensions, time and differing requirements. | Named product/provider, completed supplier switch or go-live. The website “Implemented” label is a decision-record status. CLM-0346. |
| OEP, December 2024 reported in a January 2025 boardpaper | Staff-record transition and successful December payroll processing by a new provider, with refinement planned. | Original approval/selection rationale, independent accuracy tests, full benefit or later performance. CLM-0364. |

The first three contexts were inspected through original web readers with selected paraphrased notes retained; raw retrieval returned 403. They are not labelled retained raw originals. Restricted recommendations were not accessed. OEP is a direct employer management update, not an independent audit of payroll results.

These examples support recording the platform, work/service scope, commercial route, organisational boundary and retained work where relevant. Keeping one while changing another is coherent. Do not require a full component map for a small choice. CLM-0347.

A continuity constraint can make continuation reasonable at that point without establishing permanent inability to change or complete satisfaction. Service merit and constraints can coexist. Do not infer psychological inertia, an unreported later tender or a supplier intervention that would certainly have won the business. CLM-0348.

## Delay, component cancellation and notice status are different

West Sussex’s 10 March 2026 official communication postponed planned HR/payroll implementation from April to October and described scale, reliable payroll and schools/term-time context. A 13 October update then declared specified employee self-service access live, with support and historical-payslip limits. Those two sources are one programme, not two independent employer cases. CLM-0349–0350.

This is a dated rollout history. A postponed implementation does not by itself establish a deferred investment approval, and a later self-service declaration is not proof of complete payroll delivery, accuracy, savings or that waiting caused a better result. Keep each object's state and source date visible.

NIAO’s 2004 summary records cancellation of a payroll component in May 2001 while personnel/pensions components delivered benefits. It also reports that equipment needed by continuing components complicated termination of the existing arrangement. This historical audit summary illustrates component and exit-dependency boundaries. It is outside the recent 24-month practitioner window, not a current employer pattern; the full underlying report/contract was not inspected. No historic monetary figures or claim that earlier cancellation would have been superior is adopted. CLM-0351–0352.

The Education Payroll New Zealand portal labels an advance information notice cancelled. Its body explicitly says it does not commence procurement or commit to an RFP/award. The cancellation date/reason and later upgrade route are unknown. This is a narrow publication-status example outside the proposed UK population, not evidence of a cancelled tender or abandoned payroll upgrade. CLM-0353.

A stop or pause therefore needs its actual object, date, stated basis and next position. A proposed remedy, adopted action and observed effect remain separate. CLM-0354.

## Buyer research helps define questions, not fill in decisions

The published studies supply useful distinctions but do not reconstruct actual proposal histories:

- CIPP 2024 distinguishes recent implementation, current tendering, future exploration and no change with satisfaction. A response label is not a verified renewal, approved budget or within-employer transition history. CLM-0355–0356.
- Moorepay's experienced switching ease and anticipated reasons for not switching concern differently routed populations. They cannot be combined into a conversion rate or used to diagnose irrational staying. Existing chart/prose/guide conflicts remain held under CLM-0051/0052; no disputed amount/direction is reused. CLM-0357–0358.
- Azets’ 2026 E2 asks what would trigger change. That prospective wording cannot become a past-switching explanation because a companion article sounds retrospective. Separate importance/premium questions also do not establish available budget, a specified price commitment or a purchase. CLM-0359–0360.
- SD Worx separates current arrangements from preferred arrangements over three years. Preference is not approved spend, migration or an observed future outcome. CLM-0361.

The Azets original describes a May 2026 pooled survey of 205 senior/C-suite/payroll decision-makers across six markets, with 52 UK respondents. Its method also documents the large-employer profile and four separate commentary interviews. Overall country sample counts are available; item-valid counts/routing, recruitment/weights and country-specific item results remain unestablished. The report was commissioned by Azets and conducted by Reputation Leaders. It is not automatically representative of UK payroll employers. These are method descriptors, not market findings; no trigger/premium/preference percentage or forecast is adopted.

Independent source review found the full method page 32 after the first extraction focused on the opening sample statement. Country bases, commissioned researcher, employer profile and the separate interviews were corrected before canonical merge. The review preserves unknown item bases rather than incorrectly saying all country information is unavailable.

## A reasoned stop can be a legitimate result

GDS discovery guidance investigates the problem, constraints and alternatives before commitment and allows stopping when that investigation supports it. This is public-service guidance, not evidence every declined payroll proposal was wise or a private-employer phase requirement. The R06 application is to establish what stopped, what need remained and what alternative or reconsideration was actually supported. CLM-0362.

HMRC's current RTI guidance supplies one concrete transition mechanism: payroll-ID changes without correct linkage can produce duplicate employment records and disputed charges. This is relevant evidence for a specific data/continuity condition, not a measured probability, a universal reason for delay or a rule to wait until year-end. No employer's actual reason or successful mitigation is inferred from general guidance. CLM-0363.

## How to make the analysis useful

For each actual case, retain the original words and knowledge basis. Record the selected/retained option separately from the specific decision position, then delivery and benefit. Preserve successive decisions and mixed components rather than force a single whole-project label. No status is inferred from silence. Continued service alone is not a recent deliberate renewal. CLM-0365.

The unchanged Q007 dispositions are approved, declined, deferred, withdrawn/abandoned, pending, no formal approval required, not known and prefer not to disclose. A statement about a rollout or a procurement notice may not establish any investment disposition. Pre-proposal needs, older-only context, uncertain eligibility and refusal follow the existing Q006 route, not an automatic indecision label.

Possible responses can include a better-defined option, more relevant evidence, practical transition/retained-work support, staged scope, revised timing or accepting an appropriate stop. Whether any response was relevant, adopted and effective must be established in the case. Supplier advice is not observed successful mitigation, and some constraints should be respected rather than overcome to obtain a sale. CLM-0366.

**For practitioners:** clarify the real choice and unresolved condition, retain a workable incumbent where appropriate and establish evidence for the next specific step. **For suppliers:** fit the service, evidence and transition support to the buyer's actual scope and timing. **For JGA:** explain how needs become action or a reasoned non-change without treating every gap as a recruitment opportunity.

## Evidence gaps and next module

Current private-employer explicit declines/withdrawals, hidden alternatives and actual remedy effects remain gaps for permissioned practitioner cases. Not measured, not known, not applicable and not disclosed remain different; none means zero benefit or failure.
