## Judgment

The first-pass decision-object approach holds, but it needs five selective refinements. A dated job title is still insufficient: establish the remit effective then, the organisational layer, what was intended versus exercised, the object being accepted and any relevant affected-party condition. These are fields for interpreting actual cases, not a new approval ladder or extra mandatory questions.

The current [R04 readout](R04-DESKTOP-READOUT.md), [pathway](R04-DECISION-PATHWAY.md) and [structured map](R04-decision-map.json) now carry these refinements. ## The strongest corrections to tempting interpretations

**A reporting line is not a transfer of powers.** A role advert can specify provider oversight or local resource allocation without establishing a spending envelope, contract-signature right or what happened after appointment. An interim or successor may have a bounded remit. Keep effective period and handover visible when relevant rather than assume a complete transfer.

**Actual practice and described authority can differ.** In the Chazey-published Herbalife account, supplier implementation calls and later client governance are the adviser's account of practice. This justifies examining both what was meant to happen and what the contributor actually observed. It does not establish a legal breach or that providers generally overstep.

**There is no supported simple small-versus-large buying rule.** The inspected founder-targeted page supplies useful direct involvement and operational permissions, but not a complete buying process or verified headcount. A global case supplies another context, not evidence that all large employers require committees. Simple, delegated, shared and retained arrangements remain possible.

**More approvals are not automatically better governance.** The 2026 public material deliberately separates revised approvals from continuing assurance. It provides a counterpoint to a compulsory committee model, without proving that the reform was implemented everywhere or improved outcomes. Older governance records cannot be treated as the route still effective in 2026.

**Approval needs an object.** Approving hours for worker payment/invoicing is different from accepting a project deliverable or commissioning a service. A helpful operational contact might hold neither supplier-selection nor contracting rights. The existing R04 distinction between payroll approval, payment, migration and go-live remains intact.

The independent verification passed 243 checks covering provenance, IDs, text/hashes, index metadata, four-tool/two-repeat coverage and retained source-status limitations. ## Practical value for the report

For practitioners, the case record will identify the next actual decision and who can take it within the current remit, including a valid simple or retained-incumbent route. For suppliers, it prevents confusing user support, acceptance of work, implementation control and purchase authority. For JGA, it can make leadership/cover briefs more precise about mandate, scope and handover without making hiring the research's default conclusion.

The map retains twelve optional functions and the fourteen-question core. Five cross-cutting conditions are selective; a respondent does not need to reconstruct a corporate hierarchy or produce confidential documents. The framework remains an analytical design until original cases refine it. No recruitment quota, new respondent cohort, power score or validated buyer archetype is added.
