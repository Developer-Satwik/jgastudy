# The Table exploratory conversation guides

Version 0.1 • 28 October 2026 • Internal draft for user review

These guides support up to four exploratory conversations, ideally two potential commercial buyers and two prospective council members. They are not evidence that conversations occurred or a substitute for six comparable-model reviews. Confirm the concept, participant arrangements and introduction route before use. Count unique eligible practitioner contributors separately; overlapping membership requires permission and actual practitioner evidence.

## Buyer guide

Explain that JGA is assessing a commercial research offer and that this conversation will inform whether and how it should proceed. Ask B01 and B02 before showing the prospectus, to reduce the effect of the concept on recall. Do not ask for confidential plans, exact budgets or names of internal staff.

1. B01 What recent product, commercial or strategy decision required a better understanding of payroll buyers? Establish the concrete decision and the person's involvement.
2. B02 How did you obtain that insight, and what was useful or missing? Probe actual research purchases and free alternatives before hypothetical preferences.
3. B03 After reviewing this prospectus, which parts, if any, would help a decision you face? What duplicates material you already have? Seek a specific use rather than general approval.
4. B04 What evidence and context would you need to trust and use the findings? Probe seniority, organisation types, geography, recency, recruitment, method and the limits of a small qualitative cohort.
5. B05 How would an organisation like yours assess paying for this? Establish budget ownership, approval, expected use and required licence rights. Distinguish internal use from external redistribution, quotation, lead generation or exclusivity. Do not imply those rights are included or controlled by us yet.
6. B06 What would have to be true before a defined proposal was worth reviewing? Establish the next decision, who needs to be involved, timing and reasons not to proceed.

Only after unprompted needs and a research-only response, explain two alternatives in a consistent order: Model A, a defined research licence; Model B, research supported by a council/event programme. Rotate or record presentation order in subsequent conversations if feasible. Ask what the event adds and whether research alone would have value. Do not conflate research licensing with access to participant identities or sales introductions. Do not supply a price anchor until the actual product and rights are defined; later price-testing must be versioned.

## Prospective council member guide

Explain that this is feasibility work for a proposed council, not an invitation to a booked event. The working concept is a senior payroll peer group contributing questions and experience to research, with possible private-dining discussions. Planned member attendance and research access would be free, subject to the eventual model. No venue, date, recurring commitment, recognition or sponsor access is confirmed.

1. M01 Which payroll decisions or challenges would make peer research useful to you? Ask for a recent example before describing benefits.
2. M02 Which possible benefits would be worthwhile, and which would add little value? Explore research access, peer exchange and optional recognition separately without assuming interest.
3. M03 Would contributing an industry question and some research input be practical? If yes, what question and mode would suit you? An expression of interest is not consent or enrolment.
4. M04 What conditions would make the peer group useful and comfortable? Probe relevant seniority, balance of perspectives, vendor involvement, confidentiality and use of contributions.
5. M05 What would make participation practical or impractical? Explore time, travel, employer permission, accessibility and hospitality rules; do not collect unnecessary personal details.
6. M06 If no event took place, would contributing to and receiving the research still be worthwhile? Ask what a dinner or live discussion would add.

## Capture and interpretation

Record participant role and eligibility in approved restricted storage; keep only appropriate pseudonymous analytical references here. For each response retain guide/concept version, date, actual use case, alternatives, evidence needs, decision authority, conditions, objections and any permitted next step. Distinguish exact quotation, paraphrase and researcher interpretation. Keep skip/not known/not applicable/prefer not to disclose separate.

Do not convert enthusiasm into a sale or willingness to pay. Separate research-only interest from event interest, hypothetical willingness from actual previous purchasing, a referral from a buying decision, and a requested proposal from a committed contract. No partner or participant is contacted from this draft.
