# WP03 operational collection routes

29 October 2026. Internal working design for the parent collection pack. No person has been introduced, verified, contacted, interviewed or recorded through this file. No collection platform or participant register has been activated.

## Scope and the practical unit

The report targets 6–10 distinct verified senior payroll professionals. The Table permits up to four exploratory conversations, ideally two commercial buyers and two prospective council members. These are different units: people, contribution records, Table conversations and publication permissions must be tracked separately.

Use one stable `P` participant reference across both workstreams and a separate `INT` reference for each contribution/session. The approved restricted environment holds the identity lookup and permissions. The ordinary project workspace receives no identifiable records. Nothing about a role title, a JGA introduction or accepting a call establishes professional eligibility, permission or a completed contribution by itself.

The proposed UK-relevant employer-side population and current guide remain unapproved. Do not extend the cohort to recruitment providers merely because their public material is useful context. A suitable public professional profile or JGA confirmation can support eligibility, but the record must establish actual relevant remit and the verification basis. Do not ask for identity documents or employee records.

## Separate the report count from the Table count

| Record or count | What qualifies | What does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduced/contactable person | An appropriately shared introduction through the agreed route. | A completed response, consent or report contribution. |
| Verified report-eligible person | Relevant senior payroll remit/involvement and a recorded professional verification basis within the client-confirmed scope. | An impressive title or unexamined profile match. |
| Completed report contribution | Actual substantive input on the report's topics, linked to the guide/version and questions covered, with permitted collection/analysis use and appropriate note quality. | An appointment, a greeting, only a council RSVP, or a general expression of interest. |
| Distinct usable report contributor | Verified eligible person with an actual contribution usable for the intended analysis; count that person once across modes or sessions. | One person counted twice for written input plus a call, or once per role/workstream. |
| Completed Table conversation | An actual exploratory conversation/contribution event covering the buyer or member purpose, with mode/version, covered questions and permission recorded. | A referral, invitation, scheduled meeting, buyer title or attendance interest alone. |
| Material cleared for publication | The specific text/aggregate/context and attribution treatment fit the recorded permission and contextual identification review. | General participation permission treated as approval for a named quote, employer identification, council recognition or sponsor contact. |

Do not impose a fixed number of answered questions or require every optional probe to make a contribution usable. Record what was actually answered and its limitations. A report-eligible person without a recent decision can still supply useful operating/priorities input under the guide. The final question base and publication-clearance base can be smaller than the distinct-contributor count.

For overlap, one prospective member may also count once as a report contributor only if eligibility, actual report-relevant input and the relevant permission are present. A buyer does not become a payroll practitioner because they buy payroll research. A single combined session can cover both purposes transparently; it is one Table conversation event, not two because the person has two roles. Two substantive Table conversations with the same person are two events but still one person; do not use repeated sessions to conceal the four-conversation cap. Log short factual clarification separately and explain its treatment rather than inventing an automatic counting exemption.

## Collection lifecycle

1. **Prepare and clear the route.** Confirm the client focus/population, exact guide versions, introduction route and the separate collection/data arrangements. Prepare the introduction brief and participant information. User permission is required before sending anything.
2. **Introduce without collecting more than needed.** Ask for relevant remit and a route to participation. Do not ask Nick to send sensitive case histories, employee records or private budgets as an introduction. No contact details enter this workspace.
3. **Confirm eligibility and understanding.** In the approved environment, establish actual professional remit and verification basis, intended report/Table roles and the purposes explained. Do not collect a whole employer dossier.
4. **Record the actual choices.** Participation/analysis, recording if proposed, publication treatment, named quotation, employer identification, recognition and recontact are distinct. The participant can decline an optional use without being pressured to accept it. Missing permission is not a “yes.” Root's data plan must settle the notice, processing instructions and any release/withdrawal process before use.
5. **Collect a proportionate contribution.** Interview or equivalent written response can use the unchanged report guide. Notes are the working default; recording is disabled unless separately approved and agreed. The guide's earlier 30–40-minute estimate is untested, not a promise; confirm the practical time/mode before scheduling. Table guides remain distinct and disclose feasibility/commercial purpose.
6. **Write and review the note.** Preserve question IDs, routed wording, dates, mode/version, source of knowledge, exact quote versus paraphrase and missingness. Record scope/conditions and contrary evidence. R08's views are selective analysis aids, not a second questionnaire read aloud.
7. **Separate use from release.** Link observations to the permitted analysis and later public wording. Record agreed factual/quotation checks and follow-up limits. A contribution can be analysed without every quotation being publishable; do not publish a named or identifiable account by default.
8. **Close and count accurately.** Update distinct people, contribution events and Table conversation events separately. Record any gap or shortfall for client review; do not replace absent contributions with public cases or the earlier AI prompt study. Retention/closure instructions come from the agreed data arrangement, not an invented period in this route design.

## The existing report routes

| Situation | Route under guide version 0.2 | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A qualifying recent case emerges | Confirm the selected case and proceed with Q007–Q013; Q001–Q006 and Q014 retain their existing roles. | Record the actual event/object/date and direct involvement, not every event in the programme. |
| A need was never raised internally | If willing, use the existing Q006 pre-proposal clarification, then Q014 unless a qualifying case emerges. | Do not classify the need as pending, declined or deferred. |
| No recent case but useful operating/priorities input | Keep Q001–Q005 and the willing Q006 clarification; move to Q014. | No-case does not mean no value or ineligible if the person's remit otherwise qualifies. |
| Only older or uncertain history | Label it separately as context; do not force it into the recent-case base. | An event within the recent window can qualify inside an older programme, but its substantive event must be established. |
| Person declines case detail or a question | Stop that request and use a permitted remaining route, if they wish to continue. | Do not rephrase repeatedly to obtain what they declined. |
| Written contribution | Use the same core/version, with relevant questions and selective follow-up. | Written mode does not mean every probe must be answered or that comparable depth is guaranteed. |
| The participant identifies no unmet need or prefers retention | Preserve the account. | Do not manufacture a problem or treat recruitment/purchase as the expected outcome. |

Keep `not_asked`, `not_answered`, `not_known`, `not_applicable`, `not_measured`, `not_yet_assessed` and `prefer_not_to_disclose` distinct where relevant. The decision, selected option, delivery and benefit fields keep their own guide definitions. Blank input does not default to pending or failure.

## Minimum blank-record specification

This is a specification, not a request to populate personal data here. Use the approved environment after the gate.

- **Restricted participant lookup:** participant reference; minimal contact/identity reference; actual professional remit; eligibility/verification basis and date; report/buyer/member roles; introduction source. Store no identity document.
- **Restricted permission record:** participant reference; information/notice and purpose versions; actual permission/choice evidence and date; allowed analysis/publication/attribution treatment; optional recording, quote, employer, recognition and recontact choices; agreed checks/restrictions; relevant permission-change reference. No choice is pre-ticked.
- **Contribution note:** `INT` and `P` references; report/Table purposes actually covered; date/mode/guide/concept versions; question IDs and routed wording; source of knowledge; exact quote/paraphrase/analyst interpretation labels; missingness; relevant safe context; permission reference and note-review status.
- **Analytical observation:** contribution/question references; permission/use limitation; scoped observation; quote/paraphrase type; module/code references; source/knowledge basis; contradiction or qualification; release status. Avoid unnecessarily identifying combinations of descriptors.
- **Count/release view:** distinct eligible people, actual contribution events, Table events, analysis usability and separate publication clearance; keep counts reconciled without exporting identities.
