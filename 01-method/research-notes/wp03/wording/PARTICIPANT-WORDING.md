## Participant information core

Use this short information block with the separately approved privacy and handling information before asking someone to decide. It is an introduction to the participation choice, not a substitute for the complete notice.

JGA Recruitment is commissioning The Payroll Investment and Transformation Report 2027, with research support from Riseklix. We would value your perspective on payroll needs and a decision you were involved in, including improvements that progressed, sensible renewals, or changes that did not go ahead.

You can contribute through a short structured conversation or written responses. We will agree a practical format with you. Participation is voluntary: you can skip questions, decline to discuss a case or stop the discussion. Please avoid employee payroll records, confidential documents, exact private budgets and information you are not authorised to share.

The research will inform JGA’s report and related research material, which may be published and licensed commercially. Identifiable responses will be treated confidentially. We will review contextual details before using a de-identified summary, since removing a name does not always prevent someone being recognised.

Recording, direct quotations, your name, employer identification and future contact each require separate agreement. Taking part does not enrol you in The Table, commit you to an event or purchase, or give sponsors access to you. We will provide the privacy and handling information before you decide whether to take part.

Internal release note: do not describe the intended publication as solely academic or non-commercial. “Related research material” must be defined in the approved notice and permission record, with any future-edition reuse separately specified. Do not infer broad reuse permission from accepting the interview.

## Separate participation and use choices

These are optional permission prompts for an operator to select where relevant, not additional research questions or one bundled mandatory form. Record the actual wording, scope, answer and date in the approved restricted permission register. No choice is preselected. A participant’s agreement to take part is not automatically the project’s data-protection lawful basis.

| Choice | Suggested plain wording | Scope to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participation and notes | Are you happy to take part on the basis of the information provided, with us taking written notes or using the written response you choose to provide? | Identify the information/notice version and mode. No agreement is inferred from an introduction, attendance or receiving the invitation. |
| Recording, only if proposed | Would you be comfortable with a recording for the agreed research purpose? It is optional; we can use written notes instead. | Ask only after the actual recording tool, access, purpose and handling information are approved and explained. A refusal does not prevent a notes-only route. Do not assume permission to send audio to a transcription or AI service. |
| De-identified paraphrase | May we use a de-identified summary of this contribution in the JGA report and the specific related research uses described in the information? Are there contextual details you want left out or reviewed first? | Publication/licensing scope must be specific. Check indirect identification and permissions before release. Research participation without usable publication permission may still have a different analytical/counting status; do not invent one here. |
| Exact quotation | May we use this exact passage as a quotation for the described report use? We can show you the proposed wording and context before it is released. | Quote text and context require their own record; a general willingness to be quoted does not approve every later extract. An edited paraphrase is not an exact quote. |
| Name and role | Would you like this approved quotation or contribution to carry your name and the role wording we show you, or should we keep identifying details out? | Store the exact approved display text and permitted use. No response means no named attribution. Context may still identify someone even without a name. |
| Employer identification | May the approved contribution identify your employer in the wording we show you, and are there any employer restrictions we need to respect? | Do not infer this from approval to use the person’s name. Do not ask the participant to disclose restricted information or promise authority they do not have. |
| Contribution clarification | May we contact you about a factual clarification or the proposed wording of this contribution? | A limited research/quote clarification choice, not future marketing, a wider research subscription or permission for sponsors to contact the person. |
| Future research invitation | Would you like us to invite you separately if a later research opportunity is relevant? | Optional and separately scoped. It does not permit reuse of this contribution in a future edition unless that use is also specified and cleared. |
| The Table discussion | Separately, would you be interested in receiving information about an exploratory discussion on the proposed Table council? | Interest is not consent to join, an event booking, recognition, recurring participation, hospitality or sponsor access. Use the appropriate member guide, or buyer guide if the actual purpose is commercial discovery. |

Do not present a general “agree to all” choice. Named attribution, employer identification, quotation, recording, future contact and Table interest can differ. Declining one is not a refusal of every other option. If a choice is unclear, record not yet agreed rather than assume approval.

## Withdrawal, corrections and sharing language to finish

No participant-facing withdrawal deadline is invented in these drafts. The approved information must explain how to ask a question, correct a contribution, change a permission or request withdrawal, and what practical limits apply after a report or licensed output has been distributed. Do not promise unlimited withdrawal or that already distributed copies can always be recalled.

The working agreement provides a contract-level return/deletion arrangement: at JGA’s choice, participant personal data is returned or securely deleted within 30 days after completion or termination, with remaining backups deleted within 90 days where technically necessary, except required protected legal records. That is not automatically the participant-facing research retention wording, a confirmed publication cutoff, or evidence that the executed version has been reconciled. Use it as the working contractual baseline while JGA confirms the actual instructions and notice.

For participant information processed on JGA’s behalf, the working section 12 identifies JGA as controller and Riseklix as processor acting on documented instructions. Each party’s own business-contact, invoicing and legally required records are treated separately. A materially different research role must be documented before collection; these invitation drafts do not approve one.

Do not promise absolute anonymity. Use confidential handling and reviewed de-identification language with the actual intended recipients and output rights. Do not promise participants access to a finished report, compensation, a place on a council or a guaranteed commercial outcome unless separately agreed.
