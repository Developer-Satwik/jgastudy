# WP03 data arrangements: contract baseline and remaining operating decisions

29 October 2026. Internal preparation for the collection pack. Five primary HTML pages and the generated agreement were inspected. This is not a final participant notice, legal determination, tool-security audit or authorisation to collect data.

## Carry through the existing agreement

Section 12 of the generated agreement already describes JGA as controller and Riseklix as processor for participant information processed on JGA's behalf. Each retains responsibility for its own business-contact, invoicing and legally required records. A materially different research role must be documented before collection. The user reports signature; the executed copy has not been independently retrieved.

That contractual arrangement is the working baseline, not a blank item to renegotiate. Actual purposes and responsibilities still need to follow it. The [ICO role guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/controllers-and-processors/controllers-and-processors-a-guide/) explains why activity matters as well as labels; the live page is marked under review following legislative changes. We do not infer a different role from the fact that Riseklix exercises analytical judgement.

The same section already requires:

- Documented lawful instructions and proportionate security, restricted access and identity/analysis separation.
- JGA's prior written authorisation and appropriate safeguards before an external recording, transcription or AI service receives identifiable or confidential research data.
- Tools, recipients, hosting and access locations recorded with JGA before collection.
- Any required international-transfer safeguards and assessment before relevant access outside the UK. The agreement is not an IDTA.
- At JGA's choice, return or secure deletion within 30 days after completion or termination; technically necessary remaining backups deleted within 90 days, except legally retained protected records.

The 30/90-day provisions are not a newly selected retention policy or a completed deletion event. Record the actual trigger and JGA's choice. JGA's own retention and participant-facing explanation still need confirmation. The agreement text path, hash and precise section references are in [source-review.json](source-review.json).

## Proposed minimal route

Prepare for a short conversation or equivalent written response using the existing core questions. Recommend manual notes, no recording and no external AI/transcription upload by default. These are proposed operating choices, not settings changed or tools approved.

Keep professional identity, contact and permission records in an approved restricted environment outside this workspace. Use only appropriately cleared analysis in its approved environment. A participant ID alone does not make a response anonymous. Do not use ordinary project files, an available connector or an existing personal account as proof of suitable storage.

Where the transfer arrangement is not ready, section 12 allows JGA to retain identifiable contacts/responses and provide genuinely anonymised material. That fallback requires an actual assessment; names removed, a withheld mapping key or UK server hosting alone do not settle it. The [ICO transfer guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/a-guide-to-international-transfers/are-we-making-a-restricted-transfer/) addresses remote access, service entities and pseudonymised information. No mechanism or compliance result is selected here.

## Keep participant choices and data basis distinct

The [ICO consent page](https://ico.org.uk/consent), updated 23 October 2026, distinguishes ordinary agreement from consent as a data-protection basis. Do not automatically pick a basis because someone agrees to an interview, accepts a quotation or signs a form. No scientific-research exemption or special privilege is assumed for this commercial commission.

The notice must explain the actual arrangement, rather than promise unlimited withdrawal, absolute anonymity or a publication cut-off we have not agreed. The [privacy-information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/) supplies useful notice categories, but its live transfer row retains legacy EU/European-Commission wording. That row is not copied as current UK transfer instruction; use the dedicated transfer guidance and actual route.

The [MRS B2B landing page](https://www.mrs.org.uk/standards/mrs-guidelines-for-businesstobusiness-research-insight-and-other-data-processing-activities) was inspected for its stated scope and status only. The linked full PDF was not inspected in this strand. No detailed rule, MRS membership or accreditation is claimed.
