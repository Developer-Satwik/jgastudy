# Work packages and research questions

Do each substantive investigation thoroughly, then update its memo, registers and status. Other researchers can challenge evidence or explore a bounded source question in parallel; that does not mean every main module is being completed at once.

## Report modules

| ID | Problem and decision | Required evidence and alternative view | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | What existing research already explains, and what would make this publication useful | Original methods, topics and populations of current studies; existing free and paid substitutes; evidence that an alleged gap is already served | Landscape map and specific differentiation hypotheses |
| R02 | Which operational needs and service gaps have material consequences | Workload, accuracy, reporting, capacity, skills, data and supplier examples; also functioning processes and satisfied teams | Need → consequence → desired outcome matrix |
| R03 | Which technology or operating-model change is worth considering | AI awareness versus deployed use, integration/data requirements, outsourcing/in-house trade-offs, measured outcomes; unsuccessful and successful implementations | Capability and trade-off map with terms defined |
| R04 | Who turns a payroll need into an organisational decision | Actual sponsor, budget, approval and veto roles; procurement path; differences between seniority and decision authority | Evidence-led decision pathway |
| R05 | What evidence, funding and implementation conditions make a case investable | ROI/TCO, compliance/risk, continuity, migration, integration, capacity and alternatives; benefits that are not readily monetised | Business-case evidence and risk checklist |
| R06 | Why buyers stay, switch, defer, reject or abandon | Recent actual choices, switching costs, dissatisfaction without action, renewal success, stalled/rejected business cases | Outcome-specific barrier and response matrix |
| R07 | What priorities are funded and what contextual differences are supported | Desire, intention, approved spend and delivery status; defensible size/sector/region contrasts; missing or sparse groups | Contextual priorities table and evidence gaps |
| R08 | How unmet needs connect to investment and supplier action | Synthesis of previous modules and practitioner cases, with contrary cases | From Ambition to Investment chapter and repeatable Decision Framework |

R02 to R07 each ends with implications for practitioners, suppliers and JGA's publication. Do not assume a difference between groups if data do not permit one. R08 must not convert a qualitative framework into an invented numerical Confidence Index.

## The Table modules

| ID | Problem and decision | Required evidence and alternative view | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T01 | What business mechanisms already serve this audience | Six varied comparable models, current offers, rights, prices if public and free substitutes | Comparable-model matrix with unknowns explicit |
| T02 | Who would buy and why; why leaders would participate | Buyer jobs, member needs and current alternatives; reasons not to buy or join | Buyer/member hypotheses and proposition options |
| T03 | Whether introduced buyers and members find a defined proposition useful | Up to four exploratory conversations, ideally two buyers and two members; actual budget/authority/next steps versus enthusiasm | Structured validation notes and offer changes |
| T04 | Whether events improve the research-only model enough | Incremental research depth, repeatability, commercial benefits and operating burden; a no-events alternative | Model A/B comparison |
| T05 | What the pilot and annual programme cost and when cash is needed | Units, source inputs, staff time, FX, tax/service, deposits, sales/renewal assumptions and downside cases | Auditable financial workbook |
| T06 | How a funded UK pilot could launch at sensible exposure | Partner rights/benefits, member proposition, venue brief, funding and operating prerequisites | Partner package, invitation and pilot plan |
| T07 | Whether to proceed, revise or pause | Findings from T01–T06, unresolved assumptions and explicit thresholds | Executive recommendation and phased 90-day roadmap |
