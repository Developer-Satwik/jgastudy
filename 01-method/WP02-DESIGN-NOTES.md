## Instrument design

The proposed core guide will include short role/context items and substantive questions about needs, a recent decision, evidence, authority, constraints, alternatives and future priorities. Optional probes support depth; they are not a requirement to ask every probe of every contributor.

A recent-case recall window of 24 months is proposed. Record the collection date and actual decision period. A practitioner without such a case can inform needs/priorities but does not enter the actual-decision-case base.

Keep three coding dimensions separate:

1. Decision disposition: approved, declined, deferred, withdrawn, pending or no formal decision.
2. Chosen option: renew/retain incumbent, alternative provider/platform, internal process change, deliberate no change, mixed or none selected.
3. Delivery status: not started, underway, live/completed, paused, stopped or not applicable.

An approved renewal is therefore not misclassified as the opposite of approval. Approval does not establish delivery, and desired future work is not counted as funded investment.

## Burden and testing

The scheduling estimate is provisional and untested. A short conversation or equivalent structured written contribution is possible. Before invitation, confirm the actual mode and time requested. An internal route walkthrough is a structural check, not a cognitive interview or evidence that participants understand the guide.

Use selective probes and let contributors skip questions. Test comprehension, outcome labels, recall, flow and timing with suitable people when possible. Material wording changes are versioned. A pilot contribution counts only if eligible, permissioned and analytically comparable.
