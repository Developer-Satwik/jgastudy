# Original practitioner and commercial input

Working design. No recruitment, interview, collection platform or participant record has been created yet.

## Separate purposes and counts

The report targets 6–10 distinct verified senior payroll professionals. The Table includes up to four exploratory conversations, ideally two potential commercial buyers and two prospective senior council members. Eligible and permissioned council conversations may count toward the report cohort as well. A single person has one participant ID even if contributing to both streams.

Track separately: people introduced, eligibility verified, accepted, scheduled, contribution completed, contribution usable, and permission for publication. Do not report introductions as completed responses or count multiple conversations with one person as multiple participants.

## Recruitment brief to prepare

Ask Nick for people with relevant senior decision responsibility, not merely impressive job titles. Seek recent cases across approval/implementation, delay/abandonment and retention/renewal where possible. Useful variation includes payroll delivery model, organisational scale, international responsibility and role in the actual investment decision.

Do not impose a statistical quota or promise sector/country comparisons from 6–10 people. If the accessible network is concentrated, describe that honestly and adjust the interpretation. Client introductions create a selection context to report, not a reason to discard the research.

Contributors should know the commissioning organisation, research purpose, how the findings may be published or licensed, expected burden, voluntary nature, recording choice and contact route. Estimate duration after testing the guide. A short structured interview or equivalent written response is acceptable under the agreement; neither should become an unbounded consulting session.

## Core question design

The following are design areas, not final wording:

1. Eligibility and actual role in a payroll investment or operating-model decision.
2. Most recent relevant decision in an agreed recall period, with a route for no such decision.
3. Stage/outcome, keeping approval, implementation, delay, rejection, abandonment, renewal and active consideration distinct.
4. Original problem and consequence; what works well and did not need changing.
5. Desired outcome and functionality/service expectations that were or were not met.
6. Aspirational, planned, funded or delivered improvement.
7. Sponsor, budget owner, approvers and veto roles.
8. Business-case evidence and trade-offs that mattered in the actual decision.
9. Implementation risks, continuity, integration, data and internal capacity.
10. Reasons to remain with or change a supplier without assuming dissatisfaction.
11. Experience versus expectations when sufficient implementation has occurred.
12. Advice for the next decision and one question they believe the profession needs answered.

Map each core question to the report module and decision it supports. Write optional probes for specific routes. Avoid a large forced-ranking task, vague AI language, double questions and unnecessary exact confidential budget requests. Include not-known/not-applicable/prefer-not-to-say choices as appropriate.

## Pilot and guide control

Test interpretation, flow and burden with one or two suitable participants where access permits. An eligible pilot can count toward the target only if permission and comparability are retained. Record wording changes, reasons and any effect on combining responses. If only an internal walkthrough is possible, say so in the method record instead of claiming external validation.

Once approved, preserve the exact guide version. Keep a repeated core for future editions; document later rotating modules separately. Future city cohorts are not the same as following the same people over time.

## Collection safeguards and records

Before receiving identifiable participant information, establish the collection arrangement in the agreement, permitted tools, access locations, notices and required safeguards. The identity/permission register stays in approved restricted storage outside this workspace. The ordinary project folder is not declared secure storage.

Record professional eligibility by a relevant public profile or JGA confirmation. Do not ask participants for identity documents or unrelated personal data. Separate permission for participation, recording, quotation, employer identification, membership recognition and recontact.

The analytical record contains participant ID, guide version, collection date/mode, question IDs, response notes, missing status, attribution limits and verification/quote-check references. Names removed from a quote do not make it anonymous if a small public council can identify the speaker from context.

## Analyse promptly

Write the structured note after each contribution, preserve exact quotes as exact and paraphrase as paraphrase, assign observations/codes and record contrary cases. Revisit the emerging codebook when evidence does not fit. Have a second reviewer check important interpretations and public quotations where feasible, with access only to appropriately cleared material.

Report counts for the specific question base, not percentages that imply a representative market. Do not claim saturation solely because the target number was reached. Distinguish a participant's view of a mechanism from independently demonstrated cause.

## Table commercial guide

Ask about a real decision better intelligence could support; current alternatives and purchased research; budget/approval; essential evidence and rights; objections; timing; and a concrete next step. Present package options only after unaided needs questions. Record interest, use case, budget-holder involvement, next meeting and commitment as different levels of evidence.

For prospective members, investigate relevance, peer mix, willingness to contribute a question, research-only participation, continuing benefits, confidentiality and employer hospitality rules. A premium venue is part of the concept, not proof of research value.

## Access fallback

Monitor recruitment early. If access or consent makes the target unlikely, inform JGA and agree a changed route, sample or timetable. Do not silently replace original contributions with desktop material or describe unavailable commercial conversations as completed validation.
