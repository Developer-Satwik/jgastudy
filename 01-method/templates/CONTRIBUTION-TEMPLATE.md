# Contribution note template

Do not enter identifiable participant details in this workspace. Use this template in the approved collection environment after the fieldwork gate.

- Contribution ID and participant ID.
- Eligible role verified by route; do not place contact identity here.
- Practitioner / commercial buyer / prospective council member roles, with permitted overlap.
- Date, mode, guide version and questions asked.
- Permission-record reference and relevant attribution restrictions.
- Question-by-question response, distinguishing exact quote from paraphrase.
- Missing / not asked / not applicable / don't know / prefer not to say.
- Relevant context retained at a safe level of detail.
- Observation/code IDs and noteworthy contrary cases.
- Agreed factual/quote checks and follow-up limits.
- Analyst, review status and unresolved questions.

For a commercial conversation, also record use case, alternatives, budget owner, objections, timing and evidence of next-step interest. Keep actual commitment separate from general enthusiasm.
