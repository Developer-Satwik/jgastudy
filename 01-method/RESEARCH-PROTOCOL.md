# Research and evidence protocol

Version 0.1. Working design for internal use, pending the first-week scope and collection checkpoint.

## Research design

Combine a documented evidence review, original qualitative practitioner input and a separate commercial feasibility analysis. This is not the previous AI recommendation benchmark. AI tools may assist with search terms, public-source comparison and drafting, but generated answers are not market evidence, participant responses or verification of a source.

The report's main analytical unit is a specific payroll need or investment decision in a stated organisational context. The commercial unit is a defined buyer, research product/licence or event-supported programme. Keep these units separate when linking the two streams.

## Source selection and search

1. Define the module's question, population, terms and decision before searching.
2. Search official professional bodies, original research publishers, credible analyst research and relevant employer/implementation evidence. Use vendor research with funding and method visible, not as independent endorsement.
3. Begin with recent evidence, normally 2024–2026 for current payroll conditions, but retain older material when it contributes a unique mechanism or foundational method. Publication date, fieldwork date and page crawl date are separate.
4. Follow references to original reports. Search engines, summaries and press releases are discovery routes unless their own content is the relevant evidence.
5. Search at least one deliberate counterroute: successful implementation, reasons for satisfaction/renewal, studies with different populations, or a credible argument against the emerging interpretation.
6. Log useful queries, reviewed sources, exclusions and unresolved access. Do not claim systematic-review completeness or a formal PRISMA process without doing one.

Do not bypass paywalls, CAPTCHA, account restrictions or consent forms. A gated report remains access-limited unless obtained legitimately. No account creation, purchase, subscription or form submission is part of routine source discovery. Ask the user when genuinely required; continue independent research meanwhile.

## Appraisal before extraction

For each material source, inspect purpose, population, recruitment, dates, sample size, question wording, denominator, missing responses, geography, commercial funding, limitations and relevance to the current decision. Mark unavailable information as unknown. A professional-looking PDF is not proof of sound sampling.

Avoid composite quality scores that imply more precision than the appraisal supports. Record concrete reasons for fit, concern or exclusion. A source can be useful for understanding a product offer while unsuitable for estimating prevalence.

Treat syndicated copies of one study as one evidence base. Do not use several articles repeating one survey as independent corroboration. When two surveys disagree, investigate different definitions, populations, timing or collection rather than averaging the numbers.

## Source states

`queued` → `discovery_page_inspected` → `original_reviewed` → `extracted` → `publication_checked`.

An access limitation or exclusion is a separate state. Moving a record forward requires actual work. Hashes and local paths exist only when a capture has really been retained. An inspected landing page is never relabelled as an inspected full report.

## Evidence classes

| Class | What it means | Publication treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published finding | A finding from a traceable third-party source | Preserve population, dates, question/base and limitations |
| Practitioner observation | Permissioned contribution from an eligible participant | Describe cohort and context; distinguish quote from paraphrase |
| Analytical interpretation | Our synthesis or proposed explanation | Link to supporting and conflicting evidence; label interpretation |
| Derived calculation | A reproducible transformation of recorded inputs | Preserve formula, input IDs, units and rounding |
| Scenario assumption | An input chosen for financial or future planning | Label assumption and test ranges; do not call it a quote or forecast fact |

Verification means the source actually supports the proposed wording. It does not turn a small sample into a universal fact or demonstrate a causal mechanism.

## Required numerical context

Every substantive percentage or benchmark requires the original measure, base, unit, population, geography, collection period and exact page/table/section. Preserve whether a measure is current use, preferred future model, intention, approved budget or realised outcome. Missing context may prevent using it as a headline even when it is still useful background.

No pooled percentages across incompatible studies. No global league tables from sparse qualitative subgroups. For a small practitioner cohort, use clear counts where appropriate and show who answered that particular question. Distinguish no answer, not asked, not applicable, don't know and prefer not to say.

## Practitioner collection and analysis

- Target 6–10 distinct verified senior payroll professionals through introductions. Purposeful variation is useful; representativeness is not promised.
- Use an agreed recall period for concrete cases, provisionally a recent 24-month decision, with routing for no relevant decision and active consideration.
- Pilot clarity and timing when access permits; log material question changes. Do not claim saturation automatically from the target sample size.
- Use a common core with optional probes. A long research question bank does not mean every participant must answer every probe.
- Code observations with a codebook. Retain evidence that challenges the main theme. Review headline coding and quotations independently where feasible.
- Count unique participants separately from contributions, interviews and overlapping workstreams.

The Table's buyer conversations are commercial discovery, not disguised neutral market research. Explain purpose honestly. A person's general enthusiasm is not a buying commitment.

## Privacy and permissions

Before fieldwork, confirm controller/processor roles, approved tools, access locations, participant notice, lawful collection instructions, attribution choices and any required international-transfer safeguards. The existing service agreement is not itself a completed transfer mechanism.

Store identifiable records in approved restricted storage outside this workspace. Keep pseudonymous analytical files only if their context and access arrangements are appropriate; pseudonymisation is not anonymisation. Do not send raw contact details or confidential responses to an unapproved transcription or AI service.

Separate participation, recording, quotation, employer identification, council recognition and future contact choices. No permission is assumed from an introduction. Consent to join research does not imply permission to receive sponsor sales outreach.

## Rights and captures

Capture only what is needed and permitted. Preserve original filenames or clear source IDs, access date and a hash for retained files. Avoid unnecessary blank-page screenshots or duplicate downloads. For a PDF finding, retain the original and record the relevant page; for a changing product/price page, a dated excerpt or screenshot may be useful.

Attribution alone is not a licence to reproduce charts, resell reports or distribute entire third-party PDFs. Record reuse terms and use original paraphrase/analysis with supporting references. Use only brief necessary excerpts; request permission or substitute a suitable source where needed.

## Module completion and review

A module is ready for synthesis when its core questions have supported answers or explicit gaps; material contrary evidence has been sought; every headline claim links to suitable records; and practical implications follow from the evidence. Continue if a decisive conflict remains unresolved. Stop if additional searches only duplicate existing evidence and all decisions are covered.

Freeze client checkpoint versions. Log scope, method and wording changes; do not overwrite an original response or silently reclassify an adverse finding. The final report can be clear and confident while preserving the limitations needed to interpret its conclusions correctly.

## Method basis

The plan draws on [questionnaire design guidance](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/questionnaire-design-guidance/), [MRS B2B guidance](https://www.mrs.org.uk/standards/mrs-guidelines-for-businesstobusiness-research-insight-and-other-data-processing-activities), the [qualitative quality framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/quality-in-qualitative-evaluation-qqe-html) and [ICO effective anonymisation guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/anonymisation/how-do-we-ensure-anonymisation-is-effective/). These inform the working design; they do not establish certification or professional accreditation.
