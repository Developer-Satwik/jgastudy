# Practitioner discussion guide

Version 0.2 • 28 October 2026 • Internal draft for user review

Proposed audience and questions require review before use. No participant testing or interviews have occurred.

## Before the conversation

Confirm eligibility and collection permissions. Explain JGA commissioning, Riseklix research support and intended publication/commercial licensing. Agree note-taking, any recording and separate attribution choices. Do not collect employee records, exact confidential budgets or unnecessary identities in this workspace.

Use open questions first. Probes are a menu, not a checklist. Prioritise a relevant no-case clarification, intended outcome/baseline and observed result where delivery permits. Accept successful arrangements, no unmet need, justified renewal and deliberate no change. The earlier internal 30–40 minute estimate remains untested and may change; confirm burden after a real timing check. Written contributions use the same core with selective follow-up.

## Q001 Role in decisions

What is your role in decisions about payroll technology, providers or major process changes?

Purpose: Establish actual decision involvement.
Maps to: R04. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- Distinguish recommending, evaluating, implementing and approving.

## Q002 Operating context

How is payroll currently delivered in your area of responsibility?

Purpose: Interpret the experience in its operating context.
Maps to: R02, R03, R07. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- In-house, outsourced or mixed; broad organisational scale; relevant countries and team responsibilities. Do not request employee records.
- For outsourced or mixed delivery, clarify work retained by the employer, information supplied to providers and responsibility for handoffs.

## Q003 Improvement priority

What, if anything, would you most like to improve about payroll in your organisation?

Purpose: Identify the participant’s own priority before thematic prompts.
Maps to: R02. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- What consequence makes this important, and who is affected?
- What currently works well and should be preserved?
- Clarify whether the account is first-hand, based on internal reporting, or an interpretation.

## Q004 Unmet needs

Where, if anywhere, do the payroll technology or services you use or have recently evaluated fall short of your needs?

Purpose: Identify a concrete capability or service gap without presuming dissatisfaction.
Maps to: R02, R03. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- Ask for a specific example, workaround and where the problem begins.
- Do you know of an option that would meet this need? If so, what explains why it is not being used?
- Use the answer to distinguish perceived availability, suitability, affordability/value, readiness or ownership. Preserve not evaluated, not known and no significant shortfall; do not infer market absence.

## Q005 Future priorities

Which payroll improvements, if any, do you expect your organisation to prioritise over the next 12–24 months?

Purpose: Separate ambition from organisational commitment.
Maps to: R03, R07. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- For up to two priorities, establish whether each is an idea, under evaluation, in a plan, approved or being delivered. Clarify funding separately where relevant; preserve not known.
- What would have to change for this priority to move forward or become less important?
- Only after the unaided answer, use AI, automation, integration or outsourcing as optional prompts. Label prompted themes separately. If technology arises, clarify the task, pilot/live stage and oversight rather than assume a common definition.

## Q006 Recent decision

Thinking about the past 24 months, what is the most recent payroll investment, provider renewal or proposed change in which you had direct involvement?

Purpose: Anchor the investigation in one recent real case.
Maps to: R04, R06. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- Approximate timing, proposal type and direct involvement. Investment may include people, capability, training or controls as well as software and providers.
- A substantive approval, renewal, re-scope or go-live decision in an older programme can qualify within the window. Record programme start and selected milestone dates separately; routine activity is not automatically a new decision.
- If no recent case is recalled, and the participant is willing, ask: Has an improvement you mentioned been put forward internally? Choose the relevant follow-up: What happened next? Or: What has prevented it being raised?
- Only never-raised needs are pre-proposal. If a qualifying recent case emerges, confirm it and resume Q007–Q013. Older-only or uncertain cases stay separately labelled context; otherwise continue to Q014. Do not repeat a declined request to share a case.

## Q007 Decision reached

What decision had been reached on that proposal as of today?

Purpose: Establish the actual decision position independently of delivery.
Maps to: R04, R06. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- Approved, declined, deferred, withdrawn/abandoned, pending, no formal approval required, not known or prefer not to disclose.
- Separately ask what has been implemented, if applicable. Preserve chronology where an earlier approval was subsequently changed.

## Q008 Original trigger

What prompted this proposal or review?

Purpose: Connect the case with its initial need, intended outcome and opportunity.
Maps to: R02, R04. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- What improvement was the proposal meant to deliver, and how would you know it had worked?
- After the open answer, clarify any mandatory deadline, lifecycle/contract timing or discretionary opportunity. Do not presume failure or force a single trigger.

## Q009 Authority and influence

Who had the authority to approve or stop the proposal?

Purpose: Identify decision power and conditions.
Maps to: R04. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- Sponsor, budget owner, final sign-off and veto or condition-setting roles.
- Record functions rather than names. Separate known facts from the participant’s understanding.

## Q010 Evidence requested

What evidence, if any, was requested to assess the proposal?

Purpose: Establish the business-case requirements and what value meant in this case.
Maps to: R05. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- After the unaided answer, select relevant probes: baseline/measure, cost scope, who expected to benefit, risk, compliance, continuity, capacity, references or a pilot.
- Clarify implementation, ongoing support, training and transition costs if they mattered. Distinguish reduced spending from capacity at unchanged spending; do not monetise every benefit or double count.
- Do not request confidential documents or exact budgets; not measured or not known are valid answers.

## Q011 Evidence that mattered

Which evidence, if any, appeared to influence the decision most?

Purpose: Distinguish evidence produced from evidence believed to affect a decision.
Maps to: R05, R08. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- What makes you say it mattered?
- Did the proposal or decision change after the evidence was supplied?
- For a pending proposal ask: Which evidence, if any, has appeared to influence the assessment so far? For deferred cases retain the main question. For both pending and deferred cases also ask: What evidence, if any, is still needed before a decision can be made? Record routed wording.

## Q012 Alternatives and choice

What other options were considered?

Purpose: Understand the choice set and selected option.
Maps to: R03, R06. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- Renewal, keeping the present arrangement, process change, another provider/platform, mixed solution or no change.
- Which option, if any, was selected, and why? Retaining an incumbent is an option, not a decision disposition.
- Where relevant, clarify whether staying was preferred on merit, constrained by transition or contract timing, or both. Do not assume lock-in.

## Q013 Conditions and constraints

What conditions or concerns, if any, most affected whether or when the proposal could proceed?

Purpose: Explain conditions enabling progress, observed results and constraints.
Maps to: R05, R06, R08. Route: recent_case.

Optional probes:
- After the unaided answer, select relevant probes: affordability, migration, integration, upstream data ownership, continuity, compliance, security, skills, training, adoption, capacity or competing priorities.
- Ask what enabled progress as well as what constrained it. If useful, ask what supplier action could have reduced the barrier; label that as a proposed remedy rather than an observed effect.
- If delivery permits: What has actually changed, and how is the result being assessed? Clarify the benefit owner, baseline/measure and assessment date where known. Where time or capacity was released, ask where it went.
- Keep expected benefit, go-live, observed change and attribution separate. Not measured, not yet assessed, not applicable, not known and prefer not to disclose are distinct; none automatically means failure.

## Q014 Next research question

What is one question about payroll investment that you think the profession should answer next?

Purpose: Retain priorities beyond the researcher’s assumptions and inform future research.
Maps to: R08, T02. Route: all.

Optional probes:
- Optional: advice to another payroll leader facing a comparable decision.
- Answering does not imply council membership, recontact or attribution permission.

## Analysis controls

- Separate future priorities from selected historical case.
- Code decision disposition, chosen option and implementation status separately.
- Code benefit assessment separately from delivery: measured or observed change is not implied by go-live.
- Never-raised needs, older context and confirmed recent cases use different bases; do not force uncertainty into a case category.
- Gap explanations are participant-attributed, may overlap and do not prove that a market capability is absent.
- Record unanswered, not asked, not applicable, not known and prefer not to disclose separately.
- Keep quotations separate from paraphrase; participant attribution does not prove causation.
- No automatic population percentages or saturation claim from 6–10 contributions.

Decision disposition: approved; declined; deferred; withdrawn/abandoned; pending; no formal approval required; not known; prefer not to disclose.
Chosen option: incumbent retained; alternative provider/platform; internal process change; deliberate no change; mixed; none selected; not known; prefer not to disclose.
Delivery: not started; underway; live/stabilising; operating/completed; paused; stopped; not applicable; not known; prefer not to disclose.
Benefit assessment: measured result; reported change without a measure; not yet assessed; not measured; not applicable; not known; prefer not to disclose. Separately record direction and source of the result, including mixed or adverse outcomes.
Preserve dates, chronology, exact instrument version and routed wording. Eligibility for a case and permission to publish are separate.
