# The Table exploratory conversation guides

Version 0.2 | 30 October 2026 | Proposed for user/client review; collection inactive

This draft carries forward the same B01–B06 and M01–M06 structure. The fourteen report questions and original Table guide remain unchanged in their files. Version 0.2 adapts buyer wording to the actual use and makes concept exposure explicit. It is not an approval to switch the active collection protocol; existing WP03 release decisions and count rules still apply.

## Start with their situation

Explain that JGA is assessing a possible commercial payroll research/peer offer, that feedback may inform whether and how it proceeds, and that this is not an invitation to a booked event. Do not imply a current product, partnership or confirmed membership. Keep purpose disclosure concise, then ask B01/B02 or M01 before listing benefits. Participation and uses must follow the separately approved information/permission arrangements.

After that unaided account, show only the relevant short concept from the proposition pack. Begin with research alone, then ask what an event would add. Record the actual concept version/order if subsequently approved for use. A short qualitative conversation cannot establish market size, price elasticity, representativeness or a reliable conversion rate.

## Buyer or potential funding partner

### B01

What recent decision needed a better understanding of payroll needs or how payroll decisions are made? What was your involvement?

Optional context/probes: For supplier/HCM/consulting/investment roles, establish the product, commercial, client or strategy decision. For an employer-side use, ask about the actual payroll decision. Do not assume either route or a missing solution.

### B02

How did you obtain the information or insight you used, and what was useful or missing?

Optional context/probes: Actual internal work, public sources and past research/service purchases where relevant. No need for confidential plans, exact budgets, personal staff names or client data.

### B03

Looking at this proposed research offer, which parts, if any, would help that decision? What duplicates something you already have?

Optional context/probes: Show only the relevant research-only concept after B01/B02. Record the version shown, use and overlap. Show an event alternative later to distinguish its incremental value.

### B04

What evidence and context would you need to trust and use those outputs?

Optional context/probes: Case relevance, role, geography, period, method, gaps and small qualitative sample limits. Intended output may be unachievable; retain that signal. Do not present an index, benchmark or proven causal mechanism.

### B05

How would an organisation like yours assess paying for a defined version of this?

Optional context/probes: Actual user/payer/beneficiary, approval route, intended internal/public/client use and already granted free access. Ask only relevant probes; no price anchor, full procurement map, presumed licence or promise of contacts.

### B06

What would have to be true before a specific proposal was worth reviewing?

Optional context/probes: Next actual decision, evidence, capacity, rights, timing, objections and a permitted next step, if any. Research funding and separately defined commercial access can be combined only if actually appropriate and agreed.

## Prospective practitioner member

### M01

Which recent payroll decisions or challenges, if any, would make peer research useful to you?

Optional context/probes: Ask before the benefits card. Establish actual context and alternatives without requiring a recent investment case or disclosure.

### M02

Which parts of this proposed programme would be worthwhile to you, and which would add little value?

Optional context/probes: Show member concept after M01. Explore free research, relevant peer discussion, practical follow-through and optional recognition separately. Do not imply a booked event or confirmed council place.

### M03

Would contributing one industry question and appropriate research input be practical? If so, what question and mode would suit you?

Optional context/probes: Optional contribution; no guarantee every question becomes a study question or every topic is answered. Interest is not permission, enrolment or actual report input.

### M04

What conditions would make the peer group useful and comfortable?

Optional context/probes: Relevant roles, actual peer mix, provider/funder involvement, meeting mode, confidentiality, recognition and use of contributions. No names or employer-sensitive details required.

### M05

What would make participation practical or impractical?

Optional context/probes: Time/travel, employer permission, hospitality, accessibility and preferred format only where relevant. Do not collect unnecessary personal details or promise travel/accommodation.

### M06

If no in-person event took place, would contributing to and receiving the research still be worthwhile? What would a live discussion add?

Optional context/probes: Compare remote, written and in-person value without implying exact equivalence, capacity or annual membership. No event attendance is counted from a preference.

## Record only what actually happened

Use the existing approved restricted records and appropriate pseudonymous analysis, not this workspace for identities. Keep exact quote, paraphrase and analyst interpretation separate. Record concept/version shown, actual use and alternatives, evidence/rights needs, specific objections, unknowns and any permitted next step. A suggested permission or price is not an agreed term. Skip, not known, not applicable, declined and not asked remain distinct.

The agreed Table limit remains up to four substantive exploratory contribution events, ideally two commercial buyers/partners and two prospective council members. A combined event counts once; repeated substantive events count separately. An introduction, RSVP, reaction or research-question suggestion alone does not establish an eligible report contribution. Report contributors count each eligible person once only with actual usable permitted input.

Do not show a price anchor while the product/use/rights are undefined. Later price testing, if authorised, needs its own dated/versioned actual offer. Do not promise contacts, a respondent list, meetings, quotations, confidential raw records, independent editorial veto or exclusive findings. If a transparent commercial-access combination is considered, specify it separately with actual permissions and capacity.

No participant data, tools, notice, recording mode, transfer route or sending is activated by this guide.
