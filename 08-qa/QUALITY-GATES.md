# Quality gates

## Scope and baseline

- Current agreement and latest change decisions govern.
- 6–10 distinct practitioner target and up to four Table conversations correctly tracked.
- Executed agreement reconciled when available; signed/paid status recorded as user-reported and supported by supplied email.
- No revival of earlier podcast, article package or four-conversations-total assumptions.

## Research and question gate

- Every question informs a defined reader decision.
- Population, dates, terms and routing clear; neutral wording; no presumption of dissatisfaction or investment.
- Pilot or walkthrough status honest. Approved guide version preserved.
- Collection/permission/tool/transfer arrangements ready before fieldwork.

## Source and claim gate

- Original material inspected where a substantive claim depends on it.
- Publication/fieldwork/access dates distinct; sample, base and measure retained.
- Funding and population fit appraised; duplicate sources not counted as independent.
- Support and counterevidence linked; interpretation distinguished from observation.
- Rights checked; no long copied passages or unlicensed charts in client outputs.

## Practitioner and synthesis gate

- Unique participants not inflated by overlapping calls.
- Quote versus paraphrase verified; permissions applied.
- Missing and not-applicable distinguished; no market-wide statistics from purposive interviews.
- Adverse or contradictory evidence retained. Context combinations checked for identification risk.
- Framework labelled analytical; no invented validated Confidence Index.

## Commercial and model gate

- Free substitutes considered; buyer and member value assessed separately.
- Interest not equated to commitment; public prices not achieved transactions.
- Model A/B use comparable common inputs; incremental event benefit explicit.
- Staff time, taxes/service assumptions, FX, deposits and cash timing accounted for without double counting.
- Formula and stress checks pass. Financial recommendation can be proceed, revise or pause.

## Production and handover gate

- All deliverable IDs map to final files.
- Every major claim/chart traces to evidence or an explicit assumption.
- Dates, definitions and numbers consistent across report, preview and workbook.
- All document pages rendered and visually reviewed; editable charts/formulas/files open correctly.
- Public, confidential JGA and restricted source material separated.
- No participant identities, private calls or unrestricted third-party PDFs leak into public assets.
- Start-here index and manifest prepared; no dependency on Riseklix hosting.
- One consolidated editorial revision and correction of scope defects handled separately.
- Written acceptance captured; silence is not acceptance.
