# T02 independent proposition review

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Passed for internal proposition preparation and later user/client review. This is a review of design coherence and evidence boundaries, not buyer/member validation, a licence grant, collection activation or final publication approval.

The review inspected the working agreement's report, Table, commissioned-IP and JGA responsibility clauses; original Table concept and hypotheses; reviewed T01 conditions; the full integrated proposition, segment, rights, delivery and guide maps; and the underlying buyer/partner/member drafts. It applied 331 integrated integrity and scope checks, following 197 preliminary checks. All 146 protected baseline artifacts remained unchanged.

## The draft is sufficiently concrete for the next design step

- TP01 specifies a dated finished edition, task-relevant reading path and evidence context, while treating briefing and prepared client/public material as separately scoped options. It does not replace the present commissioned report with a smaller pack or a new bespoke study.
- The five contractual buyer categories remain visible. Employer-side use is an additional hypothesis. Actual user, payer and beneficiary are established by intended use rather than an organisation label.
- Free research for participating practitioners remains intact. No teaser downgrade, artificial individual-only restriction or assumed second employer sale of equivalent access is introduced.
- TP02 separates defined research funding, briefing and disclosed recognition from optional commercial access. A transparent separately defined combination remains possible, with no default identities, private answers, meetings, editorial veto or exclusivity.
- TP03 offers a practical research/peer exchange rather than hospitality alone. The follow-through note is an optional benefit to test and cost; no advice desk, fixed publication cadence, permanent council tenure or unlimited support is promised.
- Eight delivery objects expose edition, preparation, servicing, rights, participation, follow-through, event increment and administration work. Roles are requirements only: no staff availability, hours, costs or funding are invented.
- The event-supported route is assessed against research-only and remote/written alternatives. The first report does not depend on events, and the changing-cohort programme is not called a longitudinal panel.
- Future customer-use options do not reserve a new Riseklix licence or veto over JGA's commissioned IP. Underlying participant and third-party constraints remain separate. These are design options, not customer terms already granted.

## Discovery and counting remain consistent

The proposed Table guide keeps six buyer and six member questions. Neutral B01 accommodates supplier, adviser, investment and employer uses without silently making employers the primary market. Actual recent needs and alternatives precede concept exposure; research alone precedes the event comparison. Actual wording/version/order would be recorded if later approved for collection.

The original guide and WP03 policy remain unchanged; the proposed version is inactive. The fourteen report questions are untouched. The scope still allows up to four substantive Table contribution events, ideally two buyers/partners and two prospective members, separately from six-to-ten verified eligible report contributors. Interest, a suggested industry question, an RSVP, membership, attendance and actual usable permitted research input remain distinct. Combined/repeated-event and person-count rules are preserved.

No price anchor, buyer, member, permission, exposure, order, paid commitment, financial input or fieldwork event is recorded. A request for a proposal is not a sale, and cash received would not by itself prove useful delivery. T03 validation and T04/T05 economics remain open.

## Wording refinements resolved

1. Neutral buyer discovery wording covers the actual use before showing benefits, with the earlier guide preserved.
2. Recognition does not by itself authorise attendance disclosure or attribution; contextual identification remains possible.
3. The exact member concept script now calls the follow-through note a possible extra, matching its optional payload status.
4. Partner funding now names the actual edition and purpose. Existing commissioned/completed research is not presented as a newly commissioned study; additional release/briefing/distribution work is distinguished. Costs and bundled entitlements are counted once while legitimate partner/licence revenues, cost recovery and profit remain possible.

No substantive issue remains open for this internal preparation stage. Final rights/currentness, actual evidence, capacity, commercial terms and collection arrangements still require their own later work; the review does not turn those into a long new mandatory form or add arbitrary thresholds.
