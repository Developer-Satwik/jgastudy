# T02 preliminary independent review

30 October 2026. Review of proposition design scope, not fieldwork, legal certification, rights grant, price recommendation or launch approval.

## Basis inspected

The working generated agreement, particularly sections 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9; scope baseline; Table design brief; first T02 hypotheses; T01 second-check readout; Table validation guide; WP03 counting/routing policy and participation/use choices; financial model specification. The executed agreement copy has not been independently retrieved. This review uses the existing project baseline without reopening the agreed fee or replacing it.

## Conditions for a useful internal proposition

1. Keep likely commercial segments visible: payroll technology, outsourcing, HCM, consulting and investment organisations are expressly in the commissioned Table assessment. An employer decision-user is a plausible additional use case, not an assumed sole buyer.
2. Say who will use the output, for which actual decision and what is purchased. The sponsor/funder, end user, budget owner, practitioner and distribution route need not be the same person or firm.
3. Define a minimum useful asset rather than a generic trend report: concrete question, relevant original evidence, alternatives, usable implications and a bounded interpretation/briefing where feasible. Do not suggest the small purposive sample can rank suppliers, estimate demand rates or certify best practice.
4. Preserve the free research promised to practitioner contributors. Do not silently turn it into a teaser, invent new individual-only restrictions, or forecast a second employer licence for equivalent access.
5. Specify paid incremental service or permission as an option to validate, not a right already granted or a rule already imposed. Internal use, external quotation, adviser client deliverables and resale can differ; actual terms and underlying contributor/third-party permissions govern.
6. Respect the agreement's JGA commissioned-IP arrangement. This is JGA's future product design, not a Riseklix platform licence or veto over JGA-owned commissioned work. Participant/source restrictions remain separately real.
7. A founding research partner can receive defined output, recognition and agreed reuse benefits. No automatic private answers, participant identities, meetings, endorsement, editorial veto, exclusivity or favourable findings.
8. A transparent separately defined research/access combination is possible in principle. Do not make the design falsely binary, but do not turn possibility into included commercial access.
9. Define member value beyond hospitality without adding an unstaffed advice desk, continuous support, fixed annual publication cadence, recurring service-level promise or guaranteed peer connections.
10. Keep one industry question, relevant experience, research access, optional council recognition and possible peer gathering distinct. An exploratory conversation is not membership or an invitation to a booked event.
11. Compare the same intended outcome with research-only, remote/written and event-supported routes. The venue/city is a delivery choice; it does not establish the buyer's country set or evidence need.
12. Count programme, edition, cohort, person, seat, entitlement period, partner and customer distinctly. Twelve-to-fifteen practitioner places per proposed event is capacity, not a sampled or representative panel. Include hosts later in the seat/cost plan.
13. Show delivery requirements and bounded workload. Existing evidence and personnel do not establish future availability, useful findings, permissioned case count, editor/moderator capacity, budget or cash timing. Future bespoke or recurring production remains separately agreed.
14. Discover actual recent decisions, alternatives and purchases before introducing concept wording. The current Table buyer B01 is supplier-facing; employer/adviser adaptations should preserve intent and be explicitly proposed, with the fourteen report questions untouched.
15. Treat evidence of interest proportionately. A useful comment, request for a proposal, employer approval, signed order, received money and delivered benefit are distinct. Up to four Table contribution events cannot establish market size or price elasticity.

## Practical issues to test in integration

- Is the primary product concrete enough to explain in a short paragraph, yet small-cohort evidence and permissions remain honest?
- Are partner and member minimum benefits deliverable without committing services outside this research-and-preparation contract?
- Can a reader tell which choices require further definition without facing a long new form or an invented approval checklist?
- Do the exact concept scripts belong after unaided discovery and carry a consistent draft version?
- Are further user/client decisions necessary for actual collection retained, with nothing sent or activated?

The internal T02 design can be complete for the present step while T03 buyer/member validation and T04/T05 economics remain open. No new external research is needed merely to write these propositions from the reviewed evidence. Fresh facts, prices or legal claims should not be added without their own review.
