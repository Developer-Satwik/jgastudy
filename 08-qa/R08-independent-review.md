## Correction resolved

The draft prose correctly distinguished evidence requested, supplied, accepted and believed influential, but its first structured V04 field combined acceptance and perceived influence. That would have blurred the distinction the synthesis aims to preserve. Root split these into separate optional `evidence_acceptance_if_known` and `perceived_influence_and_basis` fields and separate evidence labels. The regenerated routing record and final review verify the correction. No compulsory question or data collection was added.
