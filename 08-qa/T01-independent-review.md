Reviewed 29 October 2026. Passed for internal six-model desktop comparison and preparation of four untested T02 proposition hypotheses. This is not publication approval, fieldwork or commercial validation.

The integrated check passed 1,110 checks covering every model-to-candidate-to-canonical mapping, source and claim references, the 60-file capture inventory, all 120 preservation files, all 355 earlier claim records, the 191 earlier source records' base fields and review history, and all 81 existing findings. Direct source review confirmed the important distinctions set out in the [preliminary source review](t01-review/PRELIMINARY-SOURCE-REVIEW.md): dated HR.com offer versus separate sponsor output; ISG research reading versus promotional licensing; annual organisation support versus report licensing; Conference Board Center access versus individual Council admission; CPO free editorial content versus unknown private forum terms; and free approved iVentiv delegates versus its explicit commercial access package. The HR.com rendered original method page was also visually inspected.

Two synthesis wording refinements were requested and applied:

- Publicly offered product architecture is not described as commercially demonstrated or viable.
- Research funding and commercial access may form a separately defined transparent combination. The comparison does not impose an unsupported binary choice, and private identities, answers and meetings are not default rights.

A provisional source concern about CIPP's expiry year was disproved on full inspection: tier footnotes explicitly state 14 June 2026. The retained current-tariff uncertainty is appropriate; no unsupported year-removal was required. Fosway's unavailable original remains screened/discovery-only rather than core evidence.

The six selected models have genuinely different exchanges and sensible direct/adjacent comparability labels. The comparison does not treat them as six identical competitors or average unlike price units. Its four T02 hypotheses include actual-use questions, counter-signals and explicit unknowns; no buyer commitment, market-size estimate, representative demand rate, price recommendation, sponsor entitlement, event success or launch conclusion has been invented. Nothing was sent or purchased.

No substantive issue remains open for this internal preparation stage. Actual buyer/member contributions, rights/operating definitions, fully costed research-only versus event-supported modelling and final client/publication review remain later work.
