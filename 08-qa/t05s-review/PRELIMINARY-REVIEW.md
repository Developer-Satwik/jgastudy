# T05S preliminary independent review

1 November 2026. Review of T02 proposition/delivery boundaries, the original T05 research cost map, T05R delivery refinements and the T05S preservation baseline. This is a design critique before the component is available; it is not a completed workbook review.

The proposed per-unit catalogue is appropriate if quantities remain blank and all three cases describe task effort only. A numbered unit is a costing comparison, not a market forecast, purchasing unit, confirmed capacity, selling price or actual cash cost.

Checks required in the final component:

- Separate contacts, discussions, proposals, negotiation rounds and orders. Lost/abandoned attempts retain their actual work; do not derive earlier stages from orders or impose a success rate.
- Define edition, organisation, asset, session, support request, invoice and optional renewal-review units separately. Never sum unlike unit costs into a programme total.
- Keep research edition creation, reusable reading routes, rights checks, existing editable assets and original first-edition pricing/use design with their original WR tasks. Add only actual incremental service work; credit a named shared asset once.
- Count a bundled licence, partner briefing, approved recognition asset, shared session and free practitioner research once. Free access is not a paid research sale; it can still require distribution labour. Do not treat one free access grant as one new edition.
- Leave unknown commercial/sales rates and all programme quantities blank. A zero means a task was explicitly excluded or genuinely requires zero work, not that its rate or applicability is unknown. A full labour value stays unavailable while any applicable rate is missing.
- Use the published research and coordination inputs solely as transparent, limited proxies. Research day normalisation remains 372/7.5 = GBP49.60/hour. Coordination remains GBP46.19/hour. Neither establishes qualified sales/partner negotiation capability, actual JGA rates, supplier booking increments, tax, expenses or additional payroll cash.
- Keep a single shared delivery event separate from recipient entitlements, a negotiation round separate from a signed agreement, and a billing action separate from money received or fulfilled work.
- Identify within-scope correction/rework as existing delivery obligations and avoid presenting it as an extra-priced service. Optional later change requests need their own agreed scope.
- Optional renewal review is work only if deliberately chosen. It cannot imply automatic renewal, recurring revenue, future refresh or a changed USD1,500 commission.
- Preserve the first 33 financial records exactly, append separate new hours/unknown-rate/blank-quantity records, and keep appended source claims bounded to the support actually available.

Preliminary preservation results: all 333 baseline artefacts and all 13 pre-change snapshots match their recorded SHA-256 hashes. These checks must be repeated after integration.
