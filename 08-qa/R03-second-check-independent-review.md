## Findings

1. Historical expectations and observed results remain distinct. Earlier CIPP/JGA material supplies dated lineage, not a fulfilled forecast or a numerical adoption trend. The existing implementation cases retain scoped positive assurance and management-reported progress alongside difficulties; the revised Scottish Government chronology is preserved.
2. Continuing readiness is a defensible conditional implication. HMRC and SAP examples do not become a universal release schedule, proof that updates cause failure or a mandate to replace supported systems. Ordinary cycles, transition, updates and exceptional changes remain distinct.
3. Work redistribution is not assumed to be either a saving or a burden. The map asks who performs the task, who benefits and what result occurs. Employee self-service, reduced central handling and total organisational effort are separate measures.
4. Service units remain sufficiently specific. Global processing, payment, HR records, employment-provider relationships and partner coordination are not treated as equivalent products or legal recommendations. Brand/country counts do not become a fit score or evidence of entitlement.
5. Individual capacity, enduring leadership, employer-directed work, consulting-led outputs and ongoing execution are distinguished. Hays/Robert Half declarations are used for engagement scope rather than verified payroll capability, outcomes or contract obligations. P08 allows development and different durations; a job title does not automatically supply authority.
6. Useful delegated capability is preserved as a positive alternative. GDS/NCSC guidance supports assessing accepted dependence and retained responsibilities, not pursuing zero dependence or preferring in-house operation by default. Public-sector requirements are not silently imposed on private employers.
7. Export, backup, usable receiving arrangements and recovered operation remain separate. No inspected provider is said to lack a control, no recovery test was performed and no universal deadline or switching-cost result is inferred.
8. The earlier prompts are explicitly identified as researcher-written constructions. They serve analyst challenge and source discovery; unaided participant priorities remain first. No model count, fictional director requirement or constructed geography becomes real buyer evidence.
9. Scope is preserved. There are eight options and five cross-cutting conditions, no ninth response, scored maturity model, extra mandatory questionnaire, new employment/security workstream or completed investment model. The canonical question map still exactly matches the unapproved version0.2 snapshot. R04/R05 authority/economics work remains subsequent.
10. Structured and narrative coverage is consistent. P01–P08 and LC01–LC05 are present in JSON; the twelve mapped sources and eighteen mapped claim IDs resolve to canonical records. Existing source, beta, UI, access and numerical limits have not been cleared by the new material.
