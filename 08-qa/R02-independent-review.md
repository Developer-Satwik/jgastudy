# Independent review of the R02 desktop synthesis

28 October 2026. Reviewer: r02_capacity_evidence. Read the integrated desktop readout, nine-row needs/consequences matrix, canonical selected claims and underlying capacity, service-boundary and consequence memos.

## Judgment

Passed for internal desktop synthesis. No substantive correction is required. This does not approve a final publication, establish practitioner findings or validate commercial demand.

## Checks

- Capacity and task evidence is used to identify work and dependencies, not to prescribe staffing or infer productivity from employee-to-staff ratios.
- Targets, achieved service, corrected employee outcomes and benefit evidence remain distinct. The Deloitte numerical holds are preserved rather than repaired or used in the conclusions.
- Official guidance, supplier specifications, survey answers and employer records are treated as different evidence classes. A declared capability is not delivered performance; an observed need is not proof of market absence.
- Public employer examples are bounded and their public-sector selection limits are explicit. No industry-wide ranking or private-market prevalence is inferred.
- Remedial payment principal, overpayments, recoveries, write-offs and administration costs remain separate. No transferable payroll-only cost, causal ROI or retention effect is claimed.
- The matrix proposes indicators for actual cases and labels them accordingly. It does not claim those indicators have already been measured in the JGA cohort.
- The R02 conclusion concerns needs, consequences and ownership. It leaves technology and operating-model choices to R03 and preserves original practitioner integration and final-use checks.

No other project files, client documents or canonical records were changed during this review. No external action occurred.
