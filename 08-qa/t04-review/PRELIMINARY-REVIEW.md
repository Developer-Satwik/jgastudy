# T04 preliminary independent review

30 October 2026. Review criteria for commercial routes and model design, not an audit of an Excel workbook, a budget, sales validation or launch approval.

The working agreement, Table brief, financial specification and reviewed T02 offers support preparing Model A research-only, Model B UK pilot and a separate annual four-location event programme. They do not supply current prices, contracted customers, staffed capacity, venue availability or a chosen Texas city.

## Decisions the integrated design must make clear

1. Compare routes on an explicit common horizon and research-asset boundary. One edition sold through multiple licences is one produced asset, not a fresh research cost for every buyer or every city.
2. Keep research-use, partner support, optional briefing/customer-use preparation and event increment distinct. A partner bundle must not also appear as an independently sold identical licence.
3. Preserve full free research for practitioner contributors. Do not assume that an employer buys equivalent access again, or narrow the free benefit to manufacture revenue.
4. Keep scenario quantities and prices as testable inputs. Public offers and expressions of interest are not achieved sales, renewals, conversion rates or committed recurring cash.
5. Define cash operating contribution, full resource result and first-investment recovery separately. They answer different questions; none should be relabelled net profit without a clear boundary.
6. The current USD1,500 project includes USD1,000 report and USD500 Table feasibility. Neither is a recurring edition cost quote. A recovery view can include defined initial investment, but must not duplicate it in common research, annual city costs and optional servicing.
7. The user-reported USD750 upfront payment and remaining USD750 balance have different statuses and triggers. Do not call the full project fee sunk/paid, or treat the commission as partner revenue. Remaining project cash obligations and past investment should be distinguishable from a future operating period.
8. Staff/JGA opportunity cost is not necessarily additional cash payroll. Cost the actual role/time/resource and avoid counting the same person or labour twice under multiple labels.
9. Twelve-to-fifteen practitioners are not total venue seats. Count distinct seated hosts, moderators and other required persons; one person can occupy multiple roles. Any two-host or seventeen-seat test is an explicit constructed scenario, not an agreed staffing plan.
10. A room's seated capacity, standing capacity, suggested menu price and private-room minimum spend are different units. A menu amount cannot substitute for a quoted private-event total.
11. Preserve original currency, unit, date, VAT/service-charge wording and known inclusions. Do not infer tax recoverability, double-apply VAT, add service charge on an unverified base or treat unknown charges as zero.
12. A credited deposit affects cash timing, not necessarily incremental cost. Cancellation exposure, balance dates and minimum-spend treatment require source-specific terms or a labelled unresolved input, not generic hotel assumptions.
13. A public venue screen is not a selected venue, date-availability check, private-room quote or assurance of privacy, acoustics, access or participant acceptability. Unknowns remain unknown.
14. The annual programme must reconcile cities, editions, shared administration and local costs. Dublin and Texas need their own currency/geography inputs; an undecided Texas city cannot support a specific travel budget.
15. Show the difference between lower/higher hypothetical inputs and a forecast. A zero-sales downside is a useful stress test, but missing prices/costs must not become healthy zeros or invented positive margins.
16. T03 buyer/member validation, approved collection, final rights and publication/currentness remain open. T04 can complete a reviewed design while T05 workbook, formula recalculation and sensitivity checks remain unperformed.

## Evidence and review boundary

Current contracts and source records retain their existing statuses. It will inspect the specific route and public-cost artifacts when available, with source facts separated from analyst applications.
